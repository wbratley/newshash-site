Yorkshire Wildlife Park live: Man dies after 'jumping into enclosure' to 'play with tiger'

Yorkshire Wildlife Park live: Man dies after 'jumping into enclosure' to 'play with tiger' Yorkshire Wildlife Park confirmed there had been a "serious incident" at the tiger enclosure, adding that an emergency response was underway and the site would be closed today and tomorrow A man has died after being attacked by a tiger at a wildlife park. Police and paramedics raced to Yorkshire Wildlife Park at about 1.35pm, following reports a man had "jumped into a tiger enclosure". South Yorkshire Police said the man had suffered "serious injuries", while the tiger was uninjured and remained inside the enclosure following the attack. Police later confirmed the man had died. Witnesses told how a black screen was erected at the enclosure, with reports of "shots fired" shortly before the park was evacuated and an air ambulance arrived. Footage emerged online showing a man lying on the grass in the tiger's enclosure, before being attacked. Key Events Recap - What we know after man dies in tiger enclosure Good morning and welcome to the blog if you are just joining us. Here's what you've missed: - A man has died after entering an enclosure housing two tigers at Yorkshire Wildlife Park, near Doncaster. - South Yorkshire Police were called to the park shortly after 1.30pm on Saturday, October 10, following reports that a man had entered the enclosure. - The man suffered serious injuries and was pronounced dead at the scene. His family have been informed, but police have not publicly identified him. What happened? - Police believe the man climbed into the enclosure housing the tigers. The circumstances surrounding why he entered the enclosure have not been confirmed. - Eyewitnesses said the man had been dancing happily with his AirPods in around 20 minutes before the incident. Other witnesses reportedly believed he wanted to play with the tigers, although this has not been confirmed by police. - Footage filmed shortly before the incident also appears to capture the man asking a couple to take a photograph of him with the tigers. What happened to the tigers? South Yorkshire Police said the tiger involved was uninjured and remained in its enclosure following the incident. Officers also confirmed there was no wider risk to the public. Is Yorkshire Wildlife Park open? The park was evacuated following the incident and confirmed it would remain closed for the rest of the weekend, including Sunday. The attraction said its emergency response procedures had been activated and that it was working with the authorities to establish what happened. What we know about the man who died The man suffered serious injuries and was pronounced dead at the scene. His family have been informed. Police have not publicly identified the man, and details about his age, identity and why he entered the enclosure have not been confirmed yet. There will likely be more details released today. Authorities have also urged members of the public not to share footage of the incident online. No further details about the circumstances surrounding the incident have been confirmed. What is the Land of the Tigers attraction? Land of the Tigers is a purpose-built, two-acre reserve at Yorkshire Wildlife Park, near Doncaster, which opened in 2011. The attraction is home to endangered Amur tigers, one of the world's largest tiger subspecies, native to the Russian Far East and parts of north-east Asia. The reserve was designed to provide the animals with a naturalistic environment, with visitors able to observe them from a raised walkway. Two Amur tigers were identified as living at the park in reports following Saturday's fatal incident: Tschuna, a tigress who arrived in 2013, and Altai, a younger male who arrived last year. South Yorkshire Police said the tiger involved in the incident was uninjured and remained in its enclosure. The force also confirmed there was no wider risk to the public. Yorkshire Wildlife Park, which first opened in 2009, said it had activated its emergency response procedures following the incident and was working with the authorities to establish what happened. Enclosure is home to two endangered Amur tigers The enclosure at Yorkshire Wildlife Park is home to two Amur tigers - an endangered subspecies with around only 500 left in the wild. The animals, native to far-east Russia, northeast China, and parts of the Korean Peninsula, are the largest big cat in the world. One of the tigers, named Altai, arrived at the park from Norway in 2025. The male tiger joined Tschuna, a 17-stone female who came to the park in 2013 as part of an international breeding programme aimed at safeguarding the species. Tschuna had three cubs in 2015. They were all subsequently relocated to other wildlife parks around the world to support the programme. Male Amur tigers can weigh up to 300kg or more, while females typically weigh between 110 to 160kg. Yorkshire Wildlife Park's 'Land of the Tigers' habitat The tiger enclosure at Yorkshire Wildlife Park opened in 2011. Dubbed the 'Land of the Tigers', the area allows the big cats to roam woodlands and grasslands or play in a waterfall pool, according to a description on its website. Visitors can see the animals by walking along a 300 meter-long raised walkway. The park has two Amur tigers, an endangered subspecies with around 500 left in the wild. It's also home to a number of other wild animals, including lions, leopards, cheetahs and polar bears.
