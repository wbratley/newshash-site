Middlesbrough fire live: Girl, 7, and aunt die in house fire as police probe gangland revenge theory Two men have been arrested on suspicion of murder after a house fire in Grangetown, Middlesbrough, left two people - including a child - dead and four others injured A seven-year-old girl and her aunt have been named locally as the two victims of a fatal house fire in Middlesbrough, as police investigate whether the blaze could be linked to a 'gangland dispute' that led to a crash on the A66 on Saturday. Valentina Foster, seven, and her aunt, Natalie McDonald, 34, died in the devastating fire at a house on Birchington Avenue in Grangetown this morning, according to reports. Two men, aged 23 and 31, have been arrested on suspicion of murder and remain in custody. Cleveland Police said officers were called to the property shortly before 5.30am. The force said officers "cannot rule out any links to wider organised crime at this time" as they investigate the circumstances surrounding the blaze. One woman remains in a critical condition at the Royal Victoria Infirmary (RVI), while three others are in hospital with non-life-threatening injuries. The house fire occurred less than a mile from the scene of the A66 crash that killed seven people - five young males and two police officers - in the early hours of Saturday. The tragedy happened when a Volkswagen Passat, which was travelling the wrong way along the dual carriageway while evading police, collided with a police car.
