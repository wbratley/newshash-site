Serbian leader Vučić concedes defeat to student-led movement in snap election

Unbiased summary

Serbian President Aleksandar Vučić conceded defeat on Sunday after snap parliamentary elections, acknowledging that the student-led movement Student List won the vote. Based on preliminary results and pollster projections, the Student List is expected to secure between 135 and 140 seats in the 250-seat parliament, while Vučić's Serbian Progressive Party (SNS) will hold around 104-105 seats. The election followed nearly two years of protests triggered by the collapse of a train station canopy in Novi Sad in 2024 that killed 16 people. Turnout was nearly 70%, the highest since 1992. Celebrations erupted in Belgrade and other cities. Vučić said his party won about 1.7 million votes to the Student List's 2.2 million, and he alleged interference by independent media and unnamed European countries.
