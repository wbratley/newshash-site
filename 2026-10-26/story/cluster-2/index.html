Two killed by falling trees as Storm Boelo leaves 90,000 without power across UK

Unbiased summary

Storm Boelo brought severe winds to the UK over the weekend, with gusts reaching 84mph in some areas. Two men were killed by falling trees: a 60-year-old in Dumfries and Galloway, Scotland, and a man in his 20s in north London. The storm, which followed Storm Austen, caused widespread power cuts affecting over 838,000 properties at peak, with about 90,000 still without power by Sunday evening. An amber wind warning was in place for Scotland, Northern Ireland, and northern England until 9pm Sunday. The Met Office warned of flying debris and dangerous waves. Travel disruption included flight delays, rail cancellations, and road closures. A yellow wind warning is issued for Tuesday as further unsettled weather is expected.
