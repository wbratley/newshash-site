First look at never-before-seen Tyson Fury vs Anthony Joshua belt on the line as Brits go face-to-face for first time

Article text

BOXING fans got a first look at the never-seen-before new belt up for grabs when Tyson Fury and Anthony Joshua finally face off. The heavyweight legends will step into the ring in Cardiff in the early hours of December 12. And now the blockbuster showdown has a special title on the line. Turki Al-Sheikh joined the pair on stage at the end of their press conference on Wednesday as they went head to head for the first time. And he hoisted the new-look ‘Battle of Britain’ belt aloft – designed and commissioned specifically for this epic fight. It features various emblems including a golden centrepiece which reads “Battle of Britain, Awarded by HE Turki Al-Sheikh” followed by the Ring logo.
