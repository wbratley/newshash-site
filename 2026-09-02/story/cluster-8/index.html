Keir Starmer resigns as MP, triggering by-election in Holborn and St Pancras

Unbiased summary

Sir Keir Starmer, the former Labour leader and prime minister, has resigned as Member of Parliament for Holborn and St Pancras, citing a desire to focus on international affairs, defence, security, trade, and technology. He announced his decision in a statement to the Camden New Journal on Tuesday, shortly after Parliament resumed following the summer break. Starmer had previously indicated he would remain on the backbenches until the next general election. His resignation triggers a by-election in his London seat. Labour is now led by Andy Burnham, who replaced Starmer in July after poor local election results and a loss of party support. Green Party leader Zack Polanski has been suggested as a potential candidate for the by-election, and novelty candidate Count Binface has hinted at running. Starmer's statement also noted he would continue work on tackling violence against women and girls.
