Patrick Clancy and wife Rachel confirm they are 'starting a family' in first interview since Lindsay's mistrial

- Listen to our Lindsay Clancy podcast series - Join The Crime Desk: Keep up to date with the most chilling cases and investigations by signing up for free HERE Patrick Clancy and his new wife have revealed they are starting a family together around three-and-a-half years on from the day that his ex-wife Lindsay Clancy strangled his three children with exercise bands inside their suburban Massachusetts home. Patrick and Dr Rachel Danis sat down with 60 Minutes to share news of their future plans together in their first interview since Clancy’s case ended in a controversial mistrial earlier this month. While the newlyweds didn't confirm if they are currently expecting, when correspondent Ross Douthat asked 'and you guys are starting a family?', Rachel confirmed 'yeah' with a smile and nod. The couple, who married this April in New York City, said that they plan to tell their child about Patrick's late children Cora, Dawson and Callan. 'I think every parent can agree, you have different relationships with your kids, but they're all your kids. And I can't wait to tell them about their brothers and sister,' Patrick said. The couple spoke out just weeks after Patrick had an emotional courtroom reunion with his ex-wife during her high-profile murder trial. Clancy was charged with three counts of first-degree murder for killing Cora, five, Dawson, three, and Callan, eight months old, inside the family home in the wealthy town of Duxbury, Massachusetts, on January 24, 2023. The 36-year-old labor and delivery nurse strangled the children with exercise bands in the basement after sending Patrick out of the home to pick up medicine from CVS and a takeout order from a local restaurant. Clancy survived but was left paralyzed from the waist down. The mom-of-three did not deny killing her children but pleaded not guilty by reason of insanity, saying she was suffering from severe postpartum depression and psychosis at the time. Her six-week trial in Plymouth, Massachusetts, sparked a national conversation about postpartum mental illness and criminal responsibility - quickly capturing attention all across the globe. Ultimately, after seven days of deliberations, a mistrial was declared after the 12 jurors failed to reach a unanimous verdict. In an unprecedented move, it emerged during deliberations that a lone male holdout - since identified as Michael P Desronvil - was refusing to join the other 11 in finding her not guilty by reason of insanity. Several jurors have since spoken out to accuse him of showing reasonable doubt but refusing to apply it to the law. Desronvil has hired an attorney and released a statement pushing back on their claims, saying he believed Clancy ‘knew exactly what she was doing.’ Patrick became one of the key witnesses in the trial, testifying to his then-wife's spiraling mental health in the months leading up to the killings and the devastating day when he found his three children dead. A few months after the killings, Patrick moved to New York City, sold the family home and filed for divorce. The Daily Mail exclusively revealed in July that the 38-year-old tech executive had since found love and remarried Rachel, a fertility doctor. She noticed an 'adorable photo' of children as the wallpaper and asked: 'Who are those cuties?,' Rachel recalled. When Patrick told her they were his children, she said she first asked if they lived in Massachusetts. 'And Pat said, "They died." And this is just at the cashier,' Rachel told Douthat. 'I guess I had so many questions, and yet at the same time I didn't know exactly what to ask.'
