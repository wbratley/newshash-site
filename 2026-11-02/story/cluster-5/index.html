Henry Nowak's family describe trauma of learning of his death and watching police handcuff him as he died

Unbiased summary

Henry Nowak, an 18-year-old student at the University of Southampton, was stabbed five times on 3 December 2025 while walking home from a night out. His attacker, Vickrum Digwa, 23, falsely told police that Nowak had racially abused him. Police body-worn camera footage showed officers handcuffing Nowak as he lay dying and repeatedly said he had been stabbed. Digwa was convicted of murder and sentenced to life imprisonment with a minimum term of 21 years. Nowak's family (father Mark, stepmother Katie, mother Lucy, and sister Olivia) spoke to ITV for a documentary, describing their 'world changed twice': first when police informed them of his death, and again when they watched the footage. The family described 'unbelievable pain.' Home Secretary Shabana Mahmood announced an independent review into the murder, led by former lord chancellor Alex Chalk, to examine missed opportunities to intervene.
