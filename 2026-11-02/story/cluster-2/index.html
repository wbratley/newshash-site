Private jet crashes in Florida neighborhood, killing all nine on board

Unbiased summary

A Learjet 60 business jet crashed into a residential area near West Palm Beach, Florida, on Sunday at approximately 12:50 PM local time, killing all nine people on board. The aircraft had departed from Austin, Texas, and was en route to the Bahamas. According to air traffic control recordings, the pilot reported turbulence, icing, and precipitation before requesting a climb. The plane then entered a steep dive, reaching descent rates of up to 30,000 feet per minute and exceeding design limits. The pilot declared an emergency. The crash caused a large fireball and crater, but no injuries on the ground have been reported. The FAA and NTSB are investigating. The transportation secretary called it a 'horrific tragedy.'
