What happens when you leave Celebrity Traitors? Joanna McNally reveals the 'military' procedure BBC bosses take to sneak the star out of the Scottish castle

Article text

Joanne McNally has revealed exactly what happens when you leave Celebrity Traitors as she detailed the 'military' procedure BBC bosses take. The comedian was banished from the hit show at the roundtable on Thursday night, just missing out on a place in the final. But after she revealed she was a Faithful and exited the room, Joanne has shared the step-by-step producers take to sneak the stars out of the Scottish castle. Speaking on her My Therapist Ghosted Me podcast, co-host Vogue Williams asked: 'When you leave what happens, you just left and go home?' Joanne explained: 'So you get off the podium, they rush you out the side door and your bailed into a van like Whitney Houston in the Bodyguard. 'You have a little backpack there, it's like an emergency backpack in the castle at all times so that if it's the night that you do go you have your essentials. 'And then you're taken to a different hotel.' She continued: 'Then the next day, you actually have a full day's work to do in the castle because you have to do all your B-shots and there was a video of me in the bath, so it's all that stuff and your final interviews. 'All that stuff has to be done, I always wondered why in the castle if you needed to go to the bathroom or anything it's this big kind of production. 'It's because they have to make sure the castle is clear and there's not people who've been banished the night before because they were in the castle doing all their extra last bits of work. 'So they were making sure no one crossed paths. It's such a huge production and they run it so military'.
