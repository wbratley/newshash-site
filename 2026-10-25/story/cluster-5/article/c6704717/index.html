Weekly horoscope for October 25 – 31: Maggie Hyde reveals what the stars have in store for every zodiac sign

Article text

THE Sun’s world famous Mystic Meg horoscope column has entertained readers for decades with predictions for their love lives, money and careers. Read on to see what Mystic Meg with Maggie Hyde has written in the stars for you today. ARIES MAR 21 – APR 20 Some form of soul-searching seems inevitable, especially if a cherished plan suddenly bombs out or proves to be more trouble than it’s worth. Don’t take your famous independence too far. Realise that there’s a lot of help on hand, especially from a friend who has a will of iron. The most likely culprits for slowing you down will be an extra busy time at work or a health issue. Most read in Fabulous Keep away from those who’ve got the lurgy and, if you’re unlucky enough to get a bug, stay home and rest. No heroics. Get back to winter recipes that are both nutritious and firm favourites with the family. GEMINI MAY 22 – JUNE 21 There may be all sorts of heavy issues going on around you but don’t get pulled in. Help where you can, but you really don’t have to solve everyone else’s problems. Your own closest relationships will benefit from your light touch and ability to keep everyone talking. Even so there are some secrets going on or a strange situation could yet get even stranger. CANCER JUNE 22 – JULY 22 The week gets off to a bright start with the full moon lighting up your friends and social zones. Get some mates nights out in the diary, or suggest events about things that interest you or just because they sound like fun. You won’t be short of company. If you’re a parent it’s worth the childcare costs, especially if you’ve been spending too much time at home. LEO JULY 23 – AUG 23 Think about this year’s experiences that have changed the inner you or your life, especially in the last five months. Someone or something that you didn’t know before summer should be noticeable, possibly even a bit overwhelming, but you can cope! You can also kick start your system with exercise and a great diet. What your energy soar – and your sex appeal. VIRGO AUG 24 – SEPT 22 Feeding the family is a job that never stops but one that you can make easier. With Mercury in your ideas zone magazines and websites are great hunting grounds for clever menus, especially if you’ve got fussy eaters to please. Finding quick answers for people issues is not so easy, so keep an open mind and make sure every plan has a get out clause. LIBRA SEPT 23 – OCT 23 When it comes to family there’s a lot to be said for biting your lip. Voicing every irritation can sometimes make bad feelings worse, and your talent for tolerance will tell you what is best left. However, something that’s linked to decisions or events that happened about three weeks ago will need attention. This may include honouring a forgotten promise.  SCORPIO OCT 24 – NOV 22 Venus in your sign reminds you of the importance of feeling good when you look in the mirror. No matter what stage the diet may be at you can still look fabulous in the right clothes. Accentuate your best features, dress to kill and feel the power. Who’s that person always telling you what to do? Smile, listen, then say you appreciate the concern but you make your own decisions. SAGITTARIUS NOV 23 – DEC 21 Mars is currently working with your planet Jupiter in the most adventurous zone of your chart. If you thought you were busy before, now you’ll be at full stretch. You may find yourself wondering how you used to fill that thing called free time. But it’s called living life to the full. Just look out for the sulks with someone who’s feeling neglected. CAPRICORN DEC 22 – JAN 20 You may feel as if you’re constantly playing the waiting game, but it’s a game that you can win hands down. In the meantime, dwell less on what could go wrong. Instead try imagining a plan working out and how wonderful you’ll feel when everything drops into place. It’s not the easiest of times for starting new projects, but it’s ideal for doing that research. AQUARIUS JAN 21 – FEB 18 Just when you think life is a bit too samey along comes a change of events that will need a lot of your attention. Even if this is something that you’ve been hoping for it’s still a different matter when a possibility becomes a reality. The bigger issue is all about climbing out of any kind of rut, so pace yourself but trust that fate is steering you in the right direction. PISCES FEB 19 – MAR 20 If you find yourself crying easily over something small it’s almost certainly because something big is very close to the surface. Nobody should bottle up their emotions, but that’s especially true for a Pisces. Cry on the shoulder of the person who knows you inside out.
