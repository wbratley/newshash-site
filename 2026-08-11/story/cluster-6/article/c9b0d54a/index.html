Reform announces plans to deport all foreign criminals to El Salvador if Nigel Farage becomes Prime Minister

Reform announces plans to deport all foreign criminals to El Salvador if Nigel Farage becomes Prime Minister - See more Daily Mail on Google - save us as a Preferred Source Reform UK plans to deport foreign criminals to jail in El Salvador if Nigel Farage becomes Prime Minister, the party announced today. The party leader unveiled the measures today alongside Zia Yusuf at a press conference at the Royal Horseguards Hotel, in London. Under the proposals, all 10,000 foreign criminals 'would be deported from our jails' and sent to the Central American country if their home nations refuse to take them. They would also be banned from returning to Britain. Mr Farage also pledged to create 12,000 new prison places if he was handed the keys to 10 Downing Street by building 'pretty cheap' and 'basic' pop-up Nightingale prisons. He said the policy would create an extra 22,000 prison places compared to Andy Burnham's plans. Britain's annual £600 million bill to imprison foreign nationals would be slashed by half under Reform UK's prison plans, Mr Yusuf said. 'To house a foreign national offender in El Salvador will be half the cost of housing a foreign national offender in this country, and will give us more prison spaces,' Mr Farage said. Mr Yusuf said under its proposals the party can pledge that 'killers, rapists, and violent thugs would never be released a day early'. Mr Farage had earlier told reporters: 'Prison sentencing has proved to be Andy Burnham's first big test as Prime Minister. 'In the wake of the PC (Andrew) Harper case, the Prime Minister's instincts were, "well, of course they shouldn't be released early". 'But once he's told the practical facts of life, he has to back away and back down.' Lee Anderson, the party's chairman, is to travel to the Central American country within months for talks with President Nayib Bukele's government. Mr Farage has held initial discussions with Salvadoran officials about the possibility of renting places at the country's prisons, which are known worldwide for their tough, spartan regimes. Nigel Farage has already held discussions with Salvadoran officials about the possibility of renting places at the country's jails, which are known for being tough Inmates at El Salvador's Counter-Terrorism Confinement Centre (Cecot) are crammed behind floor-to-ceiling bars with nothing to do Mr Farage pledged to create 12,000 new prison spaces through the construction of pop-up Nightingale prisons and promised an end to early release for dangerous offenders and reintroduce short sentences for shoplifters. Unveiling Reform's prisons policy last night, the party's home affairs spokesman Mr Yusuf vowed to 'change the law so that those who commit serious crimes such as murder, manslaughter, rape and serious violence serve their full sentences'. He said: 'Unbelievably, there are over 10,000 foreign nationals in British jails. 'We will deport all foreign nationals from our jails. If their home countries won't take them back we will send them to serve their sentences in countries like El Salvador and Kosovo. 'We will use the space created to ensure that serious criminals stay where they belong. Behind bars.' The plans echo a US policy in which Donald Trump struck an agreement with El Salvador to deport migrants from the United States. Mr Farage today said the 'pretty cheap' pop-up Nightingale prisons would be for 'lower-level' offenders. 'They would be very basic, and we have to admit that. There might not be that many colour TVs,' the party leader said. He added: 'They’re going to be people who have been convicted of things like shoplifting, for example. But absolutely, they will be secure.' Temporary Nightingale hospitals and courts were introduced during the pandemic to provide additional space for treatment and hearings. Mr Farage said sending foreign prisoners overseas or back to their home countries would save Britain several billion pounds over the next decade. Mr Farage also promised an end to early release for dangerous offenders and to reintroduce short sentences for shoplifters. Mr Yusuf said the measures would halve the £600 million yearly cost of imprisoning foreigners. He said it means the party can pledge that 'killers, rapists, and violent thugs would never be released a day early'. 'The British people are being tormented by convicted criminals who should be in jail,' he said. 'We live in a country that is becoming ever more lenient on those who break our laws, and ever harsher on those who follow them. All that changes under a Reform government.' Mr Yusuf said the conditions in El Salvador would not 'be a summer camp' but vowed to make sure they wouldn't be cruel. It comes as the Government has faced pressure over its early release scheme, which starts in October in a bid to tackle prison overcrowding. Some 5,000 people are expected to be released early under sentencing changes, down from 6,000 after the exemptions announced as part of an urgent review of the scheme. Under current plans, PC Andrew Harper's killers remain eligible to be freed halfway through their 13-year sentences after they were convicted of manslaughter in 2020 following Mr Harper's death in 2019. More than 850,000 people have signed a petition calling for Jessie Cole and Albert Bowers to remain in jail, with a Government source saying the Prime Minister is prepared to 'explore every option' to block their early release. El Salvador's most famous prison is the Terrorism Confinement Centre (Cecot). A cornerstone of Mr Bukele's war on drug cartels, it houses thousands of shaven-headed inmates who sit in silence in windowless concrete cells where they are kept locked up for 23-and-a-half hours a day. 'They just sit on their bunks, day in day out, and the prison lights stay on 24/7, never dimmed. All will die in this prison. It's a living death,' he said. The 57-acre facility was built to hold up to 40,000 prisoners and currently houses an estimated 15,000 inmates. Many are suspected members of rival gangs that terrorised the country for decades, alongside convicted murderers and rapists. Mr Madeley said: 'Nothing, absolutely nothing, can prepare you for the sight of 3,000 shaven-headed men crammed behind floor-to-ceiling bars. No doors. No screening. 'They sit there in permanently open view through the bars, on tiers of metal bunks four-high - no mattresses, just thin cotton sheets - staring out. It's one hell of a sight' President Bukele ordered the mega-prison to be built in March 2022 as part of his campaign against El Salvador's gangs, and it opened a year later. There are no workshops, libraries, opportunities to learn kitchen skills and no visitors. Mr Madeley added: 'All meals must be taken in their cells, inside which they spend 23-and-a-half hours every day, with just 30 minutes outside for brief, heavily guarded exercise.' A Labour Party spokesman said: 'Keeping the public safe is and always will be Labour's first priority. The Government has already made clear we are working at pace to deport foreign criminals and we're building more prison places to deliver justice for victims and keep criminals behind bars. 'Reform have no serious answers to the Tory prisons crisis and would lead the system to collapse. This would result in a free-for-all for dangerous and violent offenders, including murderers and rapists. 'Nigel Farage and Reform simply can't be trusted to keep Britain safe.'
