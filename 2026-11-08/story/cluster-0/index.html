Trump offers Dana White White House press secretary role after midterm losses

Unbiased summary

President Donald Trump posted on Truth Social offering the White House press secretary job to UFC CEO Dana White, calling him a 'violent Fighter and Warrior' and suggesting he bring six UFC fighters as assistants. The offer followed Republican midterm election losses and the ousting of Chief of Staff Susie Wiles, who was replaced by Stephen Miller. White, a longtime Trump supporter, responded ambiguously, saying he was flying to Washington for a Monday meeting with Trump. Reports conflicted on whether White had declined or was considering the offer, with one White House aide describing it as a joke. The press secretary position had been vacant since Katie Zacharia quit after five days in mid-October.
