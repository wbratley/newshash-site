A key former staffer and ally of Robert F Kennedy Jr accused outgoing Chief of Staff Susie Wiles of taking money from corporations and foreign interests to sabotage RFK's Make America Healthy Again (MAHA) agenda. While many, including Trump, celebrated the work Wiles did, former Kennedy advisor Calley Means accused Wiles of pay to play involving her daughter in an interview with Joe Rogan. Means, who departed the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) two weeks ago, alleged that companies willing to hire Katie Wiles for lobbyist work would get preferential treatment in the White House. One of his most explosive allegations was that companies who hired the daughter of Susie Wiles, Trump's longtime White House chief of staff, as a lobbyist received higher priority for funding from the administration. 'If you're a special interest and you want to get something done, the secret's out. You pay Susie's daughter, and it was openly discussed when they had a client,' he told Rogan. Means also blamed Wiles for shutting down MAHA reforms because of pressure from corporations and need for fundraising money. 'Susie said to me many times: "We need money for the midterms, Calley. We need money for the midterms,"' he said, accusing her of shutting down a study into the effects of sugars on children.
