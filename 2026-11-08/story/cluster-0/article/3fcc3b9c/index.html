Dana White speaks out after being offered press secretary role as he reveals he's flying to DC 'right now' and will meet Trump in just hours

Article text

Dana White has broken his silence after being offered the job of White House press secretary by Donald Trump, revealing he is flying to Washington, DC, immediately and will meet the president face-to-face on Monday. The UFC boss chuckled when questioned about the extraordinary offer by a reporter in London on Saturday, before declaring: 'I'm flying to Washington, DC, right now.' White, 57, refused to reveal whether he had already discussed the position with Trump but confirmed he would be meeting his longtime friend at the White House. 'I'm sure we'll have more to talk about after that,' he teased, keeping speculation swirling over whether he could actually become the administration's next chief spokesperson. 'I've got a lot of jobs already,' he added when pressed by a reporter at a press conference. His comments come after conflicting reports emerged over whether White was seriously considering the job or had already rejected the president's offer. TMZ reported that the fight promoter would meet Trump in the Oval Office on Monday to discuss the position, citing sources with direct knowledge of the planned meeting. But CNN claimed White had already turned down the unorthodox proposal, according to a source familiar with the matter, while one White House aide suggested Trump had been joking. Now White's confirmation that he is heading to Washington has added another twist to the saga, although he has stopped short of saying whether he intends to accept the role.
