Israel's President Herzog tasks opposition leader Gadi Eisenkot with forming government

Unbiased summary

Israel's president, Isaac Herzog, announced on Saturday that he would task opposition leader Gadi Eisenkot, head of the Yashar party, with forming a new government. The decision follows consultations with all parties after the 27 October election, in which neither Eisenkot's opposition bloc nor Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's coalition secured a majority in the 120-seat Knesset. Eisenkot's bloc won 48 seats, while Netanyahu's camp won 53; Arab-led parties won 19 seats, potentially making them kingmakers. Herzog's office stated that 67 Knesset members recommended Eisenkot and 46 recommended Netanyahu. Eisenkot will have 28 days to form a coalition, with a possible 14-day extension. The formal appointment ceremony is scheduled for Sunday.
