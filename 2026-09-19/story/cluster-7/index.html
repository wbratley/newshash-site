England squad announced: Alexander-Arnold and Palmer return, several players omitted

Unbiased summary

Thomas Tuchel named a 27-man England squad for four Nations League matches. Trent Alexander-Arnold and Cole Palmer were recalled, while uncapped players Rio Ngumoha and Alex Scott were included, along with Jarrad Branthwaite. Several players were left out, including Morgan Gibbs-White, Phil Foden, and Adam Wharton, while Ollie Watkins, Ivan Toney, and Noni Madueke were dropped from the World Cup squad. Tuchel noted Alexander-Arnold needs to improve defensively and justified Gibbs-White's omission by saying it 'makes no sense' to select more than three No 10s.
