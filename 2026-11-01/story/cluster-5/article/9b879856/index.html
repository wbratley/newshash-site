RAF drones monitor Russian ships in English Channel for first time – after Vlad ‘sends fishing boats to spy on Britain’

Article text

RAF drones are now actively policing British waters to track Russian naval fleets for the very first time, it emerged last night. The unprecedented move comes after fears Vladimir Putin and the Kremlin are ramping up sabotage operations in Europe and preparing for a conflict with the UK and other NATO allies. Now, the Ministry of Defence have confirmed that an unarmed British Protector RG1 drone was recently deployed above the English Channel to shadow a Russian warship. Operating between late September and mid-October alongside Royal Navy warships and helicopters, the aircraft delivered more than ten hours of “intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance coverage”. The state-of-the-art surveillance craft is one of 16 unmanned models ordered by the RAF, with 11 currently operational in the fleet. Wes Streeting, the Defence Secretary, said: “Our Armed Forces work together every day, around the clock, to keep our country safe and safeguard our national interests.
