Allies of Susie Wiles are furious after President Donald Trump announced she would be leaving her role as White House Chief of Staff, handing control to hardline adviser Stephen Miller in the wake of Republicans' bruising midterm losses. Trump announced Friday that Miller would take over the powerful position on Monday, while Wiles would be moved to the Trump Political Operation. The Daily Mail first reported on Wiles' planned departure back in June, which Wiles herself fiercely pushed back on. She dismissed it as 'Friday fiction' and insisted she was 'not going anywhere.' The shake-up comes just days after Democrats made major gains in the midterms, leaving Republicans facing questions over their campaign strategy and who should take responsibility for the losses. Trump praised Wiles as having done an 'outstanding job' and insisted she had 'done a great job' as chief of staff. Wiles thanked President Trump for the opportunity to serve as his chief of staff on X, saying it had been 'the honor of a lifetime.'
