Prince Harry and five others seek leave to appeal dismissal of privacy claims against Associated Newspapers

Unbiased summary

Prince Harry, Baroness Doreen Lawrence, Sir Elton John, David Furnish, Elizabeth Hurley and Sadie Frost have applied to the Court of Appeal for permission to challenge Mr Justice Nicklin's July ruling dismissing their privacy claims against Associated Newspapers, publisher of the Daily Mail and Mail on Sunday. The claimants alleged unlawful information gathering, including voicemail interception and blagging, but the judge found they had not proved any information was obtained unlawfully. In August, the judge ordered the claimants to pay an initial £9.54m towards the publisher's costs on an indemnity basis, with the total potentially reaching £34.5m. Associated Newspapers strongly denied the allegations and described the judgment as an overwhelming victory. A spokesperson for the claimants said they respectfully disagree with the decisions, which they consider legally and factually flawed.
