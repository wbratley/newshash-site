Six-year-old boy dies after Waterford house fire that killed four family members

Unbiased summary

Six-year-old Elliot English died on Friday at Children's Health Ireland hospital in Crumlin, Dublin, from injuries sustained in a fire at his family home in the Birchwood area of Waterford on 13 October. His parents, Kim and Philip English, and brothers Callum, 12, and Jayden, nine, also died in the fire. Another child remains in critical condition. Gardaí are treating the incident as a multiple murder investigation. Six people have been arrested; four were released without charge, and Jessica Dalton, 31, and Cian Stacey, 21, have been charged with criminal damage and assault causing serious harm and remanded in custody. A Garda spokesperson expressed condolences to the extended English family.
