Russell Brand convicted of rape and sexual assault; PM considers rape trial changes

Unbiased summary

On Thursday, Russell Brand was found guilty at Southwark Crown Court of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault against four women between 2004 and 2009. He was cleared of two other rape charges and one attempted indecent assault. Brand was remanded in custody ahead of sentencing on Monday. Following the verdict, Prime Minister Andy Burnham said he would consider changes to rape trials to prevent women from feeling 'on trial', after Met Police Commissioner Sir Mark Rowley warned against 'victim blaming' and 'rape myths'. Rowley also said he would not be surprised if more victims came forward, revealing two additional rape investigations. Brand's barrister, Ian Winter KC, self-reported to the Bar Standards Board after a confrontation with a journalist.
