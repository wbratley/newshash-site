Two arrested after gunshots damage car in Swansea; critical incident declared

Unbiased summary

South Wales Police responded to reports of an incident on the A48 in Morriston, Swansea, on Friday, November 7. A vehicle was found with damage consistent with a firearms discharge. A 41-year-old man from Llansamlet was arrested on suspicion of possessing a firearm with intent to endanger life, and a 16-year-old boy from Llansamlet was arrested on suspicion of affray. One person presented to police with minor injuries; no major injuries or fatalities were reported. The incident was declared critical, and a criminal investigation is underway. Road closures were put in place, and a local fireworks display was cancelled. Police urged the public to avoid the area and not to share unverified rumours.
