Weekly horoscope for August 16 – 22: Maggie Hyde reveals what the stars have in store for every zodiac sign

Article text

THE Sun’s world famous Mystic Meg horoscope column has entertained readers for decades with predictions for their love lives, money and careers. Read on to see what Mystic Meg with Maggie Hyde has written in the stars for you today. ARIES MAR 21 – APR 20 Not everything will go according to plan but somehow this works out for the better. You don’t need to try and lead everything because the universe has already figured it out. What a relief! So, listen with interest to your workmates or holiday companions, maybe even choosing one of their ideas over your own. Write down the things you see in a dream, as these are omens. TAURUS APR 21 – MAY 21 If you’d decided that a holiday was off the agenda this summer then think again. Chatting with friends shows you how to avoid expensive mistakes or share the costs. Most read in Fabulous Spending more time outdoors is so good for you. Go somewhere green, whether it’s the local park or countryside. Making new friends in your community is a blessing for now and the future. GEMINI MAY 22 – JUNE 21 The person you were a year ago may feel like a stranger. Life can change very fast, but if anyone can keep up it’s a quick-witted Gemini. Thinking young is also one of your strengths and is the key to paving the way to an exciting future. There are two holiday ideas on the table but if you use your imagination there’s every chance you’ll find a way to do both. CANCER JUNE 22 – JULY 22 You may be a creature of habit but Mars in your sign has something else in mind other than comfortable routines. The scene is being set for changes that are probably overdue but will be worth the effort. Whatever your main concern may be, heed advice from a man in the family. It cuts to the chase, and could save you a lot of trouble, or money.  LEO JULY 23 – AUG 23 Monday morning won’t be one of your best starts to a week, it has to be said, but that’s partly down to bad sleep. Or too much partying that you might be regretting. Trust me, and don’t share those embarrassing photos! Lucky Jupiter quickly gets you back on track, and you can take heart that at least one issue will be resolved naturally in your favour. VIRGO AUG 24 – SEPT 22 You’re great at being a role model for your kids or for those younger than you in your work place. Your talent for voicing a few carefully chosen words makes them see that something is possible after all. There’s news about someone from the past, or there’s the chance to heal a rift that’s already lasted too long. LIBRA SEPT 23 – OCT 23 Others might want to reach agreements without lots of deliberation but that’s not your style. Someone close needs to understand that you don’t just need to explore things from all sides, you enjoy doing it too! Joining forces with others, especially the women in your life, is the key to just about everything this week, from having fun to getting jobs done.  SCORPIO OCT 24 – NOV 22 The week may start with a lot of grey areas but these will be cleared up, and more quickly than you’d thought possible. There’s a definite turning point, especially with an individual or a situation that’s been nothing but a worry. Pay close attention to unfolding events on Tuesday when the Moon arrives in your sign. You may even have a psychic moment. SAGITTARIUS NOV 23 – DEC 21 Your planet Jupiter starts the week with some big, bold reminders about making your presence felt. This isn’t the time to hide your light. Avoidance doesn’t suit you and those who matter will respect and appreciate your directness. Listen to the warning bell though around your energy. All fire signs are prone to burnout and you need to factor in plenty of rest. CAPRICORN DEC 22 – JAN 20 Trying not to interfere may be backfiring. If you want problems to go away, especially those that involve family or property, you need to get more involved. Do what you do best, which is to solution find with lots of common sense thrown into the mix. Make meal times family times. Also be there for a friend, especially one who’s too proud to ask for help. AQUARIUS JAN 21 – FEB 18 You know all about strong opinions. You invented them, especially on the subject of right and wrong. But do these keep you locked into what you already know, rather than opening your eyes to new possibilities? Let others have their say. And let them see your softer side too, especially your kids. They love it when you let your halo slip and tell silly jokes. PISCES FEB 19 – MAR 20 Don’t let that tendency to fear the worst take over. A worry can grow arms and legs way too fast, and then turn out to be unnecessary after all. Your body will love it when you stick to healthy eating or stop overdoing things.
