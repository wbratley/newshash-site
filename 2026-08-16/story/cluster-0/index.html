Jason Arday Found Dead

Unbiased summary

Jason Arday, a former Cambridge professor, was found dead at his home in south London. He had resigned from his position last week amid allegations of plagiarism, which he denied. The police are treating his death as unexpected but not suspicious. Arday was 41 years old and had been a professor of sociology of education at the University of Cambridge. His family has released a statement saying they are in shock and that a 'campaign of misinformation' had been too much for him.
