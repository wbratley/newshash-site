UK PM excludes manslaughter and indecent assault from early prison release scheme

Unbiased summary

Prime Minister Andy Burnham announced changes to the early prison release scheme, excluding offenders convicted of unlawful killing, including manslaughter and death by dangerous driving, as well as those convicted of indecent assault. The decision follows public backlash over the potential early release of PC Andrew Harper's killers, Jessie Cole and Albert Bowers. The scheme was originally designed to ease prison overcrowding by releasing certain prisoners early. The changes will keep hundreds more people in prison. To create additional prison space, the government plans to deport foreign prisoners and eventually review indefinite sentences known as IPPs, though these measures may take years. The changes will be introduced via secondary legislation next week.
