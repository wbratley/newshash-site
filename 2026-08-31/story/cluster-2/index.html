Cleverly resigns shadow cabinet to seek Conservative nomination for London mayor

Unbiased summary

James Cleverly, the Conservative MP for Braintree and former home secretary, resigned from Kemi Badenoch's shadow cabinet to run for the Conservative nomination for London mayor. He announced his bid via an op-ed in The Sunday Times, framing his candidacy around enterprise, opportunity, and support for Badenoch. Cleverly criticised Labour mayor Sadiq Khan and Greater Manchester mayor Andy Burnham, accusing Burnham of being anti-London and claiming Khan's policies, including Ulez, have harmed drivers. He dismissed Reform UK's threat, calling Nigel Farage a 'toddler' and insisting Reform is past its peak. The 2028 mayoral election is expected to see Khan possibly seeking another term, though he has not confirmed. Cleverly's campaign is seen as a contrast to the previous Tory candidate Susan Hall's more right-wing approach.
