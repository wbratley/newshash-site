From sustainable sponges to seriously comfy walking boots: the best August bank holiday deals on the products we love

Article text

August bank holiday is summer’s Boxing Day: the fun’s over, you’ve got no money left, and it’s probably raining. (As someone whose birthday falls at the fuzzy end of August, I can confirm that it always, always rains.) And, just as on Boxing Day, the shops erupt in discount fever. Retailers aren’t cutting prices to soothe our post-holiday blues. They’ve got a whole season’s stock to shift, and need to free up space for autumn/winter pieces and (sorry) Christmas. There’s also a solid chance that they need to encourage online spending, because when the sky turns grey the high-street looks as inviting as wet socks. Here we round up the best bank holiday reductions on products our testers love, including autumn essentials such as dehumidifiers, duvets and women’s walking boots. As ever when scouring for deals, we’ve used price-checking tools such as PriceRunner and Idealo to check price histories, compare retailers and ensure that all these deals are the real … deal. The best August bank holiday deals on the products we love The best home deals The plastic-free sponges Seep sponge scourers, pack of 12, £28.50 (was £36) One of the easiest kitchen plastic detox swaps you can make is to ditch your two-tone sponges for a compostable alternative. Seep’s dishwasher-safe sponges, made from loofah and wood pulp cellulose, have been recommended many times in the Filter, including in Hannah Rochell’s 12 sustainable cleaning subscriptions and Juliet Kinsman’s 13 ways to make your bathroom more sustainable. This deal gives you 25% off a whole year’s stash (12 sponges). The heated airer to dry your clothes fast Minky SureDri heat pod drying system Of all the heated clothes airers I’ve tested, Minky’s “pod” is one of the most effective – and noisiest, and cumbersome to set up. But it really works, and it doesn’t take up much storage space because you use it with your existing clothes horse. Load your clothes horse with washing, cover it (cover included, or use a sheet), and use the Minky pod to blast hot air into it for speedy drying results. Its Argos sale price of £27 makes it a genuine bargain. The best budget coffee maker De’Longhi Stilosa EC230, £89 (was £105) “No, it’s not a typo: the Stilosa EC230 really is that affordable,” wrote our caffeine guru Sasha Muller in his guide to the best coffee machines, and that’s when it cost over £100. Argos has now dropped the price to £89 for the bank holiday weekend. “It would be a false economy if it didn’t deliver the espresso-based goods,” wrote Sasha, and reassures us that it very much does. “The water tank feels cheap, and there’s no option to fit a water filter,” but at this price we’re not complaining. The bargain mattress topper for a softer sleep Silentnight Airmax 1000 topper, double, £30.67 (was £45.99) “Looks perturbingly like a puffa jacket or bouncy castle,” I said of this Silentnight bargain in best mattress toppers, but stay with me here: it really works, adding airy softness and comfort to a bed that feels too hard. Unlike most mattress toppers, it washes perfectly happily in the machine and costs well under £50, even when Argos isn’t cutting prices. The best duvet we’ve tested Panda the Cloud bamboo duvet, 10.5-tog double, £116.96 (was £129.95) Panda’s gloriously breathable, huggable duvet made this insomniac actually look forward to going to bed when I tested the best duvets last winter, and the thought of giving it up made me almost dread summer. Happily, there’s also a 4.5-tog summer version, which is similarly discounted (down from £114.95 to £103.46 for a double). The 10.5-tog duvet will be just what you need, though, as the nights grow chilly. The dehumidifier that helps dry your laundry VonHaus smart dehumidifier 20l, £149.99 (was £169.99) “A great all-rounder for the price,” wrote Caramel Quin when she chose this versatile machine as her top budget pick when testing the best dehumidifiers. Amazon has the best deal at the time of writing, but stocks are limited there (“8% claimed” within hours of the deal launching) and you may prefer to buy from VonHaus anyway. The dehumidifier has a laundry mode, quiet sleep mode and auto shut-off option, but sadly no Hepa air filter. The best mattress we’ve tested Otty Original Hybrid, double, £540 (was £599.99) After almost five years of reviewing mattresses and well over 50 years of sleeping on them, I still haven’t found one that beats the Otty Original Hybrid for comfort and value for money. Otty’s bank holiday discounting isn’t as generous as some rivals’, but 10% off makes this mattress an even better buy, bringing even its heroic emperor size (200 x 200cm) down to £742.50. Just remember to use the code (FILTER10). The breathable wool mattress Millbrook Wool Luxury 4000 pocket sprung mattress, double, £638 (was £759) Not quite the whopping price cut suggested by the crossed-out RRP, because the double size of this mattress has been available for £759 from a few stores over recent weeks, but £638 is still a welcome reduction and excellent value for this high-quality sprung mattress. Its comfort layer of sustainable UK-sourced wool made it feel much more cosy and breathable than many other mattresses I’ve tested. The “bargain workhorse” dehumidifier ProBreeze dehumidifier with max extraction 20l, £149.99 (was £189.99) You’re not seeing double. This is, indeed, the second laundry-drying dehumidifier reduced to £149.99 that we’ve featured here. Both worth recommending, because it’ll soon be raining like the blazes and your poor laundry won’t have a chance of drying without electrical help. The powerful ProBreeze is “a bargain for such a workhorse”, wrote Caramel Quin in best dehumidifiers, although it uses more electricity than the VonHaus. For £10 extra, you can get a version with smart app control (down to £159.99 from £199.99). The best video doorbell Ring Battery Video Doorbell Pro, £149.99 (was £219.99) Andy Shaw spent 18 months testing video doorbells, including top products from brands including Blink, Google Nest and Eufy, but none could match this battery model from Ring. Video and sound quality, night vision, fast alerts and “a host of useful features” saw it “leapfrogging its rivals”, although it’s a shame that you need a subscription to get the best out of it. Argos slashes a splendid £70 off in its bank holiday sale. The best mattress for couples Simba Hybrid Pro mattress, double, £909.30 (was £1,299) Simba’s plush mattress will normally set you back £1,299 for a double, which you may deem value for money if its exceptional motion isolation allows you to sleep in peace next to a restless partner. For me, it’s too expensive – so I thoroughly approve of this 30%-off bank holiday deal, which applies site-wide. That means you can also snap up the Simba Hybrid topper, my favourite mattress topper, for £296.65 for a double, down from £349. The best luxury duvet Woolroom Deluxe Washable 3-in-1 duvet, double, £299.99 (was £399.99) It’s still pricey for a duvet, but this is no ordinary duvet. Woolroom’s magnificent creation is three duvets in one: a summer duvet, a spring/autumn duvet, and poppers that fasten the two together to create a toasty winter duvet. Its natural, sustainable fibres excel at temperature control, and you can even wash it in the machine. It takes a while to dry, though, so a heated airer or three – or the bargain dehumidifier above – may help. I’m not in the fashion loop like Jess, more the fashion black hole, but I’m going to hazard that “an airy trouser that doesn’t hug your legs should be the linchpin of your autumn wardrobe” this year too. Make a splash with these red statement trousers from Boden, now less than half price in the bank holiday sale. The slippers you can wear with socks Regatta Solace lightweight insulated slippers, £18.90 (was £30) It won't have escaped your attention that your toes have started getting cold again. They’re down to £21, but you get an extra 10% off if you copy the code OUTDOORS10 in the promo box at checkout – the catch being that you can only have them in grey, because the lovely chocolate colour has sold out. It's available in six attractive colours including red and olive, all with a handcrafted wooden J-handle and all reduced to £49 – as seems to happen every time Debenhams has a sale (it fell to the same price on Black Friday last year). People like me who have crepey, post-menopausal skin may find it particularly beneficial, thanks to the humectants and oils that add a smoothing emollient layer, but it’s a great all-rounder for soothing dryness after being outdoors.
