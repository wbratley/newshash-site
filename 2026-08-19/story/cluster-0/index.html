Three family members die after getting into difficulty in sea at Shoreham-by-Sea

Unbiased summary

On Tuesday afternoon, emergency services were called to reports of four people in difficulty in the water off Shoreham-by-Sea, West Sussex. A man, a woman, and a teenage girl from the same family died despite paramedics' efforts. A younger girl from the family was taken to hospital in critical condition. Police believe all four entered the water from the land for a swim. The coastguard completed an extensive search and were not looking for anyone else. The A259 Brighton Road was closed while emergency services responded. Witnesses described rough sea conditions due to high winds. The MP for Shoreham, Tom Rutland, expressed condolences and thanked emergency services.
