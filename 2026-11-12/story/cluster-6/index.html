Government threatens to force media industry to improve safeguarding after David Walliams allegations

Unbiased summary

An investigation by The Guardian and ITV News alleged that entertainer David Walliams sexually harassed and abused his position over several women, including two who say they were groomed at 15; Walliams denies all allegations and says he is not a sexual predator. The allegations were published after Walliams lost a legal bid to block them. In response, Culture Secretary Lisa Nandy summoned leaders from broadcasting, publishing, film and entertainment to a meeting next week, stating she is prepared to force participation in the Creative Industries Independent Standards Authority (CIISA) if the industry's response is not robust enough. The announcement also followed the sentencing of Russell Brand on rape and sexual assault charges. Multiple government ministers called for further restrictions on non-disclosure agreements. Several organisations, including the BBC, HarperCollins, and Independent Talent Group, have cut ties with Walliams, and the Labour Party is understood to have suspended his membership.
