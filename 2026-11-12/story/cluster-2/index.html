Scottish DJ James Campbell named as victim of Krakow shooting that killed six

Unbiased summary

A 32-year-old Scottish DJ, James Thomas Campbell from Prestwick, South Ayrshire, was named as one of six people killed in a shooting in Kraków, Poland, on 7 November. A 24-year-old gunman, identified by prosecutors as Michał Oksiński, opened fire with a semi-automatic rifle in the city's central market square, killing four Polish citizens, an Austrian woman, and Campbell. He also injured 15 others, including two British men: Harry Marchant, 19, from York, who is in stable condition, and two police officers who were seriously wounded while apprehending the attacker. Oksiński died in hospital on Monday after being shot by police. He is also suspected of murdering his parents and sister at the family home. Police Scotland is providing support to Campbell's family, though formal identification has not yet been completed.
