Where is Celebrity Traitors filmed? The show is filmed at Ardross Castle in Scotland, which is in the Highland region of the country and is 30 miles north of Inverness, on the banks of River Alness. The 19th Century castle is set in over 100 acres of gardens and parkland and features Scottish baronial architecture. Over the years, it has been extensively renovated, but some parts have not been touched for many years. While watching the BBC One show, it may look like the contestants stay overnight at the castle, but in fact they only eat and socialise at the venue. The contestants are then reportedly transported to and from the venue back to an unknown hotel every evening, which is believed to be near Inverness Airport. Richard wants control...
