British National Reported Killed in Ukraine While Fighting for Russia

Unbiased summary

Bradley Townsend, a 33-year-old from Barnsley, UK, has been reported killed in Ukraine while fighting for Russian forces. He died on June 10 in the Donetsk region, reportedly from a drone attack. Townsend had left the UK in April, telling family he was going to Romania, but instead traveled to Russia and signed a contract to fight. He had no military experience and was recruited online by other British nationals fighting for Russia. The UK Foreign Office confirmed awareness of the reports and is in contact with his family. His death is the first known fatality of a British citizen fighting for Russia in the conflict. The story coincides with UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham's visit to Kyiv, where he announced provision of missile blueprints to Ukraine.
