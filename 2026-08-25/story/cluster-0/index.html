NHS England to offer at-home cervical cancer screening kits to millions

Unbiased summary

NHS England announced that eligible women aged 30-65 who have missed cervical screening appointments will be offered free at-home HPV self-testing kits. The kits allow women to collect a vaginal swab at home and post it to NHS labs for testing. Those who test positive for high-risk HPV will be invited for a follow-up clinical appointment. The rollout begins today, with invitations sent via the NHS app, text, email, or letter. Approximately 4 million women are not up to date with screenings, with only 68.8% attendance. The initiative aims to increase screening uptake and reduce cervical cancer cases, part of a broader goal to eliminate cervical cancer by 2040.
