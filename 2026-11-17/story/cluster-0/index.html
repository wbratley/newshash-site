Alan Milburn publishes review on youth unemployment with welfare and employment reforms

Unbiased summary

Alan Milburn, former Labour cabinet minister, published a government-commissioned review on Tuesday into youth unemployment, recommending sweeping changes to welfare, education, and employment support to reduce the number of young people not in education, employment or training (Neet), currently around one million (13%). Key proposals include a new Youth Allowance for under-25s replacing Personal Independence Payment and the health element of Universal Credit, lower National Insurance for employers hiring young people, and a legal entitlement to job or training support. The report aims to cut the Neet rate to 5% within a decade. It comes amid political debate over welfare reform, with some Labour MPs opposed to benefit cuts. Separately, Prime Minister Andy Burnham gave a speech criticising the UK's Brexit deal ahead of an EU summit.
