Prince Harry event clashes with brother William's Earthshot prize for FIFTH year in a row as he makes surprise Ukraine visit

Article text

Prince Harry event clashes with brother William's Earthshot prize for FIFTH year in a row as he makes surprise Ukraine visit Prince Harry's surprise appearance in Ukraine marks the fifth year in a row he has clashed with Prince William's Earthshot Prize, which is staged in India this week. The Duke of Sussex once again found himself competing for global attention with his estranged brother after telling Ukrainians they would defeat Russia during a visit to the war-torn country. While Harry has repeatedly rejected suggestions that his overseas engagements are timed to overshadow William or rival Earthshot, a pattern of high-profile diary clashes has emerged every year since 2022. His Ukraine trip came as William and Kate arrived in Mumbai for what Kensington Palace has billed as the Prince of Wales's 'biggest and most ambitious' Earthshot Prize yet. William and Kate today played cricket with Sachin Tendulkar and travelled on Mumbai's metro network as they began a three-day visit to India. The tour is also Kate's most significant overseas engagement since undergoing cancer treatment two years ago. But hours earlier Harry was revealed to have visited Ukraine, where he told wounded soldiers and medics in Lviv they would 'win the war' against Russia. Details of his trip emerged at around 10pm on Sunday, only hours before William and Kate landed in India on an overnight commercial flight from Britain. The latest scheduling clash follows criticism from Ailsa Anderson, who served the late Queen for 12 years, and questioned Harry's stated desire for reconciliation with the Royal Family now he is back in the UK. She argued that the Sussexes' repeated diary overlaps with working royals suggest that 'actions speak louder than words'. A royal commentator Victoria Arbiter is also sceptical, saying of Harry's latest clash with Earthshot: 'Every time there's something.' Prince Harry addresses attendees at the Superhumans War Trauma Conference in Lviv on a trip announced as his brother and sister-in-law landed in India His visit to Ukraine emerged just as William and Kate arrived in Mumbai for what Kensington Palace has described as the Prince of Wales's 'biggest and most ambitious' Earthshot Prize event to date A spokesman for the Duke of Sussex was to comment. He said: 'The Duke’s visit to Ukraine was completed before the Prince and Princess of Wales’ first engagement in India. Reporting to the contrary is misleading and wholly inaccurate.' The pattern dates back to 2022, when the Duke and Duchess of Sussex released the trailer for their bombshell Netflix documentary just as William and Kate arrived in Boston for Earthshot. The visit marked the Prince and Princess of Wales's first overseas tour since Queen Elizabeth II's death. As the Sussexes promoted their series, in which Harry alleged William had chosen to 'scream and shout' at him during the Sandringham Summit discussions over Megxit, the Waleses were using their US trip to champion environmental innovation through Earthshot. A year later, William travelled to Singapore for the third annual Earthshot Prize ceremony, meeting finalists, business leaders and conservation groups. At the same time, Harry appeared in a tongue-in-cheek video for a charity comedy fundraiser for US veterans in New York, joking about media scrutiny, his ginger hair and taking advice from his reiki healer. In 2024, while the Princess of Wales was undergoing cancer treatment, another clash followed. Harry accepted ESPN's Pat Tillman Award for Service despite calls from some veterans for him to decline the honour, and used his speech to pay tribute to both Mary Tillman and his late mother, Princess Diana. Meanwhile, William was spending several days in South Africa for Earthshot Week ahead of the awards ceremony in Cape Town. Harry's acceptance speech fell on November 6, the same day as the Earthshot Prize ceremony. Last year's overlap generated the biggest controversy of all. Harry was forced to deny he was attempting to overshadow William's Earthshot trip to Brazil after announcing a visit to Canada at almost exactly the same moment the Prince of Wales began a five-day tour of Rio de Janeiro. The Duke released a statement on November 5, 2025, insisting the Royal Household had been informed in advance, 'as a courtesy', of his plans to travel to Toronto for events marking Remembrancetide. However, multiple sources later claimed the announcement had caught Palace aides by surprise. Buckingham Palace and Kensington Palace reportedly received no courtesy call or email, despite Harry's team's assertion that they had been notified. A royal source told The Times it had been considered 'a bit rum' internally for Harry's camp to claim the Palace had been informed when nobody in London appeared to know about the Canada trip until it was announced publicly. The Duke of Sussex had met with wounded veterans and medics during a surprise visit to Ukraine over the weekend, telling them: 'You're going to win the war.' Prince Harry's arrival in the western city of Lviv on Saturday marked his fourth visit to the country since the start of the conflict with Russia. The 42-year-old visited the Superhumans Centre – a specialised medical, prosthetics and rehabilitation facility in the city and took the stage at the first international War Trauma Conference at the All-Ukrainian War Trauma Center. Although he acknowledged he had 'taken a risk to come here', Harry told the crowd: 'Every single time I come back to Ukraine, I feel welcome, I feel warmth. 'You're going to win the war and then you're going to be set up for success.' Harry said Ukraine's experience in helping injured military personnel and civilians will become the foundation for rebuilding the country after the war. The War Trauma Conference is the first to be dedicated to modern approaches for treating and rehabilitating people suffering from combat-related trauma. Harry was dressed casually in a dark, hooded puffer jacket, dark blue trousers and brown desert boots as he attended a series of events. His most recent over the weekend is the fourth since the conflict began He was pictured grinning ear-to-ear as he held up a little boy for a photo and when he posed with organisers of Ukraine's Invictus Games. In a school sports hall, he watched wheelchair rugby players as they vied for a place in Ukraine's team at next year's Invictus Games and joined in for a game of pickleball. Ukraine is planning to send 60 competitors – its largest ever team – to the 2027 edition of the event, which is scheduled to take place in Birmingham. The country has about 1.4million combat veterans, more than 10 per cent of whom have disabilities. Meanwhile the Princess of Wales said it was 'so wonderful' to be back in Mumbai a decade after her last visit with Prince William but admitted they needed a bit more practice when it comes to cricket. The couple were bowled over by a vibrant welcome for the start of their three-day visit to India as they were greeted by cricketing all-time great Sachin Tendulkar. Schoolchildren gathered in the streets hoping to catch a glimpse of the royal visitors and their sporting hero as they arrived. Signing autographs and posing for selfies alongside Tendulkar, William asked the students: 'Is Sachin your favourite? He's like a God or a King round here isn't he?' The princess thanked the children for coming to watch them play cricket, telling them: 'Thank you so much, it's so wonderful to be here again.' Dubbed the 'Little Master', Tendulkar kicked off the royal tour with a game of street cricket at the city's Ballard Estate, an area known for its impromptu games of 'gully cricket', for their first public engagement of the visit. He met the prince and princess as they arrived, walking down a side street of the estate before stopping off at a pop-up chai tea stall. William, wearing a KARDO handwoven cotton waistcoat made by artisans in Bengal, could be seen asking Tendulkar for some batting tips before striding out to the makeshift wicket on the closed street. Gully cricket is an informal version of the sport played by Mumbai locals in the narrow residential streets and alleyways, or gullies, that run through the city. Games spring up wherever there is space, with makeshift wickets and rules that change from street to street. William was bowled out on his third ball of the day by a student who steamed in from the north end of the street. The Princess fared better when she came in to bat, connecting with a slow delivery to cheers from the crowd who could be heard over the sounds of the Swaranjali Band of drummers, adding to the carnival atmosphere. However, she also succumbed to a looping delivery from William, nicking the ball before she was caught behind. Her husband put his hands up in triumph, clearly unbothered by the prospect of getting told off later. The Prince and Princess scheduled the cricket match at the start of their three-day visit, which will see them attend William's Earthshot Prize awards on Tuesday night, to 'highlight the power of sport to bring people together', Kensington Palace said.
