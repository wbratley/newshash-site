Man charged after five police officers left injured in Belfast car ramming incident

Article text

Man charged after five police officers left injured in Belfast car ramming incident The incident occurred on Sunday, and a man has now been charged with a number of offences including dangerous driving - Bookmark Become an Independent member to bookmark this article Want to bookmark your favourite articles and stories to read or reference later? Start your Independent Membership today. Join todayAlready a member? Log in A man has been arrested after five police officers were injured when their vehicles were rammed following a pursuit. He was charged on Monday with a number of offences including dangerous driving after Sunday’s incident, which left two police cars damaged. A 27-year-old man was arrested after the pursuit and remains in custody as inquiries continue. He is expected to appear before Belfast Magistrates' Court on Tuesday 17 November. Police said officers were alerted at around 6.15pm on Sunday to a wanted man travelling northbound on the A1 towards Belfast. Attending officers initially saw the vehicle travelling city-bound on the M1 towards the Westlink, according to police. Just before the Divis Street off-slip, the suspect driver became aware of the police presence and a pursuit ensued. West Belfast district commander Superintendent Mark Conway said the driver then moved to the opposite side of the carriageway, “causing danger to other road users in the area”. Mr Conway said: "A short time later, colleagues from the armed response unit (ARU) made tactical contact with the vehicle in order to bring the pursuit to an end. The vehicle was then brought to a controlled stop. “Two patrol vehicles were damaged, with substantial damage caused to one and minor damage to the other. Five ARU officers were also injured, but were able to remain on duty. Their injuries did not require hospital treatment.” Mr Conway said that “in order to prevent the driver from making off from the scene”, a Taser was drawn and a warning was issued, but it was not discharged. The man was subsequently arrested on suspicion of a number of offences, including dangerous driving, using a motor vehicle without insurance, failing to stop for police and driving while disqualified. Mr Conway appealed for anyone with information on the incident to come forward. “Although our officers reported minor injuries on this occasion, the consequences could have been much more serious,” he said. "The officers are being appropriately supported. “Anyone with information that could assist our investigation, including dash-cam, CCTV or mobile phone footage, is asked to contact police on 101, quoting reference number 1113 15/11/26.”
