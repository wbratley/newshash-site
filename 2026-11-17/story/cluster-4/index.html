Man charged after Leeds police pursuit crash kills two; separate Birmingham van incident and Belfast ramming also lead to charges

Unbiased summary

In Leeds, a police pursuit of a blue BMW that failed to stop at 12:44am on Sunday ended when it collided with a white Volkswagen Scirocco on Gelderd Road. The VW's driver Cameron Bell, 22, died at the scene; passenger Jade Langton, 23, died in hospital. Leigh Ross, 39, of Harrogate, was charged with two counts of causing death by dangerous driving and other offences, and appeared at Leeds Magistrates' Court on Monday, where he indicated he intends to plead guilty and was remanded in custody. Two boys and a girl were arrested and bailed, and West Yorkshire Police referred itself to the IOPC. Separately, in Birmingham, two men were charged over a 14 May incident in Stirchley where a man was run over by a van after flags were removed from lamp-posts. In Belfast, a 27-year-old man was charged after five police officers were injured when their vehicles were rammed following a pursuit on Sunday.
