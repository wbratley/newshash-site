Nigel Farage Wins Clacton By-Election

Unbiased summary

Nigel Farage has won the Clacton by-election, which was triggered by his resignation as an MP amid scrutiny over his financial affairs. The by-election saw a record 34 candidates, including satirical candidate Count Binface. Farage claimed victory ahead of the official result, citing a 'convincing, overwhelming win' with more votes than in the 2024 general election. However, he did not attend the count due to an alleged 'credible threat' against him, which was disputed by Essex Police.
