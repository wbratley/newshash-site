Weekly horoscope for August 9 – 15: Maggie Hyde reveals what the stars have in store for every zodiac sign

Article text

THE Sun’s world famous Mystic Meg horoscope column has entertained readers for decades with predictions for their love lives, money and careers. Read on to see what Mystic Meg with Maggie Hyde has written in the stars for you today. ARIES MAR 21 – APR 20 Distance yourself from negativity. It affects you more than you might think. Neither do you have to seek approval for everything you want to do, especially from your family. True achievers are bolstered by their own self-belief and those who love you will be cheering you on, not standing in your way. Everyone loves a comeback and the role is all yours. TAURUS APR 21 – MAY 21 You’ve been wise to leave certain matters well alone but it’s time to force an outcome, especially where a sibling is involved. Most read in Fabulous Talking about sensitive issues could get emotional, but it’s the only way to find out what’s wrong, and how you can really help. Whatever crystallizes for you on Thursday, personally or at work, will feel right and satisfying. GEMINI MAY 22 – JUNE 21 A new mindset is always powerful for you, and never more so than now. With Mercury all fired up in your ideas zone you have a lot to think about. The best changes are the ones that break the glass ceiling. Any issue that’s been a struggle or a muddle is resolved with new information, a different attitude or the courage of conviction. CANCER JUNE 22 – JULY 22 Warrior Mars arriving in your sign is the signal to take more control, and to prove how much difference a “can do” attitude can make. However, it’s also about being selective. You can’t be all things to all people without draining your own batteries. Friendships are being sifted and sorted. Get tough with the one who continually breaks arrangements. LEO JULY 23 – AUG 23 You’ve covered more ground than you realise. Make those big decisions with confidence, or let the not so hidden hand of fate make them for you. Either way, the eclipse in your sign is a game changer, especially if you’ve been on the wrong track. You’ll look back on this week as a time of realising that there’s more to something, or someone, than meets the eye. VIRGO AUG 24 – SEPT 22 Your analytical brain sees everything but you can all too easily get lost in the details. Where a rare lapse of judgment got you in, courage will get you out, and then you can stop fretting and regroup. News from someone abroad or far away lifts your spirits and gives you itchy feet. There are travel bargains galore as soon as you start the search. LIBRA SEPT 23 – OCT 23 As much as you’re known for your ability to see all sides of a situation, and for your cooperative skills, now is not the time to be a pushover. Recognise where you are, in fact, not prepared to compromise and say so. Encourage teenagers to stand on their own two feet. The best gift is teaching them how to step into their own light and power.  SCORPIO OCT 24 – NOV 22 Any personal desire that bites the dust will throw a shadow, but will be a blessing in disguise as time will tell. You probably already have plan B tucked up your sleeve but, even if you don’t, you won’t have to wait long. You don’t have to understand everything, just take one day at a time. Even by the weekend you’ll have a new angle on a dilemma, or a person. SAGITTARIUS NOV 23 – DEC 21 Your optimistic nature is happy with the idea that life leads you to where you need to be. Whether it’s a straight road or a U-turn, the way is now clearly signposted. All changes get off to a great start when you decide to try something, and discover that you can do it. Also make a point of spending more time with those who are like-minded. CAPRICORN DEC 22 – JAN 20 Ignored issues rarely fix themselves and this is a great week for getting to grips with anything that’s been neglected for too long. If a job is just part of a long to-do list then start by recognising what you need to do yourself, and what you can entrust to others. There are plenty of helping hands out there, including someone who’d love to return a favour. AQUARIUS JAN 21 – FEB 18 Tending to your social life lifts your spirits. Start arranging things to look forward to, for next weekend but also long term. Buying tickets for an event to enjoy with family or your bestie creates a buzz of excitement and a feeling of being lucky. On that subject, Saturday is best for buying a scratch card or choosing lottery numbers with a loved one. PISCES FEB 19 – MAR 20 This week’s eclipse will sort out what really matters. The top message is that your own wellbeing comes first. Stress levels drop when you tackle life’s challenges with good old fashioned common sense. In the middle of a lot of changes the truth is that your best really is good enough, and you’ll find that you’re more resilient than you thought.
