The key waterway has been closed for much of the time since the war on Iran began in late February. Iran's politburo-like body known as the Supreme National Security Council (SNSC) said the Strait of Hormuz will not open until the United States 'corrects its behavior,' issuing new demands that could shake up talks on a deal to manage the waterway and traffic on it. The head of Iran's SNSC, Mohammad Bagher Zolghadr, has given the list that includes some previously unheard of requests. Many have been previously brought up, including ending the war permanently, stopping the US Naval blockade, withdrawing military forces. From there, Zolghadr wants the US end sanctions on Tehran, unfreeze Iranian assets and even pay reparations to the Iranians to reopen the strait. The US would also have to stop threatening and insulting Iran and end all military action against Iranian allies in the region, state news agency Islamic Republic News Agency reports. Zolghadr, who is also a commander in the powerful Revolutionary Guard, reports directly to Iran's Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei, who is currently mulling over a deal with Oman to manage the strait.
