The Greenville Police Department said in a statement: 'Based on the Medical Examiner’s determination and the findings of the investigation, Hayden Panettiere’s death has been ruled accidental.' The ruling brings the police investigation into her death to a close, the statement obtained by CNN said. Panettiere died August 16 at a residence in Greenville, South Carolina. Her on-off boyfriend Brian Hickerson and his brother, Zach, were both at the scene when Panettiere was pronounced dead. Last month, a spokesman for the Greenville County Coroner’s Office confirmed to The Daily Mail that Panettiere died from the 'toxic effects' of multiple drugs – including fentanyl. Just a few grains of powerful opioid fentanyl, which is 50 times stronger than heroin, can cause a fatal overdose. At autopsy no signs of trauma were discovered that would have contributed to the death. 'The cause of death has been ruled as toxic effects of fentanyl, 4-ANPP, Alprazolam, Methocarbamol, and Quetiapine. The manner of death has been ruled accident. 'This case is administratively closed by the Greenville County Coroner’s Office.' Methocarbamol is a prescription muscle relaxant used to treat short-term muscle pain, while Quetiapine, commonly known by the brand name Seroquel, is an antipsychotic prescription medication used to treat conditions including schizophrenia, bipolar disorder and major depressive disorder. Alprazolam, known as Xanax, is used to treat anxiety and panic disorders and 4-ANPP is a chemical used as a main building block to make illegal fentanyl. Last week, insiders told TMZ authorities believed Panettiere had fatally consumed a single oxycodone pill laced with fentanyl. Panettiere had a 'long time drug dealer' in Los Angeles who she obtained narcotics from illegally, with oxycodone among them, sources told the outlet.
