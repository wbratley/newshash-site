Russell Brand denies rape and sexual assault charges, admits misusing fame for sex

Unbiased summary

Russell Brand gave evidence at Southwark Crown Court on Wednesday, denying seven offences including rape and sexual assault against six women between 1999 and 2009. He admitted using his fame to facilitate sexual encounters, describing his past behaviour as 'reckless philandering and idiocy,' but insisted consent is 'more important than sex itself.' Brand claimed the 2023 media reports about the allegations had devastated his life and reputation, leaving him unable to persuade anyone to testify on his behalf. The prosecution cross-examined him, and he responded to specific allegations, saying he does not recall some incidents but remembers only consensual encounters. The trial continues.
