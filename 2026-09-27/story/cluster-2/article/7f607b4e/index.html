Man City verdict LIVE: Chairman breaks silence on 115 charges in new statement Manchester City have been found guilty on 114 of the 115 charges of breaching Premier League rules and an appeal is now expected as the football world reacts Manchester City have been found guilty on 114 of the 115 charges levelled against them in their battle with the Premier League over breaches of financial rules. The verdict from the independent commission has leaked and the football world is working out how to react. City have always denied the allegations and plan to appeal against the verdict, which covers a nine-year period stretching from 2009 to 2018, meaning that the saga could yet continue for some time. City's hearing against the Premier League took place way back in late 2024. “The club has diligently respected due process for eight years on the basis the Premier League board and executive would behave as an independent, impartial and fair-minded regulator, free from partisan influence," City said in a statement. Nevertheless, rivals will now be preparing any legal action that they feel appropriate and getting ready to take advantage of whatever situation City may find themselves in. The commission can impose any punishment they see fit, with points deductions, financial penalties, or even expulsion from the Premier League, all on the table. It outlines the sanctions available to the league for a club found guilty of breaches. They include: - “Suspend it from playing in League Matches” - “Deduct points scored or to be scored in League Matches” - “Recommend that the League expels the Respondent from membership” - “Order the Respondent to pay compensation unlimited in amount to any Person or to any Club” Culture secretary speaks out Lisa Nandy has also been on Radio 4 this morning and does not want to see the process dragged out any longer for Manchester City or their rivals. Former Liverpool and Aston Villa chief Christian Purslow has been on Radio 4 this morning and discussed what Manchester City's verdict could mean.
