Essex explosion live: Inferno after huge blast as 100 firefighters tackle flames More than 100 firefighters are tackling a massive fire at a farm off Southend Road between East Hanningfield and Rettendon in Essex A massive explosion at a farm in Essex has shaken buildings up to 15 miles away, with footage showing a huge fireball erupting from the site. Essex County Fire and Rescue Service said more than 100 firefighters worked to tackle a blaze involving industrial units at a farm off Southend Road, between East Hanningfield and Rettendon, near Chelmsford, on Friday evening. The blaze, which is understood to be at Highlands Farm, has been declared a major incident due to its size. Around 50 firefighters will remain at the scene overnight and into the morning. Police have cordoned off a large area around the site. "If you live or are travelling nearby, please continue to keep your windows and doors closed," the fire service said. "Please avoid the area while we deal with this incident." Key Events Footage shared on social media show vast destruction from the explosion. A huge plume of dark grey smoke could be seen rising into the sky. Aerial footage shared by news outlet Al Jazeera showed a large area on fire. Burnt vehicles and structures can be seen among the remains. Area manager Dave Bond said in a video late on Friday: "As you can see behind me, we're still dealing with significant spots of fire. However, we do have this incident under control and surrounded. "We're in the process of scaling back, but we do still have 50 firefighters on scene. We're still working closely with our partners, including police, ambulance, utility companies, and we also have a number of specialist advisers on scene, giving us support as we move through the night. "The incident will be ongoing into the weekend, so please stay away from the area and allow us to continue operations. We apologise for any inconvenience this caused, and we thank you for your patience. "The advice we're still giving to the public is that if you do smell smoke, then close your doors and windows. But please bear with us as we continue our operations to extinguish the fire." Area Manager Dave Bond has said an investigation into the blaze will be conducted. Bond said: "There will be a thorough investigation carried out to find the cause of the fire." Residents in the East Hanningfield area have received an alert warning them about low water pressure in their area as a result of the incident. It reads: “Due to the fire brigade carrying out emergency work in the area you may experience low pressure for the rest of the evening. An update will be provided in the morning. Thanks for your patience.” Taz, a popular Mediterranean and Turkish restaurant, located on Main Road, Rettendon Common, has issued a statement. Our thoughts are with everyone involved and we truly hope that everyone is safe and receiving the care they need. "Thank you for your understanding and continued support. We hope to welcome you back very soon."
