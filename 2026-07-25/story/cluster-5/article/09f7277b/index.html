British top prosecutor at ICC removed over sexual misconduct claims

British top prosecutor at ICC removed over sexual misconduct claims The allegations first surfaced in May 2024, when a third party reported claims of sexual misconduct to the ICC Don't Miss Most Read Latest Karim Khan, the 56-year-old British barrister who served as the International Criminal Court's chief prosecutor, has been removed from his post following a vote by member states over allegations of sexual misconduct. In a secret ballot held at United Nations headquarters in New York, 82 of the court's 125 member states backed his dismissal, according to diplomatic sources. Mr Khan, who took up the role as the ICC's third prosecutor in 2021, has categorically denied all wrongdoing throughout the scandal, which has unfolded over more than two years. The Bureau of the Assembly of States Parties determined that he had committed serious misconduct by pursuing an improper sexual relationship with a junior staff member and attempting to prevent her from raising a complaint. The allegations first surfaced in May 2024, when a third party reported claims of sexual misconduct to the ICC. An initial probe by the court's Independent Oversight Mechanism was shut down after the complainant chose not to take part, with investigators concluding there was not enough evidence to support the claims. A fresh referral in October 2024 saw the case handed to the UN Office of Internal Oversight Services, whose investigation spanned more than a year and produced over 5,000 pages of evidence and testimony. The complainant, a Malaysian lawyer who served as Mr Khan's direct assistant, spoke publicly for the first time last week. She told CNN: "There is no way for something to be consensual when you have such a power disparity. "What I think many people don't understand is that Mr Khan was not just my boss, he was everyone's boss." Mr Khan's legal team mounted a vigorous challenge to the proceedings, publishing an open letter on X urging member states to reject his removal.
