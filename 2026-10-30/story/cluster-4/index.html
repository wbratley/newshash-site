Ofcom sets out age-verification methods for under-16 social media ban

Unbiased summary

Ofcom has published an assessment of methods for verifying social media users' ages ahead of the UK government's planned ban on under-16s accessing major platforms, due to take effect in March 2027. The regulator identified four approaches it considers 'highly effective': photo ID matching, banking checks, digital ID services, and facial age estimation. It acknowledged that some under-16s will inevitably find ways to bypass checks, and that many adults may also need to confirm their age. Around 150,000 16- and 17-year-olds reportedly lack a passport or driving licence. Ofcom suggested a 'Challenge 25'-style approach and said it would take action against non-compliant companies. The government intends to lay legislation before the end of the year.
