Uefa Threatens to Boycott World Cup Over Fifa's Plan to Sell Stakes

Unbiased summary

Uefa, the European football governing body, has threatened to boycott the World Cup if Fifa proceeds with its plan to sell stakes in its competitions to private investors. The plan, proposed by Fifa president Gianni Infantino, would create a new commercial subsidiary to run the World Cup and other events, with external investors able to buy minority stakes. Uefa's 55 member associations have unanimously rejected the proposal, citing concerns that it would compromise the integrity of the sport. The decision has sparked a heated debate, with some arguing that the plan would bring in much-needed revenue, while others see it as a threat to the sport's values.
