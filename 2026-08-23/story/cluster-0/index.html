Seven dead, including two police officers, in wrong-way A66 crash near Middlesbrough

Unbiased summary

On Saturday, at approximately 3:39 a.m., a marked Cleveland Police vehicle collided head-on with a Volkswagen Passat traveling the wrong way on the A66 near Middlesbrough, North Yorkshire. The collision killed all seven occupants: two police officers, identified as PC Matthew Blades, 37, and PC Tom Clough, 38, and five members of the public in the Passat, later reported to be in their late teens. Police had been following the Passat after it was spotted behaving suspiciously. The Independent Office for Police Conduct (IOPC) launched a mandatory investigation. The police chief, Victoria Fuller, expressed condolences and urged the public to avoid speculation. Prime Minister Andy Burnham also expressed devastation. The road was closed for investigation.
