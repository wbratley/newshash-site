Millions experience maximum solar eclipse in the UK

Article text

The partial solar eclipse has now reached its peak in most parts of the UK. Huge crowds turned out for the spectacle after a run on eclipse-safe glasses earlier today. Most of the UK has seen more than 90 per cent of the Sun covered by the Moon this evening in the fullest solar eclipse in this country since 1999. Millions were thrust into an eerie shadowy darkness just after 7pm as the partial eclipse reached its peak. Spain, Iceland and Greenland are getting total solar eclipses this evening.
