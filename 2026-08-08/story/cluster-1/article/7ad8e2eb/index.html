And incredibly, Simon Levy, 40, committed the second murder while on bail suspected of carrying out the first. Kemi Badenoch has led a blistering criticism of the Crown Prosecution Service and Metropolitan Police after the sexual predator was released from custody time and again to continue his depraved attacks. Levy, 40, is facing a possible whole-life sentence after he was convicted of murdering two sex workers and raping and almost suffocating a third woman, with police fearing scores more may have been targeted. The police watchdog said two officers are under investigation for misconduct over potential failures, following an Old Bailey trial which exposed repeated missed opportunities to stop Levy. Officers now believe Levy may have claimed many more victims during the many occasions he was released on bail, potentially carrying out daily attacks as he stalked unsuspecting women across the capital from 2018. Writing in the Daily Mail, Conservative leader Mrs Badenoch said 'a broken system allowed him back on to the streets to continue offending', and that soft attitudes to justice mean serious criminals feel they 'can get away with' offending. Levy, who is blind in one eye, has now been convicted of carrying out two murders, two rapes, a near-fatal suffocation and 13 sexual assaults. He killed one woman, Sheryl Wilkins in Tottenham in August 2025, after remarkably being granted bail for murder. He had initially been held on suspicion of killing Carmenza Valencia-Trujillo in south London in March last year, but was let go. This was because the Crown Prosecution Service failed to oppose bail at Levy's first magistrates' court hearing, and then did not provide the judge with damning information about his previous convictions at his next appearance before a judge. There were another three crown court hearings that Levy failed to attend for various reasons, yet no warrant for his arrest was requested, leaving him free to strike again. By this time, Levy had already been arrested by British Transport Police, in November 2024, for a string of sexual assaults on the Tube network, for which he was later convicted. But delays with sorting out an identification parade meant he was released, and carried out his first rape, in January 2025, and the murder of Ms Valencia-Trujillo. Levy was first jailed for three years in 2021 for carrying out sex attacks on two women at Notting Hill Carnival and a Camden street party in 2018. Mrs Badenoch said: 'The details of his Levy's crimes are horrifying enough but what is almost as shocking is the number of opportunities the authorities had to stop him. His case is symptomatic of the litany of failures at all levels that bedevil our criminal justice system.' The rape survivor - who cannot be identified for legal reasons - even pointed officers towards the exact location of Levy's home, but she was never formally interviewed as she was withdrawing from drugs.
