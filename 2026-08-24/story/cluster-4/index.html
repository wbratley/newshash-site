Chinese Humanoid Robots Run Faster Than Usain Bolt's 100m Record at Beijing Games

Unbiased summary

At the World Humanoid Robot Games in Beijing, a bipedal robot named Tiangong Ultra ran 100 metres in 9.39 seconds, beating Usain Bolt's 2009 world record of 9.58 seconds. Another robot, Blitz, reportedly ran 9.32 seconds in a separate trial. The five-day event, with over 2,000 robots from 16 countries, includes sports such as boxing, football, and high jump. Tiangong Ultra also set a high jump record of 2.88 metres, surpassing the human best. Some robots tumbled during races, and one crashed into a safety mattress after finishing. The event is the second edition of the games, held in Beijing's Olympic speed skating arena.
