The victims were rushed to a major trauma centre after stabbings near the area in central London on Wednesday afternoon. A woman, 47, was arrested following the attack, the Metropolitan Police said – while four men were being treated for injuries. The incident is believed to be mental health-related. Video seen by the Daily Mail shows the suspect walking past the Rock & Sole Plaice restaurant on Endell Street at 12.27pm after stabbing several men. She is wearing brown trousers, a white top and a navy blue cardigan and passes several members of the public, some of whom are eating at a restaurant. Police and paramedics were called to the scene in Endell Street at about 12.30pm following the stabbings. A police cordon was put in place and an air ambulance helicopter was seen landing in nearby Trafalgar Square. The injured men are aged 34, 39, 42 and 52, according to Scotland Yard, who said a pair of scissors was seized from the scene. The arrested woman was being held on suspicion of possessing an offensive weapon and assault.
