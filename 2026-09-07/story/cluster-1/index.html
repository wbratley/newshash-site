Reform UK admits donations row 'looks bad' after undercover sting

Unbiased summary

Channel 4's undercover investigation filmed senior Reform UK figures, including chief of staff Dan Jukes and head of policy James Orr, appearing to agree to accept a £500,000 donation from a US source via a UK-based son, a potential breach of electoral law. No donation was made. Leader Nigel Farage called the incident "looks bad" but denied any law-breaking, saying he was not listening during the meeting. He suspended both officials. Robert Jenrick, the party's Treasury spokesperson, said legal advice indicated no breach and that swift action had been taken. The Metropolitan Police are considering complaints from Labour and the Liberal Democrats.
