Seven arrested as four men injured with one critical after stabbing in Bromley Seven men have been arrested after four men were stabbed in an altercation on Bromley High Street, with one victim in a critical condition. Seven men have been arrested after four men were stabbed during an altercation on Bromley High Street. Police were called at around 2.15pm today, September 10, and attended alongside London Ambulance Service and London’s Air Ambulance. Officers found four men with stab wounds. Three were taken to hospital with injuries described as non-life-threatening, while one man is in a critical condition. All seven arrested men remain in custody as police enquiries continue. Officers said there is not believed to be any wider threat to the public. A spokesperson for the London Ambulance Service said: "We were called at 2:19pm today (10 September) to reports of a stabbing on Bromley High Street, BR1. “We sent ambulance crews, a paramedic in a fast response car, an incident response officer and an advanced paramedic.
