The secret agents behind Robert Pattinson's stratospheric rise from awkward teenage am-dram actor to global superstar and James Bond

Article text

As Robert Pattinson was officially unveiled as the new James Bond, there was a noticeably large group jubilantly cheering at London's Piccadilly Circus. After weeks of intense speculation, as the clock struck 3pm on Monday, an iconic image of Pattinson wearing a tuxedo and holding Bond's signature weapon was unveiled on a massive billboard in the heart of London's West End. Cheering down below were a large group of employees from Curtis Brown, one of Britain's leading literary and talent agencies, who had made the short walk from their Regent Street office to celebrate their star client becoming only the seventh Bond in history. 'The team at Piccadilly Circus celebrate the unveiling of the new James Bond - our very own Robert Pattinson,' Jonny Geller, CEO of the agency since 2012, wrote alongside a video of the team cheering. In another post, he added: 'Huge congrats to my colleagues Sarah and Grace for discovering this young actor.' We can reveal that Mr Geller was referring to Sarah Spear and Grace Clissold, the two women behind Pattinson's stratospheric rise from awkward schoolboy actor to Hollywood stardom. It's understood Ms Spear and Ms Clissold, who merged their formidable list of A-listers to become co-agents in 2003, have represented Pattinson 'from the very beginning' after discovering him in a youth theatre when he was just a teenager. Now aged 40, the British actor is on a generational run, garnering critical acclaim for his recent performances in Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey, The Batman, Primetime, and Dune: Part Three. Robert Pattinson has been named as the next James Bond, taking over from Daniel Craig Sarah Spear (left) and Grace Clissold (right) have Pattinson's agents since they first discovered him as a teenager Sarah Spear (with glasses) and Grace Clissold (right) with staff at Curtis Brown who gathered at Picadilly Circus to watch the unveiling of Robert Pattinson as James Bond Pattinson is slightly older than some of his rivals who had been lined up for 007, but he's also the most famous. He landed his first big role more than two decades ago, playing Cedric Diggory in Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire. But it was his starring role alongside Kristen Stewart in the Twilight film series that established him as a Hollywood heartthrob and paved the way for his rise to the top. Their on-screen romance as Edward Cullen and Bella Swan crossed over into their real lives, dating for four years. The five films became a global phenomenon that propelled them both to fame and grossed over $3.35billion worldwide. But where did our new 007 come from? Born in Barnes, south-west London, Pattinson was the youngest of three children. His father traded classic cars and his mother worked for a modelling agency. He first became interested in acting while he was at school, joining the amateur Barnes Theatre Club. He admitted he first joined 'because a lot of pretty girls went there' but accepted he 'would not be acting if it wasn't for that little club'. Pattinson joked that he worked backstage until 'all the good people left and I was the only one tall enough to play the lead'. And it appears it was around this time that Ms Spear and Ms Clissold discovered there was a budding star in front of their eyes. Given their huge roster of stars, the A-list reps have been somewhat secret agents themselves, staying out of the limelight over the last two decades. After graduating from the University of Exeter, Ms Spear began her career as an agent at Independent Creative Management Limited (ICM), a British talent agency operating out of Elstree Studios, and later moved to London Management. She made her big move to Curtis Brown in 2002 and has been there for the last 24 years. Ms Clissold, who joined London Management after graduating from Royal Holloway, University of London, moved with Ms Spear to Curtis Brown the same year. The following year, they merged their astonishing list of film stars together and launched an office as co-agents. Today, they are representing more than 50 stars together - including Pattinson, Slumdog Millionaire star Dev Patel, Line of Duty's Keeley Hawes and Australian actress Isla Fisher. And no matter the stage of the actor's career, they have been a huge driving force behind their clients' success. Another of their actors, Richard E. Grant, 69, played a starring role as the aristocratic patriarch of the Catton family in Saltburn in 2023, while he won viewers over with his no-nonsense approach on Celebrity Traitors this year. Curtis Brown CEO Jonny Geller hailed Sarah Spear and Grace Clissold for discovering Pattinson Ms Spear and Ms Clissold also represent Saltburn actor Richard E. Grant and Slumdog Millionaire star Dev Patel They also took on child actor Thomas Brodie-Sangster, who has become an international film star Australian actress Isla Fisher and Line of Duty star Keeley Hawes are also on their list of stars Meanwhile at the other end of the scale, Mia Goth, 33, recently starred alongside the likes of Pattinson, Anne Hathaway, Tom Holland and Zendaya in The Odyssey. She has predominantly starred in horror films, including A Cure for Wellness, Infinity Pool and the 'X' trilogy. Meanwhile, they also poached Meg Bellamy, 24, after her stellar performance as Kate Middleton in the sixth season of The Crown in 2023. 'The auditions I get now would have been my wildest dreams,' she said, talking about her new agents, in 2024. Ms Spear and Ms Clissold also represent Thomas Brodie-Sangster, 36, who broke out as a child actor in films such as Love Actually and Nanny McPhee and has gone on to become an international star in the Maze Runner, The Queen's Gambit and Wolf Hall. Finn Jones, 38, best known for his roles as Loras Tyrell in Game of Thrones, Gavin & Stacey's Ruth Jones, 60, actor and screenwriter Mark Gatiss, 60, and Spooks star Robert Glenister, 66, are also represented by Ms Spear and Ms Clissold. But it's clear that Pattinson has been their greatest success. 'James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honour of a lifetime,' he said after his role was officially announced yesterday. 'To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement. 'I couldn't be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team.' Pattinson previously joked that he would not be a great fit for the special agent because he was 'not very good at running.' But he fended off competition from Jack Lowden, Callum Turner and Josh O'Connor to secure one of the most iconic roles in British history to top off an incredible year. At around 3pm on Monday, Robert Pattinson was unveiled as the new James Bond He recently starred as To Catch a Predator host Chris Hansen in Primetime, which is in cinemas at the moment, and The Drama, alongside Zendaya. Many have said his audition for Bond was his starring role as agent Neil in Christopher Nolan's 2020 film, Tenet. He then went on to star as Bruce Wayne in The Batman in 2022 and played the lead role in Bong Joon Ho's Mickey 17 last year. The Daily Mail's Alison Boshoff revealed last night that Barbara Broccoli was interested in Pattinson for the role way back in 2019, but cooled on the idea when he was cast as Batman. Yesterday her successors at Amazon, to whom the franchise was sold last year, announced that Pattinson will star in three Bond movies, starting next year. Director Villeneuve said: 'Before anything else, I want to express my deep gratitude to the remarkable actors who joined us on this journey. We were surrounded by extraordinary talent, but there can only be one 007. 'I am overjoyed to welcome Robert Pattinson as our new James Bond. He has the timeless soul of 007 and brings a spirit that is completely new. I cannot wait to begin.'
