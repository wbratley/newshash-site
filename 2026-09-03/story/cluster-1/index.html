Two civilians dead, suspect killed, two officers wounded in Minneapolis apartment shooting

Unbiased summary

On September 2, 2023, at approximately 4:36 p.m., Minneapolis police received multiple 911 calls reporting a shooting at a high-rise apartment building on East 15 Grant Street. Officers arrived at 4:39 p.m., found one victim dead at the scene, and heard additional gunfire. They followed the sound to an upper floor, encountering a haze that limited visibility. Two officers were shot—one in the leg and one in the stomach—and a third sustained a non-life-threatening injury. The suspect was later found dead. A second civilian victim died after arriving at Hennepin County Medical Center. Seven patients were transported to the hospital. Officials confirmed three fatalities total, including the suspect. Police and federal agents (FBI, ATF) responded, and Governor Tim Walz offered state support. Witnesses described hearing rapid gunfire and seeing a resident with a pistol. The shooting occurred near Loring Park, a densely populated area.
