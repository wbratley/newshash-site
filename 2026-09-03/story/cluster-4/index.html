Four adults and a teenager charged in Sheffield newborn stabbing death

Unbiased summary

On Sunday morning, police were called to a property in Holywell Heights, Wincobank, Sheffield, over concerns for an infant. A newborn girl, referred to as Baby S, was found dead in an outbuilding, wrapped in a towel inside a blue bag placed on a washing machine. A post-mortem confirmed she died from 33 wounds caused by 15 separate stabbings. Four Slovak nationals—Andrea Skopova, 20; Peter Horvath Jr, 19; Peter Horvath Sr, 38; and Nina Horvathova, 37—were charged with murder, causing or allowing the death of a child, and perverting the course of justice. A 15-year-old girl, legally unnamed, was charged with perverting the course of justice. All five were remanded in custody and appeared at Sheffield Magistrates' Court on Wednesday, with a scheduled crown court appearance on Thursday. Two other women, aged 26 and 37, were arrested on suspicion of murder and released on bail. Police have appealed for the public to avoid speculation.
