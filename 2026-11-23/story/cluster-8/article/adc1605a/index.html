I’m a Celeb’s AJ breaks down in tears after Mariella brands her ‘aggressive’ as cooking war reaches boiling point

Article text

I’M A CELEB’S AJ has broken down in tears after Mariella branded her ‘aggressive’ as the camp’s cooking war reached boiling point Mariella has been at the centre of a number of rows already during the show’s first week – for her “bullying ways”. Earlier in the week the I’m A Celeb contestant left AJ Odudu in tears after yet another spat about cooking. Tonight, as the celebrities woke up, Joanna and Claire discussed the tension that had arisen between the pair. Claire then spoke to Wayne about her concerns and how they could try and detuse the situation. When Wayne spoke to AJ, she said: “I think basically my observation is the food arrives and immediately it causes her stress. You don’t even have to say anything, it just stresses her out.” Read More TV Claire then suggested to AJ : “Maybe you shoulddetusee it if it makes things get a bit tense.” “I’ll speak to her,” added Wayne adding the quicker it could be solved the better. When Mariella arrived Wayne explained that a few people had said that it felt very stressful at dinner. “It does get stressful,” agreed Mariella and added there needed to be more of a system and that it wasn’t a lovely collaborative afternoon in AJ’s and Mariella’s kitchen. Most read in TV “I just didn’t know the system, but now I do and I appreciate this conversation,” said AJ. Mariella then explained that it would have been less stressful if AJ had just sat down with her for two minutes and asked what worked best. AJ replied: “I feel like wrongly, incorrectly there’s an idea that I’ve come in saying that I desperately want to cook and that I’m going for it. I appreciate that I don’t know what’s going on.” Mariella interrupted and said: “AJ I think you’re making a drama out of a crisis, I was thrilled that there was one other person in the camp that was prepared to say they’d cook.” “You have a funny way of showing it, that’s all,” replied AJ. Mariella replied: “Well you see that’s just aggressive and that’s what keeps happening. “Please don’t use those words towards me, dramatic and aggressive are very harsh words, ” said AJ. Later, Mariella spoke to Joanna and Claire about AJ being aggressive towards her but her co-stars didn’t agree with that description. AJ broke down in tears and said she knew she had not been aggressive and that it was an insane way to describe her as she was comforted by Sharky. “You’re not aggressive, ” said Sharky before adding “You’re a lot of things, a lot of good things, but defo not aggressive, don’t worry about that.” Later, in the Bush Telegraph, an emotional AJ admitted she was very upset and explained: “Being called aggressive especially someone like me is just a triggering word.” A teary AJ concluded: “I think I’m just shocked, sometimes in life you’re just not everyone’s cup of tea and that’s sad.” This latest row comes days after Mariella drove Gavin & Stacey star Joanna Page to tears during another clash over dinner.
