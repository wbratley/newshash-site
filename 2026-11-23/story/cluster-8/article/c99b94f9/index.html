I'm A Celebrity's cookery tension heats up: Mariella Frostrup leaves AJ Odudu in tears as she calls her 'dramatic and aggressive' after Wayne Rooney attempted to diffuse row

Article text

Cooking in the I'm A Celebrity camp has once again become a major source of tension among the celebrities, with simmering frustrations threatening to boil over. And there's no sign of the drama dying down as Sunday's episode saw tensions escalate further after Mariella Frostrup called AJ Odudu 'dramatic and aggressive' during an awkward brawl in the jungle. Mariella, 64, took charge of cooking on the very first day after no other campmate volunteered for the role. However, her efforts have since sparked a series of clashes with Gavin & Stacey star Joanna Page, 49, and TV presenter AJ, 38. Now, Wayne Rooney has stepped in as camp leader to play peacemaker. Claire Sweeney told her fellow scouser that she had woken up feeling that he needed to 'diffuse' the situation because it had 'got a lot'. Wayne then pulled Mariella to the side when she was brushing her teeth and said: 'Mariella, I was just saying, at dinner, a few have said it - it feels like it gets really stressful. Is there any way we could relax a little bit?' It's not a kind of lovely, collaborative afternoon in AJ or Mariella's kitchen.' On the path, the other campmates tried to listen into the conversation between AJ, Wayne, Mariella and Claire as Leomie whispered: 'Guys, guys it's all kicking off..' AJ got off to a rocky start after serving burnt beans to the camp, prompting Mariella to remark: 'We've gone from the happiest camp in the world to one of stress and tension.' An attempt to resolve their differences later ended with the Big Brother host in tears. AJ said: 'I feel like there's an idea that I have come in saying I want to cook and that I'm going for it.' Mariella said: 'AJ, I think you are making a drama out of a crisis, I was thrilled someone was able to step in and cook.' 'You have a funny way of showing it,' AJ retorted before Mariella swiped: 'Now that's just aggressive and dramatic.' AJ hit back: 'Please don't use them words in front of me.' Breaking down in tears into Sharky's arms, AJ said: 'It's too early man, I don't understand. I think Wayne and Claire are coming from a good place and trying to diffuse tension. I know I'm not aggressive.' Speaking in the Bush Telegraph, AJ added: 'I'm just flabbergasted and just really upset. 'Being called aggressive, especially someone like me, it's just such a triggering word and I think I'm just in shock. Sometimes in life you are just not everyone's cup of tea and that's sad.' The previous night, Mariella's decision to issue instructions around the campfire in her capacity as chef had also riled Joanna, who stormed off to seek comfort from Kaiser Chiefs frontman Ricky Wilson, 48. 'Oh my God… this is doing my head in. I cannot listen to Mariella's voice anymore,' the actress declared, as Ricky put an arm around her and told her to 'stay strong'. Mariella told the Daily Mail before heading into the Australian jungle that her children had warned her against getting drawn into arguments with her fellow campmates, urging her to let things go. The broadcaster, who shares daughter Molly, 22, and son Dan, 21, with human rights lawyer husband Jason McCue, admitted she wanted to 'come out with my credibility intact'. Elsewhere, her close friend Belles Berry has since told The Mail on Sunday she fears Mariella is being pushed to breaking point. 'It is really tough on her, and it was really sad to watch her so upset because she is just trying to do the job… I hope it calms down and I hope the other contestants will not all join forces against her, because she is doing it for the good of everyone.' While the cooking drama has been a main talking point of the series so far, it doesn't appear to be enough to keep viewers watching, as the show has suffered its 'lowest ratings ever'. It averaged just 3.5million on Saturday – the first time the programme has ever fallen below 4million.
