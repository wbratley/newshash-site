Tear-stricken Celine Dion halts Paris comeback as crowd roar her to continue The Mirror was live inside Plenitude Arena to witness Céline Dion's incredible return to the stage as she put on a show to remember in her first concert since her Stiff Person's Syndrome diagnosis After six years away, Céline Dion stepped onto a concert stage again - and she couldn't hide her emotion. The 58-year-old icon burst into tears the second she stepped on stage. The Mirror was inside Paris's Plenitude Arena to witness the superstar's extraordinary comeback - with surprise covers, countless outfit changes and a setlist bursting with the best the Canadian superstar has to offer. It all came on a night that once seemed impossible following her devastating Stiff Person Syndrome diagnosis. Céline has faced years of uncertainty, gruelling rehabilitation, painful spasms and fears that she might never perform again. But tonight, all that hard work faced its ultimate test as she performed live in front of 40,000 people - and she put on a show to remember. Fans can expect classics including My Heart Will Go On, Think Twice and It’s All Coming Back To Me Now. She will likely perform her most popular songs in both French and English, with fans from around the world jetting in to Paris to see her in action. The five-time Grammy winner cancelled her Las Vegas residency due to health concerns in 2021, before revealing she had been diagnosed with Stiff Persons Syndrome in December 2022 and cancelling her Courage world tour. The autoimmune disease is a rare neurological disorder that causes progressive muscular stiffness and spasms, and there is currently no cure. Sufferers can feel locked inside their own bodies and can struggle with simple tasks like walking or swallowing. After revealing her diagnosis, Dion took a break from performing until 2024, when she made a brief public appearance to sing at the Paris Olympics opening ceremony. The singer repeatedly expressed her desire to return to the stage.
