Remembrance Sunday services held across UK with record number of former PMs at Cenotaph

Unbiased summary

On Remembrance Sunday, services took place across the UK to honour those who died in conflict. At the Cenotaph in London, King Charles laid the first wreath, followed by Prince William and other royals. Prime Minister Andy Burnham attended his first Remembrance service, joined by a record nine former prime ministers. Political leaders including Kemi Badenoch, Ed Davey, and Nigel Farage also laid wreaths. Approximately 10,000 veterans marched past the monument. Peace campaigners held alternative ceremonies with white poppies, and Prince Harry attended a separate service in Brackley. A two-minute silence was observed nationwide.
