Michael Carrick future LIVE: Man Utd sack plan as left-field option joins four-man shortlist Manchester United go into the second international break of the season in the Premier League relegation zone Manchester United boss Michael Carrick's future is still up in the air after an enthralling 4-4 draw with Aston Villa meant they'd stay in the Premier League relegation zone over the international break. The Red Devils started awfully and found themselves 2-0 down within four minutes thanks to goals from Alysson and Nicolas Jackson at Old Trafford. United did pull themselves back before half-time, with Bruno Fernandes scoring from the spot and Youri Tielemans making it 2-2. But history repeated itself at the start of the second half. A John McGinn penalty and a Leon Goretzka goal restored Villa's two-goal lead before Joshua Zirkzee and Patrick Dorgu scored in stoppage time to secure an unlikely point. Whilst there was some relief around Old Trafford, the result still leaves United in the bottom three and Carrick fighting for his job. The United board reportedly have not agreed on an alternative plan yet, though Eddie Howe, Julian Nagelsmann, Antonio Conte, and Stuttgart boss Sebastian Hoeness have all been mentioned as possible targets. Gary Neville issues verdict Gary Neville has hinted that things won't change at Manchester United, even if Michael Carrick is sacked as manager. Speaking to NBC Sports, he said: "Michael Carrick did that last season - so you can paper over the cracks and get a new-manager bounce; when the new manager comes in, everybody's happy for a couple of months, and all of a sudden, six months later, it happens again. You can keep changing the manager and thinking that's going to be the answer. But this has been proven now over eight managers in 12 years, that's not the answer. Michael could leave, Michael might stay, we don't know, the ownership don't have a clue what to do."It's rinse and repeat. None of it has worked because if you recruit badly, if you've got poor performance department, poor recruitment department, you've not got leadership that is strong right through the spine of the football club you will fail.“Every organisation in the world will fail if you've not got an aligned leadership from top to bottom - your board and your ownership right the way through to the person who is stood on the pitch trying to play football. There are real issues within that football club, you've got people doing those jobs who have never done those jobs before in senior positions." Man United 'meeting' planned Manchester United will hold a meeting next week to discuss Michael Carrick's future, according to TEAMtalk. It is said that the meeting was already planned, even before today's 4-4 draw with Aston Villa, which leaves United in the relegation zone. It's said that Jason Wilcox will ultimately decide whether Carrick stays or goes. Eddie Howe, Julian Nagelsmann, Antonio Conte and Unai Emery are all named as possible replacement options. Because what we're seeing most weeks is chaotic defending, people not organised, people not putting their bodies on the line and then they get interviewed and the manager goes 'we need to tighten up.' Carrick in tense exchanges on Sky Sports Carrick was involved in a tense exchange with Sky Sports' Patrick Davison in the immediate aftermath of the draw vs Villa. I know you have said that you walking away was the best for the club, you’d probably do it." Carrick: "It’s not going to happen, no." Davison: "What do you expect to happen? Clearly there’s going to be conversation?" Carrick: "Is there?" Davison: "You would assume so really?" Carrick: "Would you?" Davison: "What do you think Michael?" Carrick: "You’re telling me, what do you think?" Davison: "I’m asking you, Michael. What do you think?" Carrick: "You know the situation, we haven’t won enough games. But I expect to move forward with things we’ve been working on, showing a lot of the good from today tighten up a few mistakes and win some football matches." If they wanted to remove him and make a change, I think they would’ve already done it by now and found an interim solution.
