Roy Keane calls out 'casual' Michael Carrick as he questions Man Utd's honesty in epic rant

Roy Keane calls out 'casual' Michael Carrick as he questions Man Utd's honesty in epic rant Former Manchester United captain Roy Keane slammed Michael Carrick and his players for their response to the chaotic 4-4 draw against Aston Villa, which left them in the relegation zone Roy Keane has accused Michael Carrick of being too "casual" and Manchester United's players of lying to themselves after being disgusted by their post-match interviews on Sunday. United drew 4-4 with Aston Villa at Old Trafford in a chaotic game which left Carrick's future in doubt going into the international break. The home side were shambolic in defence, with individual errors helping Villa, but they showed fight to come back from two goals down on two occasions. Goals from Joshua Zirkzee and Patrick Dorgu in injury time earned a point, but United are 18th in the table having gone eight Premier League matches without a win. Carrick gave a spiky post-match interview in which he said he expected to continue as manager, while Dorgu and Matheus Cunha insisted they could turn things around. Their words did not impress former United captain Keane, who responded with a trademark rant on Sky Sports.
