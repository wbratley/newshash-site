Manchester United draw 4-4 with Aston Villa, remain in relegation zone as Carrick's future is questioned

Unbiased summary

Manchester United drew 4-4 with Aston Villa at Old Trafford on Sunday, twice coming back from two-goal deficits. Villa led 2-0 inside four minutes through Alysson and Nicolas Jackson, but United equalised before half-time with a Bruno Fernandes penalty and a Youri Tielemans own goal. Early in the second half, John McGinn converted a penalty and Leon Goretzka scored to restore Villa's two-goal lead. Joshua Zirkzee and Patrick Dorgu scored in stoppage time to secure a point. The result leaves United 18th in the Premier League, in the relegation zone, with eight league games without a win. Manager Michael Carrick gave a spiky post-match interview about his future, and reports suggest the board is considering potential replacements including Eddie Howe and Julian Nagelsmann.
