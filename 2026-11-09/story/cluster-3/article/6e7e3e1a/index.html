Maya Jama QUITS Love Island after presenting hit ITV dating show for nine series as she says it is time to 'throw myself into something new'

Article text

The ultimate bombshell has left the villa! Maya Jama has announced that she has quit Love Island after an 'unforgettable' nine series as she shared a new statement on Instagram on Sunday. The presenter, 32, took over the hit ITV2 dating series in January 2023 from Laura Whitmore and has become one of the most in-demand stars. The shock announcement comes a week after she made it to the final of this year's Celebrity Traitors. She also recently made her acting debut in Guy Ritchie's hit Netflix series The Gentlemen. Maya said 'all good things must come to an end' and that she is 'grateful' for the experience but it is now time to 'throw myself into something new'. Alongside a montage of her best bits, she wrote: 'What an incredible era and what an unforgettable time I’ve had, it’s been far too much fun to put into words or a video..but as all good things must come to an end, it’s my time to step down as the host of my beloved Love Island.
