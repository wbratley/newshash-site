UK Home Secretary Shabana Mahmood announces revised immigration rules, doubling wait for many to settle permanently

Unbiased summary

Home Secretary Shabana Mahmood announced changes to Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR) rules, doubling the standard wait for many work visa holders to 10 years, while exempting key workers (doctors, nurses, teachers) and higher earners. Those earning over £50,000 can settle in five years; over £125,000 in three. Care workers already in the UK before May 2025 will still qualify after five years, a concession from earlier proposals that would have made them wait 15 years. The changes, to be introduced in 2027, aim to link settlement to contribution. Small boat migrants may face waits of up to 30 years under separate rules. The government estimates 1.9 million will become eligible for ILR by 2030, with about 500,000 waiting more than five years.
