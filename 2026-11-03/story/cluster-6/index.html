John Lewis Releases 2026 Christmas Ad Starring Kristin Scott Thomas and Toy Dog Pickles

Unbiased summary

John Lewis has released its 20th annual Christmas advert, titled 'Gloria and Pickles,' starring Kristin Scott Thomas as a grandmother who, with a toy dog named Pickles voiced by Paul Whitehouse, searches for the perfect gifts for her grandchildren. The ad features a Sam Fender cover of The Rolling Stones' 'You Can't Always Get What You Want' and promotes the tagline 'don't give up on giving.' John Lewis marketing director Rosie Hanley stated the retailer increased its festive ad spend due to rising production costs and to mark the 20th year, expressing confidence in a strong return on investment. The ad aims to be more joyful amid tough economic conditions for families, and Hanley noted Christmas-related spending at John Lewis is already up on last year.
