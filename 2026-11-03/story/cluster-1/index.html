Nine killed in Florida Learjet crash during thunderstorm; NTSB investigates weather and possible breakup

Unbiased summary

A Learjet 60 private jet crashed in Palm Beach County, Florida, on Sunday, killing all nine people on board. The aircraft, owned by Upper Jets, was flying from Austin, Texas, to the Bahamas when it encountered a severe thunderstorm. The crew reported turbulence and ice and requested to climb to 37,000 feet. Shortly after, the plane issued a mayday call and descended rapidly before crashing near a mobile home park in Greenacres, exploding on impact. The victims included tech entrepreneur Adam Robinson, his wife Helen Sharp, their two young children, a nanny, two family friends, and the two pilots. The National Transportation Safety Board (NTSB) is investigating whether the plane broke apart in flight and the role of weather conditions.
