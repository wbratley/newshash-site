BP Exits North Sea After 60 Years

Unbiased summary

BP has announced plans to sell its North Sea operations, ending six decades of production in the region. The company currently employs 1,100 workers and operates five production hubs, producing 117,000 barrels of oil equivalent per day. Energy Secretary Miatta Fahnbulleh has prioritized protecting workers and the local community during the sale process. BP's decision follows a period of speculation about the company's future in the North Sea, with various factors contributing to the decision.
