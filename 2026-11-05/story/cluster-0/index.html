British healthcare worker with Ebola evacuated from DR Congo to UK

Unbiased summary

A British healthcare worker in the Democratic Republic of the Congo has tested positive for Ebola and is being evacuated to the UK for treatment. The UK government confirmed the case and stated that well-established procedures have been activated, with the risk to the UK public remaining low. The patient, whose identity is unknown, is in a stable condition and is expected to arrive in the UK on Wednesday or Thursday before being transferred to a high-consequence infectious disease unit. The worker was sent to DR Congo by a humanitarian organisation to help respond to the outbreak, which is the second largest on record, with over 10,000 cases and 5,000 deaths. This is the first case involving a British national in the current outbreak, which is caused by the Bundibugyo virus and has no approved treatment, though vaccine trials are underway. The Royal Free hospital in London has a high-level isolation unit, with other centres in Newcastle and Liverpool.
