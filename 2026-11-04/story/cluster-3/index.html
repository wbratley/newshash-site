Cyber-attacks target supermarket refrigeration across Europe, UK confirms limited impact

Unbiased summary

Supermarket refrigeration systems in several European countries malfunctioned after suspected cyber-attacks, with thermostats remotely manipulated to raise temperatures. Affected retailers included Carrefour, Intermarché, and Ikea in Belgium, Coop and ICA in Sweden, Delhaize in Luxembourg, Kesko in Finland, SuperValu in Ireland, and shops in Estonia. The EU condemned the incidents as deliberate attempts to disrupt the food supply chain. In the UK, the National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) reported a small number of impacts on the food supply chain, including some meat suppliers whose fridge systems heated up overnight, though no major supermarket chain was directly affected and food loss was minimal. No group has claimed responsibility, and investigations are ongoing into the motive and perpetrators.
