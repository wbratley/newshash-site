David Walliams accused of sexual misconduct by multiple women after losing High Court injunction bid

Unbiased summary

David Walliams, a British comedian and children's author, has been accused by multiple women of using his celebrity status to engage in inappropriate and exploitative sexual activities with young women and teenagers, including two who were 15 when they first met him. The allegations were published by The Guardian and ITV News after Walliams failed to secure a High Court injunction to block publication. Walliams denies all allegations, stating he is not a sexual predator and has not broken the law, though he acknowledges behaving 'thoughtlessly' in some past casual sexual relationships. The court heard Walliams had made two suicide attempts after the allegations were put to him and was sectioned. The judge ruled that protecting Walliams' life did not require suppressing publication. Separately, Russell Brand was jailed for 10.5 years for rape and sexual assault, and police have received further allegations against him.
