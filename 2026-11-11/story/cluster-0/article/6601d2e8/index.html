He sought a High Court injunction to prevent publication of the claims, arguing he would take his own life if the allegations became public. Walliams, who last year questioned his sexuality, has largely retreated from public life and romances in recent months. The comedian has only publicly ever had relationships with women but claimed that he is non-binary following his divorce from Lara Stone. Since his split with Lara, Walliams has been romantically linked to a string of high-profile celebrities including Ashley Roberts and Ashley James. Now the Daily Mail reveals the other stars he has romanced over the years.
