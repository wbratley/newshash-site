The actress, 53, shared her diagnosis in an Instagram post on Tuesday, and revealed the cancer has now spread to her 'bones'. In her post, Lucy shared that she first discovered a 'tiny, hard spot' in her breast a year ago and nearly ignored it before seeing a doctor. The Wonder Woman star said that despite the cancer being declared incurable she's 'at peace with whatever comes next'. Lucy shared her diagnosis alongside an emotional video that showed her ringing the cancer treatment bell, and she shared in her post that she's been told it's 'too late' for her to undergo chemotherapy. The star is best known for her role as Dawn Tinsley in Ricky Gervais' comedy The Office, and went on to star in the DC film Wonder Woman.
