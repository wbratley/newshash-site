On Monday night, the 55-year-old, who plays Dr. Michael 'Robby' Robinavitch in the HBO Max hit, took home Outstanding Lead Actor in a Drama Series for the second consecutive year. He beat out the likes of Gary Oldman (Slow Horses), Sterling K. Brown (Paradise), Mark Ruffalo (Task) and Rufus Sewell (The Diplomat). Wyle appeared totally stunned as he slowly rose to his feet to kiss his longtime wife Sara Wells after presenter John Mulaney called out his name as the winner. Seated directly behind the couple was Margulies, who could be seen beaming with pride as she gave her former co-star a standing ovation.
