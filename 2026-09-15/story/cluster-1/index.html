NATO jets shoot down drone that entered Lithuanian airspace from Belarus

Unbiased summary

Early on Tuesday, a drone entered Lithuanian airspace from Belarus and was shot down by NATO fighter jets near Pratkunai, south of Kaunas. Emergency alerts were issued to residents in Vilnius, Trakai, and Elektrėnai, urging them to seek shelter. Lithuanian President Gitanas Nausėda confirmed the destruction of the drone, thanking allied personnel for their swift response. The exact origin of the drone was not immediately determined, though Lithuanian authorities indicated it had come from Belarus. This is the first time a drone has been shot down in Lithuanian territory. Separately, Poland activated its air force as a precautionary measure amid Russian drone strikes on Ukraine, and had recently recovered a suspected Russian drone from the Baltic Sea.
