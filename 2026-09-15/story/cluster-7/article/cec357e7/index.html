Don't Miss Most Read Latest Richard Tice has accused Labour of “rigging the system” against Reform UK as he hit back at plans to cap donations to political parties. Speaking to GB News, the Reform UK deputy leader blasted the Government for “hyperventilating hypocrisy” in the row over multimillion-pound gifts to Nigel Farage’s party. In 24 hours, cryptocurrency tycoons Ben Delo and Christopher Harborne contributed a combined £72million to Reform. Labour’s Angela Rayner has announced that a “task and finish” group has been established in response to the donations, which will determine whether further legislation is required to place limits on such contributions. The Housing Secretary said the group would also consider ways to stop individuals from “overpower and buy their way into democracy” through financial donations. Ms Rayner has suggested any new rules could be applied retroactively, forcing Reform to return the two £36million gifts. Mr Tice insisted Reform was “very confident” the donations were legal and compliant with the rules currently in force and legislation being considered by Parliament. He also accused Labour of “hyperventilating hypocrisy” over the issue, pointing to the tens of millions of pounds the party receives from trade unions. He questioned whether Labour was being held to the same standard over demands made by its union backers. “What about the demands that unions make on the Labour Party that get met every single year?” he asked. Mr Tice claimed union demands for increased employment rights and higher public sector pay were routinely reflected in Government policy. Mr Tice predicted Britons should be “delighted that a level playing field is being created by successful entrepreneurs”.
