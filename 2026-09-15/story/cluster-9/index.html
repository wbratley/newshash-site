VAR officials stood down after Manchester derby error over Haaland goal

Unbiased summary

Manchester City beat Manchester United 2-1 at Old Trafford on Sunday, with Erling Haaland scoring the winning goal in the 60th minute. The goal was initially disallowed for offside but awarded after VAR review, as Haaland was ruled onside by United defender Patrick Dorgu's boot. However, Pro Ref later acknowledged an 'error of judgement' because City midfielder Enzo Fernandez, in an offside position, attempted to play the ball and should have triggered an on-field review. The VAR officials, Matt Donohue and Blake Antrobus, have been stood down from this weekend's Premier League fixtures. The decision was made by Howard Webb, chief refereeing officer. Pro Ref has contacted Manchester United to explain the mistake. City played with 10 men after Phil Foden's red card but maintained their perfect start to the season.
