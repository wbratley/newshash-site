Mr Polanski, 43, said he made an 'inadvertent mistake' but will not apologise for the post, which he shared to his 713,000 followers on Instagram on Monday. The image featured a man wearing a black T-shirt with the message, 'We're only making plans for Nigel' and an image of a guillotine. Mr Farage called for his political opponent to be arrested on Monday, while Reform's home affairs spokesman Zia Yusuf accused the Green leader of 'inciting murder'. In his first interview since the scandal erupted, Mr Polanski told BBC Newsnight that the police had not been in contact with him, adding he 'didn't pay enough attention to the T-shirt' and was 'horrified' by its 'dangerous message'. The Green Party leader said during the programme on Tuesday night: 'This was not my post, it was a collaboration from a photographer.
