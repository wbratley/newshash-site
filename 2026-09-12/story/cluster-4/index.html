UK air traffic control outage caused by military aircraft data, report says

Unbiased summary

A technical failure at National Air Traffic Services (Nats) on Tuesday grounded thousands of flights and affected hundreds of thousands of passengers. According to sources cited by the Financial Times, the outage was triggered by a flight plan from a UK military aircraft that introduced 'spurious' data into Nats' flight processing system. The system shut down for four hours, leading to over 2,000 cancellations. Nats operations director Kathryn Leahy reportedly informed airlines that the system lacked a dedicated backup due to an ongoing overhaul. Transport Secretary Heidi Alexander summoned Nats CEO Martin Rolfe and demanded a report within a week, while the Civil Aviation Authority launched an independent review. Nats had experienced a similar outage in 2023, with recommendations said to have been implemented.
