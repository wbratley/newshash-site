Houthi forces seize Red Sea island and port city amid Yemen displacement

Unbiased summary

Yemen's Houthi forces have captured Perim Island and the port city of Mokha along the Red Sea coast, tightening their control over the Bab al-Mandab Strait. The Houthis claimed the operation was in response to Saudi aggression and said shipping was safe except for Saudi vessels.
