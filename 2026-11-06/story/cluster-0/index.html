Russell Brand convicted of rape and three sexual assaults, faces lengthy prison sentence

Unbiased summary

On Thursday, a jury at Southwark Crown Court found Russell Brand, 51, guilty of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault against four women. The offences occurred between 2004 and 2009. He was acquitted of two counts of rape and one count of attempted indecent assault. The judge, Mr Justice Bennathan, remanded Brand in custody and told him he faces a 'significant custodial term' when sentenced on Monday. Following the verdict, police revealed Brand is under investigation for two further rape allegations, including one involving a 16-year-old, and urged other potential victims to come forward. Brand had denied all charges, claiming his encounters were consensual and that allegations were 'reimagined' following media reports in 2023.
