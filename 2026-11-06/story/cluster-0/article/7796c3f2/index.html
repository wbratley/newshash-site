Article text

THE BBC has been accused of enabling Russell Brand with the disgraced star claiming his Beeb boss was “incredibly tolerant” of his behaviour. Brand, 51 – due to be sentenced on Monday – is being monitored around the clock by guards after he was found guilty on Thursday. Brand was facing trial for three counts of rape, one count of attempted indecent assault and three counts of sexual assault. Following eight and a half hours of deliberation the jury delivered guilty verdicts on three counts of sexual assault and one count of rape, bringing the four-week trial at Southwark Crown Court to a close. He was refused bail and was sent down to the cells with two female dock officers after the verdict was handed down. The jury found Brand guilty of groping an MTV worker during a party at a Soho bar in 2004, but not guilty of going on to rape her in a toilet. Brand was also convicted by the jury of sexually assaulting a Channel 4 worker in 2009 when she had been forced to give him a massage. The convictions piled further pressure on the BBC after it was revealed Brand had boasted his boss at the Beeb was “incredibly tolerant” of his sexual behaviour.
