Seven for spectacular England in Nations League thrashing of Croatia

Article text

England picked up steam in their Nations League campaign with a 7-0 thrashing of Croatia in Rijeka on Saturday. The two familiar foes went head-to-head for the first time since a six-goal thriller in the opening round of this summer’s World Cup, and there was one more goal than that on this occasion – but all of them came from the Three Lions in a scintillating display. In front of a crowd comprising thousands of children, returning Croatian boss Slaven Bilić watched on in horror as his side were schooled by their dominant visitors in a bout that looked like it was pitting boys against men from the very beginning. Harry Kane opened the scoring and added another in the second half, taking his personal England tally to a record-extending 89 goals, with Bukayo Saka similarly on the score sheet twice. That duo’s efforts were interspersed with goals from the brilliant Jude Bellingham – who also finished with three assists – and electric Anthony Gordon before substitute Morgan Rogers put the icing on the cake late on to send Thomas Tuchel’s men into Tuesday’s meeting with the Czech Republic at Wembley brimming with confidence.
