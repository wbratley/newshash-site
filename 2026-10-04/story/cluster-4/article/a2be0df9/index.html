Weekly horoscope for October 4 – 10: Maggie Hyde reveals what the stars have in store for every zodiac sign

Article text

THE Sun’s world famous Mystic Meg horoscope column has entertained readers for decades with predictions for their love lives, money and careers. Read on to see what Mystic Meg with Maggie Hyde has written in the stars for you today. ARIES MAR 21 – APR 20 Something, or someone, was particularly heavy going over the weekend so you’ll welcome being able to put this behind you. The Monday morning blues won’t last long. You’re known for your ability to move on fast and that’s exactly what’s needed. READ MORE ON FABULOUS You’re a fabulous role model for your kids when you’re in this mood and their achievements this week are something to celebrate. TAURUS APR 21 – MAY 21 Taking life simply as it comes is one of your strengths and will work for you, up to a point. As earthy as you are, you still have excellent instincts and now’s the time to follow them. Most read in Fabulous That sneaking feeling that you should be digging below the surface is correct. In your key relationships think about deepening your connection and invest the time and effort to make this happen. GEMINI MAY 22 – JUNE 21 When the going gets tough, the tough don’t get negative. When you’re a Gemini that especially means getting inventive, and showing everyone else how problem solving really works. You also need variety in your life so take a good look at your routine. There could be obvious ways to freshen things up, such as ditching a chore you wish you’d never agreed to in the first place. CANCER JUNE 22 – JULY 22 Finding the balance between family demands and doing the things that matter to you personally is not new to you. This week’s events put this kind of conflict firmly in the spotlight and you can expect more of a juggling act than usual. Don’t let anyone press your guilt buttons. You need to make changes if you’re not following your passion or living a life that you love. LEO JULY 23 – AUG 23 Guard against turning a minor matter into a drama. Your independent streak is super strong right now but you also need the support of your nearest and dearest. Making too many demands in the heat of the moment could all too easily backfire and leave you wondering where everyone has gone! The Sun, your ruling planet, is reminding you how to make others feel special. VIRGO AUG 24 – SEPT 22 Are you over complicating things? Mercury has you thinking about organizing your life in a way that puts you more in control. This includes relying a lot less, or not at all, on the people who mess you around far too often. Finding the right help changes everything. You can then step back a bit from being so busy and give yourself the space and permission to relax more, or even daydream. LIBRA SEPT 23 – OCT 23 As the sign of the scales you know all about balance and indecision, the constant weighing up of the two sides to everything. As you travel towards the new moon in your sign at the weekend this balancing act is likely to be stronger than ever. Get ready to make the important decisions, ready or not! It’s also a fabulous time for updating your look and your wardrobe.   SCORPIO OCT 24 – NOV 22 Is there something that somehow belongs to an old version of you? What negative thing have you got so used to that you hardly notice it anymore? These are the kind of things that are starting to make noises. Wake up to the power of YOU, either for making the right changes in your own life, or for helping someone else who doesn’t have your kind of strength. SAGITTARIUS NOV 23 – DEC 21 A new cycle now gathering pace is going to be a magical mystery tour and you’re not sure what’s going on. Truth and openness come naturally to you, so it’s not easy to be up against the opposite to that. Accept that someone will have their own reasons for secrecy. It’s time to make health appointments you’ve been putting off. The right treatment or peace of mind, either way you win. CAPRICORN DEC 22 – JAN 20 You’re always busy, but what can you ditch in order to make life less overwhelming? It’s out with any situation that makes you tired or uncomfortable, such as running around for someone who’s perfectly capable of being more responsible for themselves. Tuesday brings confidences or you can share a secret of your own, but only with someone who’d never repeat it. AQUARIUS JAN 21 – FEB 18 You rather like your cool reputation, but how often do you secretly long for more understanding? You’re right in the heart of a big turning point that’s been building up for a long time, which is all about less of the brave face and more of the real you. You’re only human. Clear out your physical clutter too, especially that room or dark corner that’s become a dumping ground. PISCES FEB 19 – MAR 20 If anyone can soak up an atmosphere or tune in to other people without even trying, that’s you. That’s great when the vibes are positive, but not so great when they’re negative or fearful. Protect yourself from those who drain your energy, and see more of those who nourish your spirit.
