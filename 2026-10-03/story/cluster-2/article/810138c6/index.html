Celebrity Traitors' next murder victim 'confirmed' as Richard E Grant's exit 'sealed' Celebrity Traitors is back on the BBC for its second series after a successful run last year, and it's already seen the biggest faux pas of any of the series ever... Celebrity Traitors is back - and Richard E. Grant might have pulled off the biggest act of self-sabotage the show has ever seen, before ruining it again tonight with another faux pas. The veteran actor and presenter Maya Jama were picked as the first two Traitors with the episode ending on a huge cliffhanger as fans wait to see who they have recruited or murdered. It ended up being James recruited, and Amol murdered. As the show got underway tonight, Richard E Grant made another mistake - and refused to deny he was a Traitor once more, whilst in the mission, Hannah and Jerry found out they'd be up for the next murder. In a dramatic cliffhanger, the celebs were about to vote for their first banishment, with all eyes on Richard... Like this story? For more of the latest showbiz news and gossip, follow Mirror Celebs on TikTok , Snapchat , Instagram , Twitter , Facebook , YouTube and Threads . Key Events Amol Rajan admits he played ‘the wrong game’ Journalist Amol Rajan has admitted he played “the wrong game” after he became the first player to be murdered. Speaking on The Celebrity Traitors: Uncloaked on Friday, Rajan said: “It’s hard not to watch it and think I, obviously, with 20/20 hindsight, I played the wrong game. That was a crazy, crazy once-in-a-lifetime (opportunity).” The former Today Show host said that he felt that if he had been recruited, he would have made a good traitor. He said: “Obviously, I wish I’d been chosen, and I wish I’d been a traitor. I can say, hand on heart, that if I’d been chosen as a traitor in the first, when Maya and Richard were, if I got the nod or the tap at the roundtable, I think it would have thrown me a bit. “I’d be like, ‘what am I doing? what’s going on?’, having genuinely spent a day as a faithful, if I’d then been recruited, I think I would have been quite a good traitor because I’d actually known what it was like to be a faithful. Tell us your thoughts... A third wrote: "Loving it as always but I always tune off a bit during the tasks. Really didn't understand the grave digging thing." Maya's smart move Did you notice this about Maya's roundtable remarks about Richard? She said: “Richard, obviously we all love you. I’ve just been thinking he’s a bit wacky, he’s a bit woo-woo... You’ve been dancing around the place saying loads of crazy things…” The Love Islander presenter stopped short of giving her fellow Traitor a full throttled defence but also didn't throw him under the bus either. As such, she's played a blinder - if Richard survives, he won't feel betrayed by her, and if he doesn't, then she can claim she suspected him. Well played, Maya. Well played. Truth behind Celebrity Traitors' Jerry Hall's bonkers Bonnie and Clyde admission Jerry Hall insists being the perfect Faithful is in her blood as she joins the cast of The Traitors - but just how accurate are her claims? The fabulous Texan supermodel tells the cast of the BBC show that her uncle was responsible for capturing infamous American outlaws Bonnie and Clyde. The couple roamed the United States with their gang during the Great Depression between 1932 and 1934 indulging in a spree of criminal acts kidnapping, bank robberies and even murder. They were eventually ambushed and killed by a law enforcement posse, led by retired Texas Ranger Frank Hamer. "My relations were all law enforcement officers," Jerry says. "My uncle was a Texan Ranger - he caught Bonnie and Clyde. So beneath this dumb blonde is a smart brunette."
