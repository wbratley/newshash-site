Reform UK conference overshadowed by undercover donations sting, aides suspended

Unbiased summary

Reform UK's party conference in Birmingham was overshadowed by an undercover investigation by Channel 4 and Verbatim Investigations. The footage showed senior aides Dan Jukes and James Orr appearing to discuss bypassing electoral laws on foreign donations, including a proposed £500,000 gift from a US donor routed through a UK-based son. Both aides stepped down pending an internal investigation. Nigel Farage, the party leader, claimed he was not listening during the recorded meeting and said his family had urged him to quit, but he insisted he would continue. Police are considering complaints from Labour and the Lib Dems. The party denies any illegal donations were accepted. The conference also included protests by unions and a separate incident where a protester was removed from the venue.
