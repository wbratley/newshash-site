Masked protesters block Dover port roads, causing travel disruption

Unbiased summary

On Saturday morning, a large group of people wearing black clothing and balaclavas blocked roads leading to the Port of Dover in Kent, halting traffic in both directions on the A20 and causing delays of up to 30 minutes and four miles of congestion. The protest, which involved chanting such as 'stop the boats' and 'England till I die', began early and dispersed by around midday. Kent Police attended but made no arrests. The Port of Dover described it as a public order incident. The government condemned the disruption. The group Patriot Platform, linked to far-right activist Tommy Robinson, claimed responsibility. Local MP Mike Tapp criticized the protesters, and Reform UK leader Nigel Farage also condemned the use of masks. No violence was reported.
