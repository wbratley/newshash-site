Don't Miss Most Read Latest Marco Rubio has blamed the incident at RAF Fairford on a "foreign actor", and has warned that "a lot more news" about the circumstances will come to light in the near future. Speaking on Monday night, the US Secretary of State described the alleged terror plot as "very serious". Five men - all from London and aged in their 20s - were arrested on suspicion of terrorism and explosives offences after three vans were found close to the Gloucestershire base used by US forces. Mr Rubio told Fox News it "clearly involves the hands of a foreign actor", but stopped short of naming a specific country, saying: "I won't get into great detail about that yet." The senior Trump administration official also piled praise on the British authorities, which he described as "very cooperative". However, he said: "I know a lot of people are disturbed by the news that some of these people have been released on bail... Obviously we're engaging with them on all of that. "But suffice it to say that beyond that, I would argue that they've been very cooperative and very helpful on something that's very serious incident, and I think it ties us to the reality here." Mr Rubio acknowledged that the US was "dealing with elements in the world", adding: "In the case of Iran, for example, who have openly threatened to attack American interests globally, we're going to always take that very seriously. The Secretary of State confirmed that there will be "a lot more news that will come out" about the incident in the coming days, but acknowledged "there's only so much I can say right now at this point about it, other than this is a very serious thing". Earlier on Monday, Donald Trump admitted he was "surprised" that the suspects linked to the incident were released on bail by police. Speaking to reporters in the Oval Office, the US President said he "wouldn't have done that". "Don't let them out on bail... Is it cashless bail like us?" the US Commander-in-Chief asked.
