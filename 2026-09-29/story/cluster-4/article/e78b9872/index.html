Log in Andy Burnham is set to unveil how the UK will pay for his social care reform plans after he said he was prepared to hike taxes. The prime minister pledged to fight the next election on creating an NHS-style social care system, funded by the taxpayer and free at the point of use. He told LBC on Monday that he would “reveal details” about the funding during his leadership speech on Tuesday after saying nothing was off the table to pay for the reforms. “I will put forward my ideas. If the country says 'go faster because there's a big consensus behind it', that would be one thing,” he said. “We'll have to debate this. We'll have to get Baroness Casey to set out the details of how it can be done.” The prime minister said it would “take time” to get the details right, but insisted he would “lay out a very clear path for the country” on Tuesday. According to the Health Foundation think tank, the care service could cost up to £18.5 billion a year in 2035/6.
