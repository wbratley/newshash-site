Russell Brand sentenced to 10 years and 6 months for rape and sexual assault

Unbiased summary

On Monday, Russell Brand, 51, was sentenced at Southwark Crown Court to 10 years and 6 months in prison for one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault against four women between 2004 and 2009. He was convicted last week following a four-week trial. The judge, Mr Justice Bennathan, called Brand a 'sexual predator' with 'staggering arrogance' and said his celebrity status had protected him until a 2023 Channel 4 Dispatches documentary and newspaper articles empowered women to come forward. Brand was cleared of two further rape charges and one attempted indecent assault. He will serve at least two-thirds of his sentence and is on the sex offenders' register for life. Police are investigating two further rape allegations. His ex-girlfriend Jemima Goldsmith alleged violence and coercive control, and his father publicly disputed the verdict.
