Prince William calls for improved treatment of women in UK armed forces

Unbiased summary

Prince William, the Prince of Wales and future head of the Armed Forces, has publicly disclosed that he has been conducting private meetings with servicewomen across the Army, Royal Navy, and RAF over the past year. He heard accounts of inappropriate comments, malicious rumours, and intoxicated harassment in barracks. In an article he commissioned for Country Life magazine, where he is guest editor, William stated that military culture must be more inclusive of women and that current standards are not being met. He has raised these concerns with senior defence leaders and is considering a longer-term initiative. The article also references a Ministry of Defence survey and the case of Gunner Jaysley Beck.
