Jon Venables, convicted of murdering James Bulger in 1993, has been denied parole and will remain in prison

Unbiased summary

Jon Venables, now 44, has been refused release by the Parole Board. Venables was 10 years old when he and Robert Thompson abducted, tortured, and murdered two-year-old James Bulger in 1993. He was released on licence in 2001 but recalled to prison in 2010 and again in 2017 for possessing indecent images of children. In his latest parole review, held in private, Venables stated he was not seeking release and accepted the need for further offending work in custody. The panel considered evidence from probation officers, three psychologists, and a psychiatrist, and was not satisfied that he was suitable for release, citing concerns about how open and honest he would be in supervision. James's mother, Denise Fergus, and father, Ralph Bulger, expressed relief at the decision.
