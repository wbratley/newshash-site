I can breathe easier now, says murdered James Bulger’s father after killer Jon Venables’ freedom bid REFUSED

Article text

THE father of murdered toddler James Bulger last night told of his “huge relief” after one of his son’s killers was refused parole. Ralph Bulger said he could breathe easier after Jon Venables, 44, lost his freedom bid following a three-day hearing last month. Ralph, 60, and ex-wife Denise Fergus, 59, were blocked from attending the private review. Builder Ralph said: “I’m hugely relieved the Parole Board has seen sense and kept this vile child killer behind bars. “I can breathe easier now I know he will not be free to harm other children. “But the trauma never ends. In another two years, he will be eligible for parole again.
