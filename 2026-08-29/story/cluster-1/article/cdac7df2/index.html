Man dead and at least six injured at Christian festival as event cancelled following 'serious incident' The festival, a non-profit gathering that has run since 2009, draws up to 40,000 attendees Don't Miss Most Read A 41-year-old man has died and at least six others are injured after a large metal support beam collapsed onto a crowd at the Big Church Festival on the Wiston Estate in West Sussex this afternoon. The incident occurred at approximately 3.30pm, prompting emergency services to rush to the scene. Ambulance crews and police attended to the injured at the annual Christian music and worship event, which is being held at Wilton Park near Steyning from August 28 to August 30. At least six people were taken to hospital for treatment, with the 41-year-old man, from Eastbourne, died at the scene, Sussex Police has confirmed. The organisers said the event had been "paused" following a "serious incident", with no further programming taking place today. They said: "First and foremost, our thoughts and prayers are with those who have been directly impacted by the incident. "We understand this will be deeply disappointing for the thousands who have joined us at the festival." The festival, a non-profit gathering that has run since 2009 and drawn up to 40,000 attendees at its peak, has been placed on full lockdown, with all performances halted as paramedics and specialist response teams work to treat those affected. "From what I've gathered the worship tent sign has blown over and landed on a few people," he told The Sun. The festival-goer added he had been inside the worship tent shortly before the incident and had stepped out to head towards the main stage. Alarm spread further when the festival's live stream on GOD TV was abruptly cut. A message displayed on a large screen at the main stage read: "Due to circumstances beyond our control, the show has been stopped. "Any campers please head back to the campsite immediately. Any non-campers please remain in the main arena." Festival-goers also received an email instructing them to return to their tents and stay away from the stage areas owing to safety concerns. James Lewis of Premier, who was present at the event, said: "We've been told that the site is on lockdown, with people not allowed to get in or out." Nobody is currently permitted to enter or leave the grounds as emergency crews continue their work. South East Coast Ambulance Service confirmed it had been alerted to the situation shortly after 3.30pm. A spokesman said: "We were called to reports of multiple people in need of medical attention at Wiston Park, Steyning shortly after 3.30pm today (August 28, 2026). "Ambulance crews, critical care paramedics and specialist response teams are currently on scene with six patients confirmed so far. "We have no further information to share at this time but will provide updates as soon as possible."
