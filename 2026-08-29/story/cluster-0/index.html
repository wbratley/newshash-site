A66 crash: Seven more arrested in organised crime probe as total reaches 24

Unbiased summary

On 22 August, a head-on collision on the A66 near Middlesbrough killed two police officers, PC Matthew Blades and PC Tom Clough, and five young men in a Volkswagen Passat. The Passat was driving the wrong way and had suspected links to organised crime. Following the crash, a series of violent incidents occurred in the Teesside area, including a car ramming, arson, a stabbing, and a house fire that killed a seven-year-old girl and her aunt. Police have stated the house fire is not linked to the crash. As of Friday, seven more arrests brought the total number of people detained in connection with the crash to 24. The latest four men, aged 21 to 40, face suspicion of criminal damage with intent to endanger life, arson, and drug offences. Three others face threats to kill or possession of criminal property. Police also seized class A drugs, a vehicle, and £15,000 in cash. Hundreds of extra officers have been deployed to maintain order.
