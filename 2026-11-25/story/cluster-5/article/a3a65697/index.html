Janet Street-Porter wades into Mariella Frostrup's I'm A Celeb race row and says older people are 'patronised'

Article text

Mariella Frostrup's Loose Women co-star Janet Street-Porter has weighed in on the I'm A Celeb race row, claiming that older people are often 'patronised'. Janet, 79, discussed how Mariella, 64, handled her argument over cooking in the jungle camp with AJ Odudu, 38, and the connotations of her use of the word 'aggressive' on today's episode of Loose Women. Charlene White, 46, opened the debate by insisting: 'Mariella was wrong, she should not have used that term,' adding: 'There's a lot of other people talking about it without any lived experience whatsoever.' She said: 'I think perhaps people who are confused by that situation, really watch that clip and understand what it is like to be in AJ's situation and perhaps think about your own language.' Janet, who appeared on I'm A Celeb in 2004, agreed with her co-star, adding that 'Mariella wasn't listening', but she cautioned 'it doesn't just apply to race'. She said: 'It also applies to patronising older people and people with disabilities. I think the truth of the matter is we all ought to be more receptive to other people's needs and their boundaries. 'We must say to our audience at home, we're not giving them a lecture, we're trying to explain how things are now...
