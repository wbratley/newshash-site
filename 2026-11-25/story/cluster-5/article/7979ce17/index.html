Mariella ‘WON’T return to Loose Women’ after ITV colleagues slam her over I’m A Celeb ‘race row’, say fans

Article text

MARIELLA Frostrup ‘WON’T return to Loose Women after ITV colleagues slam her over the I’m A Celeb ‘race row’, fans have speculated. AJ Odudu broke down in tears on Sunday after Mariella branded her “aggressive” after several rows relating to cooking in the camp. Mariella, who regularly appears on Loose Women, was the topic of discussion on the ITV daytime show today. Tuesday’s instalment of the chat show saw Charlene White, 46, Janet Street-Porter, 79, Sue Cleaver, 63, and Samia Longchambon, 44, discuss their co-star and her comments towards AJ. Sue said: “I think it’s really important to say, it’s very, very difficult in there, as we know the three of us have been in there. “But I think AJ or anyone should be able to disagree, express frustration, stand their ground without being accused of being aggressive because I didn’t see anything aggressive. Read More about Mariella Charlene added: “I know AJ really well and she is the least aggressive person I know, she’s one of the nicest people in telly. “ She went on: “Mariella was wrong, she should not have used that term and I know lots of people have talked about this situation, very few black women on TV have been able to have the opportunity to talk about it, there’s been a lot of other people talking about it without any lived experience whatsoever.” Fans now think that Mariella will not be returning to the panel when she comes out of the jungle. On Reddit, one person speculated: “Well, Loose Women has thrown Mariella under the bus… Most read in TV “Can’t see her being back on the show when she is out of the jungle – I completely agree that she threw herself under the bus. “I’m just saying that there are shows out there that would be making excuses and saying that that was out of character, it was the lack of food/sleep, the stress got to her etc etc but they didn’t, which is fair play. “People tend to protect their own so its a good thing that she is being called out on the same show that she works for.” Another penned: “Yeah there’s no way ITV will have her back on the show now.” A third said: “She threw herself under the bus, as much as I hate the show we have to be proud of them for holding her accountable. “That’s how people change their way, they need to see what they’ve done is wrong and has consequences. If everyone held their friends, family, and community accountable the world would be a much better place.” Speaking about the Loose Women addressing Mariella’s comments today, another person said: “I’m glad they’ve said something about her behavior it just goes to show that even tho they are a team they will hold her accountable for what she’s done.” “I really commend them for doing this tbh,” agreed a fifth. While a sixth added: “It would’ve been really easy for them to be gentle on one of their fellow panellists or downplay it (or for ITV to ask them to do that), but I’m really glad they didn’t. Mariella was in the wrong, and it’s right that they said so.” The Sun has reached out to ITV and Loose Women for comment. Earlier in the week the I’m A Celeb contestant left AJ Odudu in tears after yet another spat about cooking During Sunday’s episode, as the celebrities woke up, Joanna and Claire discussed the tension that had arisen between the pair. Claire then spoke to Wayne about her concerns and how they could try and detuse the situation. When Wayne spoke to AJ, she said: “I think basically my observation is the food arrives and immediately it causes her stress. You don’t even have to say anything, it just stresses her out.” Claire then suggested to AJ : “Maybe you should leave it if it makes things get a bit tense.” “I’ll speak to her,” added Wayne adding the quicker it could be solved the better. When Mariella arrived Wayne explained that a few people had said that it felt very stressful at dinner. “It does get stressful,” agreed Mariella and added there needed to be more of a system and that it wasn’t a lovely collaborative afternoon in AJ’s and Mariella’s kitchen. “I just didn’t know the system, but now I do and I appreciate this conversation,” said AJ. Mariella then explained that it would have been less stressful if AJ had just sat down with her for two minutes and asked what worked best. AJ replied: “I feel like wrongly, incorrectly there’s an idea that I’ve come in saying that I desperately want to cook and that I’m going for it. I appreciate that I don’t know what’s going on.” Mariella interrupted and said: “AJ I think you’re making a drama out of a crisis, I was thrilled that there was one other person in the camp that was prepared to say they’d cook.” “You have a funny way of showing it, that’s all,” replied AJ. Mariella replied: “Well you see that’s just aggressive and that’s what keeps happening. “Please don’t use those words towards me, dramatic and aggressive are very harsh words, ” said AJ. Later, Mariella spoke to Joanna and Claire about AJ being aggressive towards her but her co-stars didn’t agree with that description. AJ broke down in tears and said she knew she had not been aggressive and that it was an insane way to describe her as she was comforted by Sharky. “You’re not aggressive,” said Sharky before adding “You’re a lot of things, a lot of good things, but defo not aggressive, don’t worry about that.” Later, in the Bush Telegraph, AJ admitted she was very upset. At the time, she explained: “Being called aggressive especially someone like me is just a triggering word.” During Tuesday night’s show, Mariella issued a sincere apology to AJ and said that she was sorry for calling her “aggressive”.
