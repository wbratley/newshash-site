Thomas Markle could face 20 years in jail as he’s indicted on child pornography charges after film negatives were allegedly found inside his storage unit The Duchess of Sussex’s estranged father, Thomas Markle Sr, has officially been indicted on child pornography charges, weeks after film negatives showing children in sexually explicit poses were allegedly found inside his storage unit. The 82-year-old is now facing two counts of possession of child pornography, and a statutory minimum of 20 years in federal prison if he were to be convicted. He is also facing potential charges in the Philippines, but is being held in federal custody without bond in Florida in the meantime. The multinational criminal investigation into Meghan Markle's dad came after his son, Thomas Markle Jr, 60, allegedly discovered a hoard of child sexual abuse material at the home they formerly shared in Cebu, Philippines. He claimed to have later told the FBI about the location of the two Los Angeles storage units where further material was allegedly uncovered. During the California raid, FBI agents seized approximately 78 items from the two units – including slides, film negatives and books that contained suspected child porn, court documents state. Among the negatives developed by investigators were photos, which seemed to have been taken several decades ago, were some showing Markle sexually abusing young girls at a Philippines motel, according to court papers. Others allegedly showed Markle with children in various stages of undress in what the FBI now believes to be the Anahaw Lodge motel, the complaint states. Markle had been renting the LA storage units since 2008 and made an autopay payment as recently as last month, according to a criminal complaint. He is now due to be officially arraigned on the possession charges in the coming weeks in the US District Court of Los Angeles. Meanwhile, a separate investigation is underway in the Philippines, where his son reportedly claimed to have found ‘hundreds, if not thousands’ of child sex images in boxes his dad had shipped over. Detectives in the city of Cebu confirmed later that they had found 'explicit content' – including footage of 'male and female minors' – on digital devices found among the contents. The Philippine National Police have since charged Markle Sr with two counts of distribution and one count of possession of disturbing material of kids being abused. It is unclear whether any further charges may be forthcoming.
