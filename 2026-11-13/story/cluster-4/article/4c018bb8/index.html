Is Outlook down? Microsoft outage impacts emails, Teams and Copilot

Article text

Is Outlook down? Microsoft outage impacts emails, Teams and Copilot Microsoft users reported being unable to send or receive emails on Outlook this evening, while others are struggling to load their inboxes altogether A major outage hit Microsoft platforms including Outlook this evening, leaving thousands of users struggling to access their emails and other services. More than 3,000 UK users reported issues on outage tracker Downdetector shortly after 6pm, with the majority struggling to log in to their accounts. In the US, almost 130,000 people have been affected. Reports have fallen since that peak, but many users continue to complain of being unable to send or receive emails, while others are struggling to load their inboxes altogether. Thousands of users have also reported problems with Microsoft's workplace messaging app, Teams, its AI assistant Copilot and wider Microsoft 365 services. The company confirmed connectivity issues across multiple regions, saying it was working to resolve problems initially reported in the US. Key Events Disruption ongoing as Microsoft tries another fix In its latest update at 10:09pm, Microsoft said it had identified a problem with a key part of its system becoming overwhelmed, causing services to struggle. Previous attempts to fix the issue had not worked as expected, the tech giant said. It is now testing a new approach, which involves restarting affected parts of its systems and gradually bringing them back online to check they are working properly before applying the fix more widely. Is Outlook still down? Hundreds of users are still logging issues with Outlook on Downdetector in the UK. Although the reports peaked at around 6pm, users are still facing problems as of 10.40pm. What's the latest from Microsoft? In its latest update, posted at 9:40pm, Microsoft says it is continuing to restart affected servers in a bid to get its services up and running. The tech giant is also looking at whether rolling back some recent changes to its systems could help speed up the recovery. But the update has prompted frustration from some users, with one pointing out that Microsoft had already said it was restarting servers more than two hours earlier. Responding to the update, the user wrote: "You told us 2 hours ago you were restarting servers." Another added: "Just keep rebooting king, it will work eventually." Map shows UK cities hit hardest by outage Users across the UK have reported problems with Microsoft Outlook this evening, with a map showing London, Manchester, Birmingham and Cambridge among the worst-hit areas. Microsoft restarts its servers in a bid to fix problem Microsoft said it would be restarting the affected servers and moving traffic away from parts of its system experiencing problems. It said: We've paused all deployments, we're moving additional traffic off the affected infrastructure, and we're restarting affected servers to return them to service. What caused the outage? Microsoft said a part of its infrastructure was experiencing "elevated load", which likely explained the connectivity errors. The tech giant said in an earlier statement: We determined the elevated load isn't driven by an increase in traffic, but by a number of servers being unexpectedly unavailable; straining the remaining infrastructure. In an update posted at 7:50pm, Microsoft said it was "testing a configuration" to fix the issue, which was "causing servers to fail automated health checks and become unavailable". Outlook, Teams and Copilot among Microsoft services hit Thousands of users have reported problems with Outlook, Teams, Copilot and other Microsoft 365 services, with many struggling to access their accounts and use the apps. In its latest update, Microsoft said it had run a "service health check method", which allowed a subset of affected servers to return to service. "We're now applying this change more broadly," it said. Outage initially reported in US before warning 'any region' could be affected Microsoft first reported connectivity problems in the US - where almost 130,000 people logged issues - before later discovering the problems could affect users "in any region". Reports of issues continue to come in from around the world, including from frustrated Brits. Microsoft 'aggressively pursuing all pathways' to restore services Microsoft has vowed to tackle the widespread connectivity issues, saying it is "aggressively pursuing all pathways" to restore its services. In an update shortly after 7:10pm, the company said: We're aggressively pursuing all pathways to reduce impact. We've paused all deployments, we're moving additional traffic off the affected infrastructure, and we’re restarting affected servers to return them to service. In the post, Microsoft linked to a page on its website where people can find updates, but users on X have complained the page will not load. The page warns of "service degredation on Microsoft 365", and displays an error message reading: "Users may be unable to connect to multiple Microsoft 365 services." Outage hits 74 countries within an hour Microsoft outage reports have been recorded in 74 countries and territories in the space of just an hour, according to Downdetector. A video shared by the outage tracker shows reports soared to almost 2,000 times their usual level. Thousands in UK hit by ongoing Outlook outage In the UK, reports of problems surged shortly after 6pm, according to outage tracker Downdetector. More than 3,300 users reported issues specifically affecting Microsoft Outlook, including difficulties logging into their accounts, accessing the website and using the app. Of those reports, 31% related to login issues, 27% concerned the website and 23% the app. The number of reports has since fallen to just over 1,800, however, users across the UK continue to report problems accessing Outlook and other Microsoft 365 programmes. Tens of thousands of users report issues across the globe Nearly 130,000 users on outage tracker Downdetector reported problems accessing Outlook in the US. Thousands of reports were also logged in the UK, peaking at around 6pm. New reports have since fallen from that peak, but many users are still facing problems. Users locked out of Outlook for more than two hours Frustrated users say they have been unable to access their Outlook accounts for more than two hours. One user in the US said: "It's been 3 hours since the first report. Still down." Microsoft confirms connectivity issues across 'multiple regions' Connectivity issues are affecting users across "multiple regions," Microsoft said. The problems were initially reported in America, but the company later discovered they may affect users "in any region". Microsoft said it had begun work to redistribute traffic across its worldwide infrastructure. Widespread outage hits Microsoft platforms A widespread outage has hit Microsoft platforms including Outlook. Users are having trouble using their email accounts and have encountered other errors across the tech giant's suite of apps, including messaging app Teams and its AI assistant Copilot. Microsoft said it is investigating the disruptions.
