Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigns amid reports of affairs and alleged Chinese espionage links

Unbiased summary

Jensen Huang resigned as CEO, president, and board member of Nvidia on Thursday, effective immediately, following reports of extramarital affairs with Alice Huang, a Chinese citizen suspected by US intelligence of spying, and with Nvidia executive Kimberly Powell. Nvidia's board appointed CFO Colette Kress as interim CEO and named Harvey Jones as chair. The board stated it had full confidence in Kress and that operations would continue. Nvidia shares fell sharply on the news, wiping billions from its market value. The board has commissioned an independent investigation into the relationship with Alice Huang. Huang's resignation came after he admitted infidelity in a memo to employees. US lawmakers have summoned Huang and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick to testify about national security concerns related to chip exports to China.
