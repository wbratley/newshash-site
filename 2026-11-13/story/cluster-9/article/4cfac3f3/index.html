Michael Carrick sacked LIVE: Man Utd players break silence as Pochettino announcement due Manchester United have dismissed Michael Carrick after just 10 games of the Premier League season, with Mauricio Pochettino lined up to replace him at Old Trafford Manchester United have finally dismissed manager Michael Carrick, with Mauricio Pochettino set to replace him. The worst-kept secret in football is out, with Carrick’s fate sealed following a meeting of the Old Trafford hierarchy - Jim Ratcliffe, Omar Berrada and Jason Wilcox - in Monaco on Wednesday. Carrick arrived at Carrington on Thursday morning, where he was given the bad news, with the club confirming in a statement that they plan to confirm a permanent successor "as quickly as possible". Barring an eleventh-hour twist, Pochettino will be that man. An agreement in principle has been reached for the former Tottenham and Chelsea boss, who is expected to take charge of two United States matches this week before joining the Red Devils in time for their Premier League trip to Liverpool. Carrick's future was the source of constant speculation in recent weeks, despite only being made permanent manager during the summer. Under his guidance, the 13-time Premier League winners have put together their worst start in 40 years and find themselves in the relegation zone. The 45-year-old leaves after securing just one win in 10 league matches since being given the reins permanently. That came all the way back in August against newly-promoted Ipswich Town, though all three promoted sides are now above United in the table. The former United midfielder was given three games to save his job following a chastening defeats to Como and Bournemouth last month. But two miraculous comebacks in four days, which earned a win against Roma and a 4-4 draw with Aston Villa, were not enough to keep him in a job. Carrick not the only manager to go today Crystal Palace boss Pierre Sage has become the second Premier League manager to be sacked today, with the club confirming the news at lunchtime, just a couple of hours after Michael Carrick's dismissal at Manchester United. Following the back-to-back departures announced within a matter of hours, Betfair has decided to pay out on both Carrick and Sage in the "first Premier League manager to leave" market. Carrick had been as big as 2/1, while Sage was backed at 4/1 three weeks ago before both clubs went on a dismal run of form. "Everyone's got an opinion in football, and it feels like the writing has been on the wall for Michael Carrick and Pierre Sage for a few weeks now," said Betfair spokesperson Sam Rosbottom. "However, it would seem that Premier League manager sackings are like London buses, you wait for ages then two come at once! Unfortunately for punters who backed Pierre Sage to be the first Premier League manager to leave his role, Man United finally got round to letting Michael Carrick know he was being replaced around an hour before Crystal Palace let go of their man. However, fair is in our name and given that both have gone on the same day, and official announcements coming in such quick succession, it seems only right that we pay out on both Carrick and Sage to be the first Premier League manager to go." In a statement confirming the exit, bottom-of-the-table Palace said: "The club would like to place on record its thanks to Pierre and his staff for their efforts during their time in South London, and wish them well for the future. An update on the process of appointing a new manager will be provided in due course." Thomas Frank is currently the favourite to replace the Frenchman at Selhurst Park.
