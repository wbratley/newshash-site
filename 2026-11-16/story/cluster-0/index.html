Prince Harry makes surprise visit to Ukraine, meets wounded veterans and praises Invictus competitors

Unbiased summary

Prince Harry, the Duke of Sussex, made a surprise two-day visit to Lviv, Ukraine, starting Saturday. He met wounded veterans and medics at the Superhumans Center, a rehabilitation clinic, and spoke at the first international War Trauma Conference. In his remarks, he said, 'You're going to win the war' and praised Ukrainian veterans as role models. He watched wheelchair rugby players competing for a place in Ukraine's team for the 2027 Invictus Games in Birmingham, and he suggested that a future Invictus Games could be held in Ukraine after the war. He also visited a school sports hall and hugged a player he recognised. A press blackout was imposed for security reasons until he left the country.
