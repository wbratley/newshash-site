UK weather: Yellow snow and ice warnings issued as temperatures set to drop

Unbiased summary

The Met Office has issued a yellow weather warning for snow covering parts of northern England and southern Scotland from midnight to midday on Tuesday, with 1-5cm of snow expected at lower levels and 5-15cm above 300-400 metres. A yellow ice warning is also in place for Scotland and northern England from 6pm Monday to 10am Tuesday. The UK Health Security Agency has issued yellow cold health alerts for north-west England, north-east England, and Yorkshire from 9pm Sunday to Thursday, warning of minor impacts on health and social care services. Temperatures have already dropped to -4.5°C in Santon Downham, Suffolk, and are forecast to fall further, with lows of -6°C or lower in some areas. Forecasters warn of potential travel disruption on higher routes and possible power outages.
