Inquest opens into death of unidentified man found in plane landing gear at Gatwick

Unbiased summary

On June 16, 2026, an unidentified man was found dead in the landing gear compartment of an Air Arabia flight that had arrived at London Gatwick Airport from Tangier, Morocco. A ground engineer discovered the body after spotting a packing strap hanging from the compartment. Emergency services attended the aircraft just after 11:30am. A post-mortem examination determined the cause of death as cardiac arrest, with hypoxia and hypothermia contributing. At an inquest opening at Horsham Coroner's Court, Area Coroner Joseph Turner stated the conditions inside the wheel compartment would have been extremely cold with low oxygen, and he had reason to believe the death was unnatural. The coroner adjourned the inquest until February 24, 2027. Sussex Police said they launched an investigation. Similar incidents have been reported in previous years at UK airports.
