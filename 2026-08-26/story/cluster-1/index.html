A66 crash kills five youths and two police officers; police make multiple arrests

Unbiased summary

On Saturday, a Volkswagen Passat traveling the wrong way on the A66 near Middlesbrough collided head-on with a marked police armed response vehicle, killing both officers and all five occupants of the Passat. The five victims in the Passat were Cole Worthy, 17, Theo Rae, 17, Makai Saddington, 18, Michael Cahill, 23, and Jacob Matusiak, 23. Police reported that prior to the crash, two cars, including a Volvo XC60, had been seen driving dangerously and ramming properties. Worthy's father's house was rammed before Worthy and others set out in the Passat. Cleveland Police have arrested 17 people in connection with the incident, which is being investigated as linked to serious and organised criminal activity. The Independent Office for Police Conduct confirmed officers had pursued the Passat for nearly seven minutes but abandoned the chase ten seconds before the crash.
