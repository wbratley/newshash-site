Dolly Parton, Country Music Icon, Dies at 80 After Battle with Cancer

Unbiased summary

Dolly Parton, the legendary country singer-songwriter, died on Tuesday at the age of 80 in Nashville, Tennessee. Her nephew, Bryan Seaver, announced her passing in a video on her Instagram account. Her representatives confirmed she died after a brief battle with cancer at the Vanderbilt-Ingram Cancer Center, surrounded by loved ones. Parton was known for hits like 'Jolene' and 'I Will Always Love You,' won 11 Grammy Awards, and sold over 100 million records. She was also a philanthropist, donating millions of books through her Imagination Library and funding vaccine research. Tributes poured in from celebrities, politicians, and fans, with President Donald Trump ordering flags lowered for a week. A private funeral was planned for immediate family.
