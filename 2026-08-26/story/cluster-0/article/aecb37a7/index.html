Dolly Parton's health woes as family reveals intimate funeral plans after country legend's death at 80: Live updates

Article text

Dolly Parton's family revealed plans for her funeral after the country legend died aged 80. The singer had been open about health challenges she faced following the death of her husband, Carl Thomas Dean, last year, including kidney, digestive and autoimmune issues. She shared her final health update on Friday, just days after canceling her Dollywood event, telling People: 'As I've shared in video messages throughout the past year, I am dealing with some health issues that I just didn't pay attention to when I was watching over Carl.' Parton's publicity team said the funeral service would be 'small' and 'private' and held for immediate family. The Jolene hitmaker's nephew Bryan Seaver confirmed her death in a video on Tuesday, which the legendary country singer had asked him to record years earlier. 'I have imagined the heaviness of this moment but haven’t truly felt it until now. It is an honor that is mixed with absolute pride and great sadness,' Seaver said. This live blog has now closed.
