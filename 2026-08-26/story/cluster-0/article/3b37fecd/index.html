Dolly Parton dies aged 80 The iconic singer has died at 80 following a decades-long career in music Don't Miss Most Read Iconic country music singer Dolly Parton has died at 80 years old. The legendary singer had been undergoing a recent health battle, and her family confirmed her death in a video statement released on Tuesday. It is understood the 9-5 hitmaker had been struggling with her health, with some upcoming gigs having been cancelled. The Tennessee-born star enjoyed a six-decade career after finding fame through some of the biggest hits in country music, such as Jolene, Islands in the Stream and 9-5. Ms Parton sold more than 100 million records world wide and was awarded 11 Grammys. She had recently cancelled performances that had been scheduled for December due to health reasons, stating she had to undergo "a few procedures." The iconic singer was also forced to pull out of a Dollywood event earlier this year due to a kidney stone. In March 2025, she received floods of support from fans after announcing the death of her husband of almost 60 years, Carl Dean. Ms Parton's nephew Brian Seaver confirmed the shock news of her death in a video shared on her Instagram page. It was captioned: "A message from the family of Dolly Parton," with Mr Seaver stating: "I am representing the Parton and Owens family today, announcing the passing of my aunt, Dolly Rebecca Parton Dean. "Sister, sis, aunt, granny to one generation and 'Gigi' to the next. "This video announcement is something Dolly asked of me years ago before I fully absorbed it as a future reality." (sic) The beloved singer's career progressed in 1980 when she starred in 9 to 5 alongside Jane Fonda. It became her first number one single in the Hot 100 in the US. Ms Parton wrote and originally sang I Will Always Love You as a goodbye to her former creative partner Porter Wagoner. He had hired her to be his sidekick on The Porter Wagoner Show and helped her to land a record deal. The song went on to become one of the defining love songs of all time after Whitney Houston covered the hit for 1992 film, The Bodyguard. Away from her incredible career, Ms Parton also dedicated herself to making a difference to others. Through her Dollywood Foundation, she established a literacy programme that posted a book each month to every child in the county from birth to age five. Since launching in 1995, the initiative has sent out more than 300 million children's books globally, with six million distributed in Britain alone.
