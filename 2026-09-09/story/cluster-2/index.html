US strikes Iranian oil tankers after missile attacks on warship; Iran retaliates

Unbiased summary

On Tuesday, US forces struck five Iranian oil tankers in the Gulf of Oman and near Kharg Island, sinking at least one, in response to two ballistic missile attacks by Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) on a US Navy warship, which the US said were evaded without casualties. Iran then launched 20 ballistic missiles at a US base in Jordan; Jordan reported intercepting 18, with two landing in unpopulated areas. Iran also claimed attacks on two US vessels and eight oil tankers, and warned crews in Kuwait and Bahrain to evacuate. Oil prices rose to near $100 a barrel. The exchange follows a recent escalation, with US strikes on Iranian tankers last week and Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia.
