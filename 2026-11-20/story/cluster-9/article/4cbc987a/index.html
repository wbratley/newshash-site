Two Afghan migrants who raped and sexually assaulted an autistic 14-year-old girl on a train came to the UK illegally on small boats

Article text

Two Afghan migrants who raped and sexually assaulted an autistic 14-year-old girl on a train came to the UK illegally on small boats Two Afghans who raped and sexually assaulted an autistic 14-year-old girl on a busy train arrived in the UK illegally on small boats, the Daily Mail can reveal. Baryalai Rahimi, 25, and 29-year-old Mirebadurahman Sadat, crossed the Channel in 2023. The younger man has claimed asylum, while Sadat has been granted leave to remain - a legal status that allows migrants to stay in Britain. Officials say they will seek to deport both men to Afghanistan - although this will be extremely challenging given the UK does not recognise the Taliban government. The pair spotted the runaway teenager at a station in rural Kent in February before following her onto the London-bound service. Rahimi raped the vulnerable schoolgirl in the train toilet while Sadat sexually assaulted her twice. CCTV showed each man walking through the carriage and leaving her alone with the other. Throughout the attack the young girl repeatedly told both men 'no' but was ignored. Baryalai Rahimi, 25, was found guilty of rape, sexual assault and three counts of sexual activity with a child Mirebadurahman Sadat, 29, was convicted of assault by penetration, sexual activity with a child and two counts of causing a child to engage in sexual activity The pair took turns abusing the victim, with CCTV capturing each man walking through the carriage and leaving the girl alone with the other. Rahimi is seen left and Sadat right During the victim's police interview, she was asked what the passengers on board the Southeastern service were doing. 'No one was really looking at us - everyone was just on their phones,' she said - before adding that Rahimi 'laughed' when she told him she was 14. After a two-week trial at Inner London Crown Court, a jury yesterday found the pair guilty on nine counts. Rahimi, of Southall, west London, was convicted of rape, sexual assault and three counts of sexual activity with a child. Sadat, of Cricklewood, was found guilty of assault by penetration, sexual activity with a child and two counts of causing a child to engage in sexual activity. Jurors heard that on February 5 this year, Rahimi and Sadat approached the girl at Sturry station near Canterbury, Kent. They all boarded a London-bound train at 3pm. Rahimi was the first to force himself on the girl and sexually assault her. During the journey he took her into one of the train's toilets and raped her. A short while later Rahimi walked down the carriage and left the girl alone with Sadat before he too subjected her to a sustained sexual assault. When the train arrived at London Bridge, they followed her onto a train to Peterborough before Rahimi got off at St Pancras and Sadat at Finsbury Park. The victim approached officers at Peterborough station and told them what had happened. British Transport Police detectives launched an urgent investigation to track down the two men. Using the victim's account and CCTV footage from the trains, Rahimi and Sadat were identified and then arrested in west London on February 10. A Home Office spokesman said: 'Our thoughts are with the victim of this sickening crime. 'The perpetrators have now been rightly convicted, and justice has been delivered by the courts. Once sentencing has taken place, we will ensure deportation action is pursued. 'We will do everything in our power to remove the vile criminals off British soil. Over 11,700 foreign criminals, including murderers and rapists, have been removed since the last election, up 41% on the previous two-year period.' Rahimi being escorted by a custody officer outside Inner London Crown Court Sadat (left) outside the court during the trial. He and Rahimi will be sentenced on December 18 BTP Detective Constable Gemma Littlewood said: 'Baryalai Rahimi and Mirebadurahman Sadat have caused irreparable harm to a child. 'They deliberately targeted her travelling alone, then conspired together so Rahimi could rape her and Sadat could sexually assault her. 'They knew her age, they knew she was vulnerable, and they heard her say no, but none of that stopped them from subjecting her to a heinous and prolonged attack. 'Today, the spotlight is rightly on these two despicable men, however I want to recognise the exceptional courage shown by the victim in reporting what they did to her.' In her police interview, played to the jury, the victim told how she felt unable to stop her abusers. The girl said Rahimi, who called himself 'Sandine', tried to lure her somewhere else while they waited for the train before forcibly kissing her anyway. She described Rahimi putting his arms around her and kissing her on the mouth, saying: 'He was standing over me and I was trying to say no. 'I felt like the kiss was a bit much.' Asked by officers whether she wanted him to put his arms around her, she replied: 'Not really,' adding she had never seen either of them before. The victim said: 'When he started getting a bit touchy, I was a bit scared, but I don't like to show people I'm scared, because obviously I like to be a nice person to everyone.' The girl described how Rahimi asked her to sit on his lap five minutes into the train journey. Asked why she did so, she replied: 'I don't know. It's a bit hard for me to say no to men. He's bigger than me, stronger, and I can't do anything. 'I just like to do what people want so it keeps me out of a dangerous situation.' Questioned what she thought might happen if she refused, the girl said: 'They get mad. They might hurt me.' She said Rahimi kissed and sexually assaulted her while she sat on his lap, adding: 'He didn't ask if he could touch me or kiss me. 'I didn't know how to say no, so I was just sitting there letting him do it.' The girl said that when Rahimi suggested going to the toilet, 'I was trying to tell him no', and described becoming upset during the assault because it 'really, really hurt'. Afterwards, the girl said Rahimi then washed his hands and told her to wait in the toilets for two minutes before following him out. Rahimi and Sadat will be sentenced on December 18.
