Two Afghan men convicted of sexual offences against 14-year-old girl on train; separate case of 59-year-old transgender woman jailed for sexual assault

Unbiased summary

Baryalai Rahimi, 25, and Mirebadurahman Sadat, 29, were convicted at Inner London Crown Court of sexual offences against a 14-year-old girl on a London-bound train on 5 February. Both arrived in the UK by small boat in 2023. Rahimi was found guilty of rape, sexual assault, and three counts of sexual activity with a child. Sadat was convicted of assault by penetration, sexual activity with a child, and two counts of causing a person to engage in sexual activity. The Home Office stated deportation action will be pursued after sentencing. Separately, Odin Valentino, now known as Victoria Winters, 59, was sentenced at Stoke-on-Trent Crown Court to three years in prison for two counts of sexual assault on a teenage girl in 2022. Winters, who is transitioning from male to female and is imprisoned in a male prison, has 15 previous convictions for 29 offences, including rape in 2011.
