UK and EU near Brexit reset deal on youth mobility, food trade and energy ahead of Brussels summit

Unbiased summary

UK and EU negotiators worked late into Thursday night to finalise a 'reset' agreement before Friday's summit in Brussels. The provisional deal includes a youth mobility scheme allowing up to 100,000 EU citizens and a matching number of young Britons to live, work or study in each other's territories annually, with an age cap raised from 30 to 31. The UK conceded on the cap after initially seeking 50,000-80,000. Negotiators also agreed to consult the UK on future EU food and farming rules, dropping UK demands for a voting role, and to work out details on tuition fees for EU students within six months. A new 'Channel action plan' on illegal migration was also discussed.
