Four men convicted of sexually abusing woman drugged by her husband

Unbiased summary

Philip Young, 50, a former Conservative councillor, pleaded guilty to multiple charges of drugging and raping his then-wife, Joanne Young, over a 13-year period. He advertised her on dating and swinging websites without her knowledge, and invited men to their Swindon home to sexually abuse her while she was sedated. On Thursday, four men—Connor Sanderson-Doyle, 32; Richard Wilkins, 62; Norman Macksoni, 48; and Mohammed Hassan, 38—were convicted at Winchester Crown Court of various sexual offences against Joanne Young. The case is the first in England where a jury convicted multiple defendants in an organised drug-facilitated sexual assault case. Joanne Young waived her anonymity and called for changes to consent laws, arguing that no one should be able to give consent on another person's behalf. Wiltshire Police acknowledged the potential underreporting of such crimes and had consulted with investigators from the Gisèle Pelicot case in France.
