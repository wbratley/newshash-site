Luke Humphries lost 10-9 to unseeded Tom Sykes in the Grand Slam of Darts second round after battling a neck injury.

Unbiased summary

Luke Humphries, the world number two and 2024 Grand Slam champion, was defeated 10-9 by unseeded Tom Sykes in the second round of the Grand Slam of Darts. Humphries, who was suffering from a neck injury, trailed 4-0 but fought back to force a deciding leg, where he missed a dart at double 10 for a 128 checkout. Sykes, ranked 72nd or 81st in various reports, held his nerve to hit double 18 and secure the biggest win of his career. The match featured high-quality darts, with Humphries averaging 102.02 and Sykes producing a strong performance. Humphries's exit follows that of world number one Luke Littler the previous night. Sykes will face Michael van Gerwen in the quarter-finals.
