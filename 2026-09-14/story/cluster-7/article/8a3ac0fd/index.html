A house 'mole' is being placed into the house for a week in a bid to 'cause havoc' for the remaining contestants who are all vying to win. Within minutes of arriving in the house, the 2026 cast were told that there was a mole amongst them, who will be doing their best to go undetected. They were told the mole - who the public know is YouTuber Tays - would nominate three people for eviction in the next episode of the show. The dramatic live launch saw returning hosts AJ Odudu, 38, and Will Best, 41, introduce the 16 contestants who would be battling it out for the £100,000 prize. The line-up includes a bunch of typically kooky hopefuls including a radio presenter, a 'stay at home daughter', and a 53-year-old content creator. Here we take a closer look at the new contestants...
