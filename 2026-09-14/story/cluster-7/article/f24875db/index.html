Big Brother 2026 line-up revealed – with YouTuber, reality star and radio host heading into the house tonight

Article text

THIS year’s Big Brother line-up has been revealed – and it includes a YouTuber, reality star and a radio host. The popular ITV show returned to our screens this evening for its 23rd series. And 16 new faces have entered the iconic house. Hosts AJ Odudu and Will Best were back to welcome them to their new pad for the next eight weeks. Are you interested to know who will be taking part this year? Let’s take a look… More on Big Brother RHIANNA, 21 Lives in: Chelsea Occupation: Stay-At-Home Daughter Rhianna said before entering the house: “I think I could surprise some people – I’ve always grown up with a stereotype and I have a resting bitch face. “If I were to win I would be so proud of myself – my whole life I’ve been living under my parents’ money.” Most read in TV HARRY, 30 Lives in: Croydon Occupation: Fitness Coach Harry said before entering the house: “I thrive on meeting new people but I do love my own space. I come alive at like 1am, I’ll be up like a night owl. “I hope to just give off good energy. If I win I’d try and remove my inner bicep tattoo because it’s a bit too intense.” CHARLOTTE, 18 Lives in: Oxford Occupation: Student Charlotte said ahead of entering the house: “As a quadruple amputee, people are surprised at anything I do, even normal things like cooking, doing my makeup, stuff like that. “If I won, my prosthetic legs are about £25,000 per leg, so probably put half in savings and half on that.” JOHANNE, 44 Lives in: Salford (from Los Angeles) Occupation: Online Marketing Johanne said ahead of entering the house: “I’ve been in the UK for some time, nd I know that BB is like a big part of British history at this point. “If I win I would start an alopecia line of hair care. I suffer from alopecia. “It’s something that happened to me when I was in my 30s.” OMAR, 21 Lives in: London Occupation: Jewellery Seller Omar said ahead of entering the house: “I don’t fit the prejudice that people have in their minds when they first see me. “I’m not what I look like. “If I won I’d invest back into myself and my family and buy all the kids from my estate school shoes.” PHILIP, 53 Lives in: Liverpool Occupation: Content Creator Philip said ahead of entering the house: “I’m a very good friend, but I’m a very bad enemy. “There’s no bulls**tting. “I’m not afraid to call someone out – and I like an audience when I do it.” PAULINE, 31 Lives in: Essex Occupation: Carer Paul said ahead of entering the house: “I’ve been watching since I was five. My default setting is laid back, very calm. “I always try to see the silver lining. “I used to be a private investigator – some of it I can’t go into detail with because it’s classified.” TAYS off of the BOV BOYS, 23 Lives in: Manchester Occupation: Streamer Tays said ahead of entering the house: “I feel like it’s the perfect role for me. “I couldn’t last for eight weeks but I’m excited to meet new people from different backgrounds. “I’m very confident I can keep the secret, but I think I’ll feel bad at times – I’ll get into competitive mode though and get the job done.” ROCHELLE, 57 Lives in: Essex Occupation: Housewife Rochelle said ahead of entering the show: “I’ve been married for 33 years; I’ve got three kids. I met him when I was 18 and married at 22. “And I have never, ever done anything for myself. “If I win I’d be buying more horses, handbags, but I’m quite bossy at home and I drive everyone mad.” MILLIE, 19 Lives in: Paisley Occupation: Bartender Millie said ahead of entering the villa: “People take one look at me and go, ‘oh my gosh, she’s such a girly girl. She’s so ditsy’. But they’re so, so wrong. I had just quit my job when I applied for BB, and I just thought to myself at the time, ‘I’m so much better than these jobs’. Why not?” FRANKIE, 23 Lives in: London Occupation: Marketing Manager Frankie said ahead of entering the house: “I feel like some people will like me and some people are going to hate me. “But I’m extremely handsome. I’m looking forward to spending time in the hot tub. “And for all the single mums that end up fancying me when I come out.” SAMUEL, 21 Lives in: Slough Occupation: Administrative Apprentice Samuel said ahead of the show: “I’ve been watching since I was young; in fact, my mum’s been watching since the very start. “A few years later here I am – I’m living out one of my dreams. “I think people might not appreciate my complete honesty.” CHELSEY, 29 Lives in: Manchester Occupation: Social Worker Chelsey said ahead of the show: “I feel like when people meet me they think ‘oh, look at her she’s come from a big house with all this money.’ But I haven’t. I fled to live in a women’s refuge and grew up in there for a few years. It’s made me the person I am today.” YASMIN, 29 Lives in: Manchester Occupation: Teacher Yasmin said ahead of entering the house: “I am messy. I’ll look at the dishes and be like ‘oh I’ll get back to it later’. “I’ve got three degrees and I’m really smart but I have no common sense. My mum has six children – she was a single mum. “So I would 100% love to help her.” LYNX, 24 Lives in: London Occupation: Property Developer Lynx said ahead of the show: “I’m looking forward to not having my phone – it will be really cool to have a full detox. “I think that if I just stay true to me, I’ll be fine. I’m not too good at lying.” ROB, 25 Lives in: Bideford Occupation: Radio Presenter Rob said ahead of the show: “Once as part of carnival I jumped off Ilfracombe Pier dressed as an elf with a cardboard Santa sleigh. “We all jumped into the water dressed as elves. “If I won I would buy a hot tub.”
