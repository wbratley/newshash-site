Grand Theft Auto VI released to critical acclaim and record pre-orders, amid PlayStation outage and school attendance concerns

Unbiased summary

Grand Theft Auto VI, the first new entry in the series in 13 years, was released on Thursday. Critics have praised the game, with review aggregator Metacritic reporting an average score of 99 out of 100. The game is set in the fictional state of Leonida, inspired by Florida, and features series' first female protagonist, Lucia. Rockstar Games spent a reported $1.5bn on development, and over 12 million pre-orders have been recorded. A PlayStation Network outage occurred on Wednesday evening, hours before launch, affecting users' ability to access online services. Schools across the UK have written to parents urging attendance, with some offering incentives. An employment tribunal in Glasgow heard evidence regarding the dismissal of 34 Rockstar staff for alleged confidentiality breaches. The game is projected to generate significant revenue and boost consumer spending.
