Donald Trump seen laughing with Vladimir Putin as he boasts 'he's my friend' at APEC meeting

Article text

Donald Trump seen laughing with Vladimir Putin as he boasts 'he's my friend' at APEC meeting The unprecedented trilateral summit marks the first time the Donald Trump, Vladimir Putin, and Xi Jinping have met face-to-face at the same time. Donald Trump boasted that Vladimir Putin was his "friend" as the US President shared a remarkable exchange with the Russian leader ahead of a historic trilateral meeting. Trump appeared to be in unusually high spirits as he entered the room and spotted Putin waiting for him. Pointing towards the Russian President, Trump joked: "Who's he? He's my friend." The pair then burst into laughter before Trump walked over to shake Putin's hand, telling him: "Nice to see you." The unusually familiar exchange saw Trump appear to share a light-hearted moment with Putin, despite the ongoing tensions surrounding Russia's invasion of Ukraine. The meeting marks the first time Trump, Putin and Chinese President Xi Jinping have held trilateral talks, with the three leaders coming together for discussions on Thursday. According to The Guardian, Trump said he believed a lot of "wonderful things" could come out of the discussions. "We've had a great relationship for many years. We know each other for a long time, and I think a lot of wonderful things can come out of this, or at least start. But I think they'll come out of this," he said. Looking ahead, the US leader will test his reputation as an expert dealmaker later on as he engages with China's Xi Jinping and Russia's Vladimir Putin, two of America's leading rivals. For now, their interactions have seemed warm. Trump also greeted Putin with a handshake and a pat on the arm as he lined up next to Xi for a photograph on Thursday morning. Trump, Xi and Putin had a brief public greeting on Wednesday ahead of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation Economic (APEC) Leaders' Meeting welcome banquet. It marked the first time three leaders met face-to-face at the same time. The meeting in Shenzhen, China, on Thursday will take place on the sidelines of the APEC summit. It comes at a pivotal moment in the power struggle between the three leaders. Trump has said that he plans to use the meeting to discuss limiting nuclear weapons and ending the war in Ukraine, but analysts are not expecting substantive breakthroughs despite the unprecedented nature of the meeting. The US president has frequently boasted of his rapport with Putin. He has even rejected new Russia sanctions, while Kyiv was undermined by disruptions in satellite support by Elon Musk's company, which it uses for its drones during the war. Musk is also along for the trip. His company SpaceX has intermittently throttled the military-grade Starshield satellite system used by Ukraine for launching its drones and intercepting hits from Russia. So far, Musk has kept a low profile. Whether Trump can bulldoze the Russian dictator is yet to be seen. Trump will also have to think about a power play when he has a separate one-on-one meeting with Xi, his third substantial meeting with him this year, right before the trilateral.
