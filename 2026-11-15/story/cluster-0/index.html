Russell Brand sentenced to 10.5 years; alleged victim Mia Latter calls for staggered age of consent

Unbiased summary

On Monday, former comedian Russell Brand was sentenced to 10 years and six months in prison after being convicted of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault; he was acquitted of two counts of rape and one allegation of attempted indecent assault. Separately, Mia Latter, now 37, who previously alleged in a 2023 Times/Sunday Times/Channel 4 investigation that Brand sexually assaulted her when she was 16 and he was 30, has waived her anonymity and called for a staggered age of consent that would make sexual relationships between adults and 16- and 17-year-olds illegal. Deputy Prime Minister Louise Haigh said the government would consider the proposal, calling it a 'massive step' that deserves scrutiny. Latter said she now describes the allegation as rape. Brand's former flatmate Mark Pinheiro has also appealed for other potential victims to come forward, claiming he witnessed additional incidents.
