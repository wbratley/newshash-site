England beat Japan 78-15 at Twickenham as Will Joseph scores record-equalling five tries

Unbiased summary

England defeated Japan 78-15 in a Nations Championship rugby match at Twickenham. Will Joseph, recalled after a four-year absence, scored five tries, equalling England's record. England scored 13 tries in total, including a first-half hat-trick from Joseph inside 20 minutes. Japan lost lock Harry Hockings to a 20-minute red card in the first minute. England led 49-10 at half-time, with tries from Freddie Steward, Ellis Genge, Cadan Murley, and Ben Earl. Japan scored a second try through replacement prop Opeti Helu in the second half, but England responded with further tries from George Martin, Ben Curry, Marcus Smith, and Joseph. Japan head coach Eddie Jones expressed disappointment, calling it a hard lesson for a young team. England head coach Steve Borthwick said the squad is being built and players have put their hands up.
