Alan Milburn to publish youth unemployment review with 'opportunity guarantee' proposal

Unbiased summary

Alan Milburn is set to publish a review into youth unemployment, recommending a legally binding 'opportunity guarantee' to provide 16- to 24-year-olds with a job, training, or employment support, with potential benefit sanctions for those who decline. The number of young people not in education, employment, or training (NEET) has surpassed one million for the first time in over a decade. Prime Minister Andy Burnham is expected to urge Labour MPs to support the reforms, which include billions in upfront investment funded by cuts to sickness and other benefits. Some Labour MPs have expressed concerns that the changes could reduce support for disabled people who cannot work. The Guardian has launched a series following eight young people's job search experiences.
