Cold health alerts issued for parts of England amid temperature fluctuations

Unbiased summary

The UK Health Security Agency (UKHSA) has issued yellow cold health alerts for the North West, West Midlands, and Yorkshire and the Humber regions from 9pm Sunday until 9am Thursday. These alerts indicate a likelihood of minor impacts on health and social care services, including increased healthcare use and greater risk to vulnerable groups such as those aged 65 and over and those with pre-existing health conditions. The alerts follow a cold snap that brought temperatures as low as -7.2C in South Newington, Oxfordshire, on Saturday morning, the lowest of the autumn so far. Milder conditions are expected on Sunday, with temperatures around 2-6C for most, along with widespread rain and possible thundery showers in the south. Colder weather is forecast to return on Monday, with sunny spells and wintry showers in the north, and light snow possible on northern hills.
