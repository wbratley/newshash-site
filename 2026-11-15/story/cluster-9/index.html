Wales defeat New Zealand 35-28 for first win in 73 years

Unbiased summary

Wales beat New Zealand 35-28 at Principality Stadium in Cardiff, securing their first victory over the All Blacks since 1953. Wales scored five tries, with Louis Rees-Zammit, Tomos Williams, Dewi Lake, Archie Griffin, Eddie James and Rees-Zammit again crossing, while Sam Costelow converted all five. New Zealand responded through Cortez Ratima, Wallace Sititi, Fehi Fineanganofo and Peter Lakai, with Richie Mo'unga converting all four. Wales led 21-21 at half-time and pulled away in the second half. The win ended a 34-match winning streak for New Zealand over Wales. Dewi Lake captained Wales and called the result an "amazing feeling." The stadium had about 20,000 empty seats.
