The announcement is set to be made in his keynote speech at the party’s conference in Birmingham. He will also blast the Tories for their “failed” 14 years in government and highlight that Andy Burnham “wants 10 more”. To bring home his argument, he will state that “time is running out to save Britain” and that the country “doesn’t have 10 years to be saved”. In the first of his two speeches at the conference, he will say: “Within days of winning the general election, we will introduce dozens of Bills and force Parliament to sit all through summer for seven days a week to pass them.” It comes as Reform UK has been reported to the police over allegations it breached political donation rules by having polling paid for by an overseas company. The party took funding from a US firm for three polls it commissioned earlier this year costing £32,500, Channel 4 News reported.
