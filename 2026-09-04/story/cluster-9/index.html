Feminist icon Gloria Steinem dies at 92 at her New York home

Unbiased summary

Gloria Steinem, the American feminist journalist and activist, died on Wednesday at her home in New York City at the age of 92. Her foundation announced her passing in a statement posted to Instagram, saying she was surrounded by loved ones and continued working for equality until the end. Steinem was a central figure in second-wave feminism, known for undercover journalism, public speaking, and campaigning on issues including reproductive rights, LGBTQ+ rights, and opposition to war. Over her career she wrote nine books and co-founded Ms. magazine. Following her death, tributes poured in from public figures and ordinary citizens, while her upcoming memoir was noted as a posthumous testament to her life.
