Senne Lammens HOWLER costs Man Utd crucial win as Mauricio Pochettino's concerns remain

Article text

A Senne Lammens' howler cost Mauricio Pochettino his first win as Manchester United boss with Sporting Lisbon securing a late draw after more VAR controversy. The Belgian made a costly individual error in the latter stages, which allowed the Portuguese side to equalise, after Bruno Fernandes' brace had put the Premier League strugglers in front. Lammens' night to forget saw him give away a penalty, which was converted by Luis Suarez. His blushes looked to be spared by his teammate as he scored twice in three second-half minutes. But the United goalkeeper dropped the ball at the feet of the Sporting attacker, who slotted into an empty net. United did look to have scored a late winner, but VAR intervened to rule that the hosts' goalkeeper had been fouled, much to the frustration of Pochettino. Despite offering little in the opening half hour the hosts were award two penalties in less than two minutes. The first came from a clumsy foul from Lisandro Martinez, albeit the Argentine will argue it was a soft decision. Senne Lammens would spare his blushes, saving from Luis Suarez. The Sporting forward stepped up minutes later when Lammens went from hero to zero, hauling down Max Araujo. Suarez would not make the same mistake, firing into the bottom corner. Amad thought he had United level shortly before half-time as he headed home, having seen his initial effort saved onto the bar. However the referee pinged Youri Tielemans for a foul in the build-up, which was another debatable decision. The Red Devils did equalise three minutes into the second-half with Fernandes, against his former side, converting the third penalty of the evening. The skipper completed the United turnaround his a second goal in three minutes - his long range effort hitting a divot in the pitch and evading a shocked Kaique Pereira. United looked set to see out the game for a much needed win, but Lammens would gift Sporting a late equaliser. The Belgian only need to take a routine high ball, but dropped it under no pressure at the feet of Suarez, who converted from close range. Adding insult to injury was VAR's decision to disallow their third of the night before Youri Tielemans was sent-off in stoppage time. No respite for Poch Two red cards, a goalkeeping howler and two penalties conceded inside two games of football. Pochettino must be wondering what on earth he's dealing with. We all know the manager takes the fall when things don't go right in this game, but even the best manager in the world would struggle to combat some of the errors United continue to make, especially defensively. Martinez's swipe in the first-half, no matter how soft, was that of a defender lacking in composure. Lammens - where do you start. Just when you thought he may feed off the confidence that stems from a penalty save, he goes and gives away a spot kick of his own. The Belgian had almost been caught napping with the ball at his feet early in the game. He was in no mans land when he came flying out from a corner, missing the ball completely. His night got even worse with perhaps his biggest error of the season, dropping a looped ball under no pressure, which cost United the win. Pochettino made four changes from the team who were well beaten at Liverpool, but he'll quickly be realising that the misfortune that exists in this United squad is impacting every player. The ex-Tottenham boss is the latest boss to try and turn this troubled club around. He's barely had a minute on the training pitch with the squad, and it seems he can't even rely on his team to be solid, secure and smart.
