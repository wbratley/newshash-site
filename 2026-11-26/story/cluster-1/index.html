Poundland enters administration, putting more than 10,000 jobs at risk

Unbiased summary

Poundland has entered administration, appointing AlixPartners as administrators, with over 10,000 jobs at risk across its 600 UK and Ireland stores. The retailer's owner, Gordon Brothers, which bought Poundland for £1 in 2024, said it explored a sale but found no viable solvent solution for the whole business. All stores and distribution centres will remain open while administrators pursue sales of the business and assets. The collapse follows the withdrawal of borrowing facilities by Gordon Brothers, according to a memo from Poundland's directors. Unions and politicians have criticised the owner's handling, and potential buyers may still acquire parts of the business through the administration process.
