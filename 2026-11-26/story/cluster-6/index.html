Ofcom receives 887 complaints over I'm A Celebrity argument between Mariella Frostrup and AJ Odudu

During the 2026 series of I'm A Celebrity... Get Me Out of Here!, an argument occurred between campmates Mariella Frostrup and AJ Odudu over cooking duties.
