Gordon Ramsay is announced as surprise new host of BBC's Top Gear after Jimmy Carr was forced to 'pull out of the running'

Article text

Gordon Ramsay has been confirmed as the new lead host of BBC's Top Gear after Jimmy Carr was 'forced to pull out' at the last minute. The unapologetic car-fanatic, 60, will be swapping the kitchen for the race track to lead a brand new line-up of presenters in 2028. Reacting to the news on a thrill-seeking drive around the race track with The Stig, Gordon screamed: 'YES! Top Gear is BACK. Woohoo, let's go!' The reboot comes four years after the show was shelved following Freddie Flintoff's crash, with filming expected to start across the world next year. Comedian and star of The Gentlemen Guz Khan, 40, has emerged as front-runner to co-host alongside Gordon. Gordon is no stranger to the show having been a guest on Top Gear on three occasions. He appeared in the first series in 2002, its eighth series in 2006 and 23rd series in 2016. The father of six, who is worth an estimated £200million, holds a racing licence and has competed on the track numerous times. He has built up an extensive knowledge of cars through years of ownership, with a collection worth more than £4.5million.
