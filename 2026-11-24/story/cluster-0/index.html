Nvidia Reinstates CEO Jensen Huang After Resignation and Trump Intervention

Unbiased summary

Nvidia's board unanimously voted to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO on Monday, less than two weeks after he resigned amid revelations of extramarital affairs, including one with a woman suspected of being a Chinese spy. The board stated it received input from senior US government officials and was satisfied with Huang's candor. President Donald Trump publicly urged the board to rehire Huang and claimed credit for the decision. Nvidia shares surged over 30%, adding roughly $1 trillion in market value. An internal investigation into the company's export control compliance continues, and Huang has not been charged with any crime.
