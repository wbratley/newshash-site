James Cleverly quits shadow cabinet to take on Sadiq Khan in fight to be next London mayor

James Cleverly quits shadow cabinet to take on Sadiq Khan in fight to be next London mayor - See more Daily Mail on Google - save us as a Preferred Source Sir James Cleverly is to resign as a shadow cabinet minister to run for London Mayor, saying the city has been 'let down by Labour'. The shadow minister for housing, communities and local government will leave his role with immediate effect. There had been speculation around the future of the Conservative MP for Braintree's career, and it is understood he felt the need to clarify his position prior to party conferences this autumn. It is unknown who will replace Mr Cleverly, 56, in the shadow cabinet, ahead of an expected reshuffle next month. If elected as candidate by the Tory party he will be the candidate to challenge Labour's Sir Sadiq Khan, who is currently halfway through his third term as London Mayor. Mr Khan has not yet confirmed if he intends to stand for a fourth term at the May 2028 election. Posting on X following the announcement, Mr Cleverly wrote: 'London needs more than an administrator. 'It needs a champion, someone who understands its potential and is prepared to fight for its future. That’s why I’m running to be the Conservative Candidate for London Mayor.' James Cleverly is to resign as a shadow cabinet minister to run for London Mayor Mr Cleverly will challenge Labour's Sir Sadiq Khan, who is currently halfway through his third term as London Mayor Mr Cleverly told party leader Kemi Badenoch of his decision on Thursday Speaking to the Sunday Times, the former foreign secretary said he had informed party leader Kemi Badenoch on Thursday. 'She took it well,' he told the paper. 'Kemi has been very gracious'. He added in an op-ed that 'London has been let down by Labour' and accused Prime Minister Andy Burnham of viewing it as a 'cash cow'. He said he intends to make 'a city to be proud of again'. Ms Badenoch is said to be disappointed to lose a pivotal member of her cabinet but supports Mr Cleverly to be the one to take on Mr Khan. But she cannot officially endorse any candidate as they must win the Conservative Party's nomination for the election. It is not known if any other candidates intend to put themselves forward for the Tory position. In 2024, Susan Hall stood for the party but lost to the incumbent mayor by around 200,000 votes. The only candidate confirmed for the 2028 election so far is Reform's Laila Cunningham, a Westminster City Councillor who defected from the Conservatives in June 2025. 'We need a credible big hitter putting themselves forward for mayor of London,' Mr Cleverly told the newspaper. He added Mr Khan has been 'asleep at the wheel' while leading the UK's largest city, accusing him of 'lots of posturing' with 'very little delivery'. Shadow ministers and fellow Tory MPs were quick to lend their support to the Essex MP. Richard Holden, shadow transport secretary said: 'Excellent news for Londoners and the whole country. 'A real heavyweight in the race who’d make a superb Mayor of London for every part of our great capital city.' And MP for Huntingdon Ben Obese-Jecty said: 'Put simply, James Cleverly will be an outstanding Mayor of London. 'Fantastic news that he’s put himself forward to be the Conservative candidate. He has my full support. London needs new leadership and James has the experience and confidence to do it.' Before first being elected as an MP in 2015, Mr Cleverly served on the London Assembly for eight years. He held posts including leader of the Conservative group in the Assembly, the Mayor's Youth Ambassador and, under Boris Johnson, Deputy Mayor. The army reservist has been appointed to various cabinet positions under three prime ministers, including foreign and home secretary.
