US flights updates: Passengers at UK airports to face disruption after FAA ground stop

Article text

US flights updates: Passengers at UK airports to face disruption after FAA ground stop The FAA has begun gradually lifting restrictions after a ground stop was ordered at airports across the USA - there are 3,000 flights cancelled and 2,000 delayed today as a result Passengers flying to and from UK airports are likely to face disruption after flights were grounded across the United States. Manchester Airport has also warned that three US-bound Virgin Atlantic flights scheduled to leave on Saturday morning are likely to be affected. Gatwick, meanwhile, is understood to be facing minimal impact. The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) said the dramatic step was taken due to "extraordinary circumstances and for reasons of safety". US Secretary of the Department of Transportation, Sean Duffy, confirmed the order came "following a communication issue impacting all Air Route Traffic Control Centers". In the early hours of this morning the FAA has begun gradually lifting restrictions as systems come back online, with some flights receiving clearance to depart for overseas destinations, including Germany and Prague. Follow our blog below for the latest updates... Key Events IN PICTURES: Travel chaos across America More than 5% of flights scheduled for Saturday already cancelled, stats suggest According to Cirium, the aviation analytics company: - There are 22,232 flights scheduled to depart from US airports on Saturday. This is around 4,000 more than Friday (Saturdays tend to be slower travel days). - More than 30% of flights departing from the US were cancelled on Friday. - And more than 5% of flights scheduled for Saturday have already been cancelled. - Some of the countries with the most flights to the US cancelled include Mexico, Canada and the United Kingdom. Full list of airport delays and cancellations Passengers flying to and from UK airports in America today are likely to face disruption after flights were grounded across the United States by The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA). At least 827 flights diverted The airports where many of the flights ended up being diverted included Washington Dulles International Airport, Los Angeles International Airport, Cincinnati/Northern Kentucky International Airport and Nashville International Airport. FAA issues update Delta says it is resuming flights Delta Air Lines says "we are safely resuming flights" after the ground stop was lifted. They posted on X: "The FAA-mandated ground stop has been lifted and we are safely resuming flights. We encourage you to check the Delta app or dl.aero/6018xB2cn for flight status and the latest updates. "Thank you for your understanding as we work as quickly as possible to fully restore." IN PICTURES: Passengers face lengthy delays Moment passengers and pilots reacted to nationwide ground stop WATCH: Press conference with Transportation Secretary Duffy FAA ground stop lifted The FAA has officially cancelled its nationwide ground stop, according to a new advisory issued by its command centre. The notice confirms the restrictions affecting all US airports have been lifted. Airspace clearing above the United States tonight SpaceX allowed to launch rocket despite ground stop Images show stranded passengers across the US Restrictions being eased - FAA update The Federal Aviation Administration said it has been gradually lifting restrictions and resuming normal air traffic operations as systems come back online, though residual delays and cancellations are expected nationwide.Some flights were given clearance to depart for overseas locations, including flights to Germany and Prague. National Weather Service says radars are 'fully operational' The National Weather Service said its radars are fully operational and working normally. “Outdated content from isolated webpages stating otherwise has been circulating, but NWS radars are operating normally,” the agency said. Control tower at airport describes situation as 'whole mess' At Newark Liberty International Airport, workers in the control tower have been describing the situation as "a whole mess right now," according to LiveATC. All scheduled flights to the United States at Mexican airports grounded The Federal Civil Aviation Agency (AFAC) reports that, due to failures in communication systems affecting various air traffic control centers in the United States, the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) implemented temporary restrictions on air operations as a preventive operational safety measure. As part of these measures, all aircraft at Mexican airports with scheduled flights to the United States were asked to remain grounded until the aeronautical authorities of that country authorize the resumption of flights. Passengers traveling to that country are advised to consult the official channels of their respective airlines and airport groups to find out the status of their flights. AFAC is monitoring the situation in coordination with the relevant authorities and will continue to provide timely updates regarding the resumption of flights. Flights from Europe suspended Eurocontrol has suspended all flights departing from the European Civil Aviation Conference (ECAC) area bound for US airports, following a request from the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA). A notice issued by Eurocontrol stated: "On request of FAA due to technical issues all traffic departing within ECAC area between 2200 UTC on 13 NOV and 0500 UTC on 14 NOV, bound for US airports is suspended." John F. Kennedy International Airport - latest advice Travelers should confirm their flight status with their airline before going to the airport, John F. Kennedy International Airport said in a social media post.The New York City airport made the announcement just after 5 p.m. ET. JFK is a major international airport for the US. Is it linked to Microsoft's widespread outage? Three senior officials said there’s nothing that indicates a connection between today’s FAA incident and Microsoft's widespread outage, NBC reports. No sign of cyberattack at this stage, officials say At this early stage of the investigation into today's communications outage, there is no indication that the incident was caused by malicious activity, multiple officials from multiple agencies involved tell NBC News. They say they haven’t seen any evidence of the type of activity normally associated with a cyberattack. San Francisco says Pacific-bound flights are cleared to leave San Francisco International Airport said minutes ago that Pacific-bound flights have been cleared to leave, including to Hawaii and Asia, NBC reports. It said that the clearance occurred at 3 p.m. local time (6 p.m. ET). Flight cancellations across the US have now exceeded 1,700 Latest from American Airlines American Airlines has told passengers to expect disruption for the rest of the day. Here's its statement: "The FAA has temporarily paused departures nationwide. "Flights already in the air can continue to their destinations. "We expect disruption to our schedule for the rest of the day, depending on the length of the FAA's nationwide ground stop. "It will be all hands on deck across American to get customers on their way as soon as possible when the FAA lifts this ground stop." More than 1.5 million passengers still scheduled to depart US airports today There are still 11,000 flights and some 1.5 million passengers due to depart from US airports today. As of 22:50 UK time, there were 600 diverted flights. More flights from UK could be impacted A spokesman for Manchester Airport confirmed three US-bound flights scheduled to leave on Saturday morning – all operated by Virgin Atlantic – are likely to be affected if the issue is not resolved soon. Some flights may operate, FAA says The FAA has said flights between Anchorage, AK, Oakland, CA, and Hawaii may operate despite the nationwide ground stop. Airports 'running out of space for planes to land' Some airports in the US are reportedly running out of space for planes to land. The Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport is said to be at capacity, while Chicago's O'Hare airport is nearing capacity too, according to NBC. Incoming flights may be forced to divert elsewhere. Some flights departing Some flights seem to be departing on a case-by-case basis. FlightRadar maps show that a flight from Boston to Abu Dhabi departed less than an hour ago. Ground stop 'may be linked to new AI system' There have been claims the ground stop may be linked to the FAA expanding use of an $875million AI tool, SMART. FAA fact sheets describe the system as combining “weather patterns, flight paths, traffic flow and controller staffing metrics” for air traffic controllers.
