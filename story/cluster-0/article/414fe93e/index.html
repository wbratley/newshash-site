Polanski falls short as Labour wins Holborn and St Pancras by-election

Article text

The Labour Party has won the Holborn and St Pancras by-election by a solid, though reduced margin of victory. The election for Sir Keir Starmer’s old seat was fiercely contested by a total of 15 candidates, including Green leader Zack Polanski and Labour’s Sagal Abdi-Wali. It marked a defining moment for Polanski, who contested the Labour stronghold in a bid to join his party’s five MPs in Parliament. However, he ultimately fell short, securing 8,928 votes to Abdi-Wali’s 12,292. Conservative Ewan Cameron came third with 1,922, while Reform’s Pete Newman finished fourth with 1,612. The seat has been held by Labour since its formation in 1983, including for 11 years by Starmer between 2015 and September of this year. That remains unchanged today, although the Green Party insists the results reflect a close race.
