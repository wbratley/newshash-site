FAA lifts nationwide ground stop after communications outage disrupts US flights

Unbiased summary

On Friday, the US Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) issued a nationwide ground stop for all departing flights due to a communications outage affecting air traffic control centres. The stoppage, which lasted over four hours, led to thousands of delays and cancellations and stranded passengers. The FAA lifted the order at 8:15pm Eastern Time after identifying the problem as overloaded routers in an aging system. Officials stated there was no indication of a cyberattack and that safety was not compromised. The disruption also affected international flights to the US, with some UK airports warning of ongoing impacts.
