Argentina's Milei proposes Hong Kong-style Falklands handover, announces oil sanctions

Unbiased summary

Argentine President Javier Milei, in an interview with The Economist, suggested the UK could return the Falkland Islands to Argentina via a Hong Kong-style agreement, citing the 1984 Sino-British Joint Declaration. In a separate televised address, he announced sanctions on companies involved in offshore oil drilling near the islands, plans for a military base in Tierra del Fuego, and reiterated Argentina's sovereignty claim, arguing islanders have no legitimate right to self-determination. The UK government responded by reaffirming its commitment to the Falkland Islanders' right to choose their own status, citing the 2013 referendum where 99.8% voted to remain British. US President Donald Trump has said he is reviewing the US position on the dispute, which Argentina sees as supportive.
