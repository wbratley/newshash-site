Binance reports $6.3bn hack, freezes $3bn; analysts attribute attack to North Korean group

Unbiased summary

Binance, the world's largest cryptocurrency exchange, reported a hack affecting approximately $6.3 billion in assets late Thursday. The company froze about $3 billion of the affected funds and stated it would cover all customer losses. An initial investigation suggested the breach began with a compromised engineer's laptop, though Binance later mentioned additional points of breach. Blockchain analytics firms TRM Labs, Elliptic, and Chainalysis attributed the attack with 'high confidence' to TraderTraitor, a North Korean state-backed hacking unit also known as Lazarus Group. Deposits and withdrawals were temporarily suspended. Binance has 335 million registered users and holds roughly $162.8 billion in assets under custody. The incident is the largest crypto hack on record, surpassing last year's $1.5 billion Bybit theft also blamed on North Korea.
