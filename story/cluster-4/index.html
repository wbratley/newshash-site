Spitting Image co-creator Peter Fluck dies aged 85

Unbiased summary

Peter Fluck, co-creator of the satirical puppet show Spitting Image, died at his home in Cornwall in the early hours of Saturday aged 85 after a recent illness, his family said. Spitting Image ran from 1984 to 1996 on ITV for 18 seasons, featuring puppet caricatures of politicians, royals, and celebrities. At its peak it drew audiences of around 15 million and won awards including a Bafta and two Emmys. Fluck, born in Cambridge in 1941, developed the show with former college friend Roger Law. A family statement described him as a private man who eschewed fame and attention. He is survived by his family and his creative legacy.
