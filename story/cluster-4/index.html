Tanker collides with docked cruise ship in New Orleans; all passengers accounted for, 32 hospitalized

Unbiased summary

On Friday afternoon in New Orleans, the tanker Atlantic Asphalt collided with the docked American Cruise Lines vessel American Heritage on the Mississippi River. The cruise ship sustained damage and partially capsized. According to officials, all passengers and crew were accounted for, with 32 people taken to hospitals for non-critical injuries, including two harbor police officers involved in rescue efforts. New Orleans Mayor Helena Moreno called the outcome a 'miracle on the Mississippi.' The cause of the collision is under investigation, though the U.S. Coast Guard noted the tanker apparently experienced a loss of steering. Multiple agencies participated in the rescue operation, and the river was temporarily closed.
