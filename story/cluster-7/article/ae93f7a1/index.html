Donald Trump congratulates Robert Pattinson on being named James Bond - but wants to know if he's 'a conservative'

Article text

Donald Trump congratulates Robert Pattinson on being named James Bond - but wants to know if he's 'a conservative' US president Donald Trump has given his verdict on Robert Pattinson's casting as James Bond – years after he urged the British actor to break up with Twilight co-star Kristen Stewart Donald Trump has congratulated Robert Pattinson on becoming James Bond - before immediately asking his aide to find out whether he's a conservative. Pattinson was named the new 007 by Amazon-owned MGM studios last week - ending years of speculation over who would succeed Daniel Craig in the role as cinema's most famous spy. His casting attracted the attention of high-profile figures around the world, with the Royal Family commenting via social media on the day of the announcement: "Welcome to His Majesty's Secret Service, Mr Bond." And now the US president - known to closely follow Pattinson's personal life - has weighed in on the news. The 80-year-old then asked Natalie Harp - an assistant known for printing out physical copies of news articles for him to read - what James Bond's political leanings were. "Is James Bond conservative? Natalie..." he reportedly shouted down the corridors of the White House, through the Oval Office door. The US president has previous when it comes to commenting on the career of the 40-year-old British actor. In 2012, he posted a string of tweets urging Pattinson, then aged 36, to dump his Twilight co-star Kristen Stewart, warning she "cheated on him like a dog and will do it again." He later wrote: "Robert I'm getting a lot of heat for saying you should dump Kristen- but I'm right. If you saw the Miss Universe girls you would reconsider." Pattinson commented on the bizarre posts after Trump began his first term in 2017, telling an interviewer they "didn't really mean anything" at the time and joking: "Can I use this in my acting?" Kristen Stewart also referenced the tweets during her Saturday Night Live opening monologue the same year, where she quipped that Trump was "in love with my boyfriend". Donald Trump is a fan of the 007 series, having previously named Sean Connery as his "all-time favourite Bond". Shortly after Amazon MGM Studios announced that auditions for Daniel Craig’s successor were underway last year, the White House X account posted an AI-generated poster showing Trump in a dinner jacket holding a gun, stamped with the words: 'MAKE AMERICA GREAT AGAIN' and '007'.
