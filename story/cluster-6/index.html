Paris Fury retracts schizophrenia claim about Tyson Fury; Netflix documentary edited

Unbiased summary

Paris Fury, wife of boxer Tyson Fury, retracted her suggestion that her husband was diagnosed with schizophrenia. The remark appeared in an initial version of a Netflix documentary. Netflix said it worked to update the footage. The British Boxing Board of Control had indicated it was investigating the comments and could deny Fury a licence to fight Anthony Joshua on 11 December if he was deemed unfit. Tyson Fury has previously spoken about receiving a diagnosis of schizophrenia. In a statement, Paris Fury said Tyson showed some symptoms but does not have the condition and has never received a diagnosis. Fury has been open about his mental health struggles since beating Wladimir Klitschko in 2015.
