Chester Zoo python undergoes first known electrochemotherapy for cancer

Unbiased summary

A 15-foot, 50kg reticulated python named Jodie Foster, living at Chester Zoo, became the first known snake to receive electrochemotherapy, a cancer treatment typically used on humans. The 23-year-old snake was diagnosed with a fibrosarcoma in her jaw, which was surgically removed in 2024 but recurred. In a 90-minute procedure involving two operating tables, vets removed the tumour and part of her jawbone, then administered electrochemotherapy to target remaining cancer cells. Months later, Jodie is eating normally, including swallowing whole chickens, and is described by zoo staff as 'bright and feisty.' The procedure was a collaboration with cancer specialists from the University of Liverpool. Ian Ashpole, Chester Zoo's veterinary officer, stated the treatment was likely a first for a snake.
