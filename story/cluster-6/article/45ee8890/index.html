Florida man jailed for life after murdering British woman he groomed on fetish website

Log in A Florida predator who called himself ‘Alpha Sadist’ has been jailed for life after being found guilty of the kidnap and first-degree murder of a vulnerable British woman he groomed on a fetish website. Dwain Hall, 54, was impassive as the unanimous verdict of the Florida jury was read out on Friday. His life sentence was confirmed shortly afterwards. During closing arguments, the court in Ocala heard how Hall had taken advantage of 32-year-old Sonia Exelby, who was mentally ill and “incapable of making any rational decisions”. Assistant state attorney Amy Berndt showed the jury the knife that she said Hall had used to stab Ms Exelby with four times. She said he then sought to keep it as a “prized possession” in order to relive the killing “over and over again for his own sexual, sadist pleasure”. She also said that just days before Ms Exelby’s body was discovered in a shallow grave, Hall had asked Google’s AI assistant about “how long would it take a large family dog to decompose”. Ms Exelby, who the court was told had a history of self-harm and had previously attempted suicide, was reported missing from her home on 12 October 2025. Her body was found by US investigators in Marion County, Florida, on 17 October. Hall was traced by police through the attempted use of the victim’s credit card by the roadside assistance company he owned. Addressing the jury on Friday, Ms Berndt, for the prosecution, said of Ms Exelby: “She was beautiful. She was kind and she was deeply loved by everyone who knew her. And she also suffered from mental illness for most of her life. She should be alive today.” While she had the support of her loving family and friends, she added: “But no-one could save her from the defendant, the Alpha Sadist. “Think about what he must have said to her over those two years, how did he manipulate her to get her to leave her home and travel thousands of miles to come here?” She told the court: “He took advantage of a severely depressed, mentally ill woman who was incapable of making any rational decisions. “If you believe that she came here and she 100 per cent wanted this man to kill her, he can’t do that. She cannot consent to that. He can’t do it. This man does not have the right to kill her, even if she wants to die.” Earlier, the jury heard that Ms Exelby’s consent was not a defence to the crime of first-degree murder. They were also told consent meant intelligent, knowing, involuntary consent, and did not include coerced submission, through the use of exploitation, threats of force, or intimidation. Ms Berndt also reminded the jury how images of the torture and rape and mutilation of women had been found on his phone. She pointed out in his biography for his online ‘Alpha Sadist’ identity, Hall had said: “Would love to find a domestic pain slut for complete control… Only complete submission is acceptable.” Ms Berndt told the court that just days before the discovery of Ms Exelby’s body, Hall had asked Google’s AI assistant on Android: “How long would it take a large family dog to decompose?” She also held up the knife Hall had sent to a friend in Ohio for safe-keeping, describing it as a “prized possession” and which was later found to have Ms Exelby’s blood on it. Ms Berndt said “He stabbed this into Sonia four times, four to seven inches deep. And why did he keep this? Because he wanted it back in six months if the storm blew over. Why? To relive it over and over again for his own sexual, sadist pleasure. Why else would he keep this? Why would he keep this?” Hall’s lawyer told the jury that Ms Exelby had travelled to the US “because she wanted to die” and raised the possibility that she had stabbed herself. While acknowledging it had been an “emotional case”, he also said it should not be decided because of disapproval at his client’s lifestyle. Hall’s legal team did not call any defence witnesses during the trial and he did not give evidence. Addressing the court after the guilty verdict, Ms Exelby’s brother Barry Wilton said: “This last year has been one of the hardest of our lives. “Losing our dear sister Sonia has had a profound impact on me and my entire family. I’m not sure we will ever be the same again. “I’ve seen a deep, lingering pain inflicted on all of my siblings. Their day-to-day lives have changed forever. I know Sonia would be so distraught to see the aftermath of her death. “Dwain, you pretended to care for Sonia. You didn’t at all. You used her to fulfil your sick fantasies. You had her life in your hands. You had the opportunity to save her, but instead you preyed on her vulnerabilities and you took her from us. “There will never be any forgiveness coming to you behind bars.
