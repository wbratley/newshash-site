Captain Tom Moore family charity applies to be struck off after misconduct findings

Unbiased summary

The charity set up by the family of pandemic fundraiser Captain Sir Tom Moore has applied to be struck off the Companies House register. Now known as the 1189808 Foundation after dropping Moore's name, the charity was founded in 2020. In 2024, the Charity Commission found repeated misconduct and mismanagement by Moore's daughter Hannah Ingram-Moore and her husband Colin, including private gains from a £1.4m book deal and an £18,000 awards appearance fee. Both were banned from being charity trustees and resigned. Moore raised £39.4m for NHS charities in 2020 and died in 2021.
