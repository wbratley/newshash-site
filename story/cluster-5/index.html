Mistrial Declared in Lindsay Clancy Case After Jury Deadlock

Unbiased summary

A Massachusetts judge declared a mistrial in the case of Lindsay Clancy, who was charged with murdering her three children. The jury, after about a week of deliberations, was unable to reach a unanimous verdict on charges of first-degree murder, lesser charges, or whether she was not criminally responsible due to postpartum psychosis. Clancy admitted to the killings but pleaded not guilty by reason of insanity. The judge dismissed the jury after they signaled three times that they were deadlocked. Defense attempts to delay the mistrial through an emergency appeal were unsuccessful. Clancy remains held in a mental health facility. A status hearing is scheduled for September 29 to determine next steps, which could include a retrial or plea agreement.
