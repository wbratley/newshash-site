Don't Miss Most Read Latest Two Latvian men have been arrested on suspicion of entering an RAF base in Cambridgeshire for a purpose prejudicial to the UK. The men, aged 36 and 32, were detained inside the perimeter of RAF Molesworth in the early hours of Thursday, October 8, after security patrols discovered an abandoned vehicle near the base. The United States uses the base extensively, with the facility hosting the Joint Intelligence Operations Centre Europe Analytic Centre. Units of the US Air Force’s 423rd Air Base Group are also stationed at RAF Molesworth. Both men, who were identified as residents in the UK, remain in police custody. Counter Terrorism Policing is now leading the investigation, with officers searching two Peterborough addresses. Commander Helen Flanagan, head of Counter Terrorism Policing London, said there was currently no evidence linking the incident to events at RAF Fairford last week. She said: “Our investigation into this incident is at its very early stages. "A big part of this is looking at any potential motivation as to why the men were present at the site, but I must stress we are keeping an open mind at this stage and enquiries are very much ongoing."
