Thomas Markle claims he only has ‘one daughter’ during evaluation, as court rules Meghan’s father fit to stand trial for child pornography offenses Thomas Markle Sr is mentally competent enough to cope with prosecution for the serious child porn charge he faces following his dramatic arrest in Florida, a court-ordered psychiatric report has concluded – even as the father of three told a court evaluator that he only had two children, seemingly omitting the Duchess of Sussex: 'I only have one daughter and one son.' The finding comes nearly two weeks after Judge Lindsay Griffin asked for an evaluation of the Duchess’s estranged father to determine if he could understand the nature and consequences of the proceedings. Markle, who, according to some reports, is suffering cognitive issues, was wheeled into federal court in Tampa, Florida, on Thursday to hear the result of the extensive mental probe by an independent expert. Handcuffed and dressed in a standard orange jumpsuit with the words Pinellas County Jail on his back, the 82-year-old spent most of his time staring down and showing little emotion as the finding was revealed. Judge Griffin told the court there was a ‘reasonable degree of medical certainty’ that the accused porn pervert 'has sufficient ability to understand the nature of the charges against him.’ However, his attorney Mary Claire Wolf said ‘we are not contesting the conclusion of the doctor,’ but that might change if and when Markle’s case transfers to California, from where the charge emanates. The lawyer said: ‘I reviewed the report with Mr Markle. I suspect he would want to contest it but I do not.' Judge Griffin said she would admit the report into evidence. The Duchess of Sussex's estranged father Thomas Markle, 82, was arrested by the FBI in Florida on Friday for allegedly possessing child porn The Duchess of Sussex said she was 'deeply distressed' by the allegations engulfing her father in the Philippines. She has not yet commented on the developments in Mexico She had written a two-page order that Markle showed a ‘lack of recall’ at his first federal court appearance in Tampa on November 6 that had raised questions in her mind about whether he was competent to handle the prosecution. Markle was branded a malingerer in a 15-page psychiatric evaluation report by Amie Gerodimos, Assistant Professor of Psychiatry and Behavioral Neurosciences at the University of South Florida. She wrote that, although he does have 'neurocognitive disorder,’ when Markle 'repeatedly states he does not understand or recall the allegation, he is malingering. 'The essential feature of malingering is the production of false or grossly exaggerated physical or psychological problems, motivated by external incentives.’ Gerodimos added: 'He understood the seriousness of the nature of the charges. It is my clinical opinion, with reasonable medical certainty, that Mr Markle is malingering because he is exaggerating a lack of recall or understanding of the charges in order to avoid criminal prosecution.’ Gerodimos said there was no psychological test that can definitively state whether Markle was feigning. But, she added: ‘It is my opinion that Mr. Markle was exaggerating deficits of remembering the allegation.’ She also wrote he was 'able to recall the allegation throughout our discussion and use the information to strategize in a way that was favorable for himself.' Gerodimos said Markle was being treated for “major depressive disorder, recurrent, moderate" with bupropion and an antidepressant medication. 'However, at the time of my evaluation, Mr Markle reported no previous or current symptoms of depression,' she wrote, adding: ‘Mr Markle reported no current psychiatric symptoms other than memory loss. 'Near the end of the interview (after discussing the allegation against him), I again asked, and he said, "I don’t think so. I am getting depression now!" He reported no thoughts of suicide. 'I inquired about a second daughter because I knew he was residing at [his elder daughter] Samantha Markle's home earlier that week. He said to me, "I don't think so. I have two kids. I think Meghan is Samantha, I only have one daughter and one son." '"I think my son is crucifying me at this moment,"' he said of his son, Thomas Markle Jr, who reported to police in the Philippines that he had found child sex images in Markle Sr's belongings. Gerodimos reported that Markle Sr also said of his son: 'I always poke him because he never went past the eighth grade in school. He did not accomplish anything. I mocked him.’ According to Gerodimos' report, Markle claimed ‘memory lapses’ while recounting his years working in Hollywood and claimed he did not know the name of his first wife, Roslyn Loveless. He said he had used marijuana ‘on a couple of occasions, never routine’ and reported trying cocaine once. There are around 20 boxes in the storage unit, some containing books about pedophilia and magazines showing children being sexually abused Markle told the professor he sees a therapist for ‘forgetfulness’ and ‘declared multiple times he is 82 years old and that is why he has memory deficits.' But he could not recall the name of the therapist before appearing to lapse into confusion over the Duchess of Sussex and Samantha again by saying ‘his sister Meghan’ could help with that. However, the professor concluded: 'It is my opinion, with reasonable medical certainty, that Mr Markle is competent to stand trial.' Meghan’s father has been held in federal custody in Florida after being charged with possessing child sex abuse material allegedly found at his two rented storage units in Los Angeles. The wheelchair-bound former Hollywood lighting director was seized by the FBI on November 6 at Maravida Senior Living in Lakeland, which provides care for seniors with Alzheimer’s and dementia. Markle moved to Florida in May this year for rehab after having a leg amputated in the city of Cebu in the Philippines, where he had been living since January, 2025. He later moved into the Maravida facility, reportedly because he had cognitive issues. At his first court appearance hours after his arrest, Judge Griffin agreed he could be in home detention at the care unit. But staff refused to take him back. He was subsequently released into the custody of eldest daughter Samantha, 61, who lives in a nearby trailer with her partner Mark Phillips, 68. Among the photos in the unit is an undated image of Markle on a bed and with his arm wrapped around a young Asian female, both fully clothed. She appears, alone and naked, in several other pictures as well However, Markle was dragged back to court on November 10 after allegedly tampering with his ankle monitor – leaving the judge to brand his living arrangements ‘untenable’ and order him to be incarcerated. The elderly amputee was then whisked to tough Pinellas County Jail in Clearwater, Florida, where he is prisoner #05907-513. He denies being a pedophile and has dismissed allegations that he hoarded child porn as ’sick lies.' Markle faces one charge of possessing child sex abuse material, which carries a statutory maximum sentence of 20 years in federal prison. FBI agents seized 78 items in the LA units that he had rented for nearly 20 years including slides, negatives and books allegedly containing child sex abuse images. Two images were of an adult man, who a witness identified as Markle, with a nude prepubescent girl, according to the Justice Department. A third showed a prepubescent girl in a room apparently in a motel chain in the Philippines. The affidavit also describes photographs that appear to show Markle with children in various stages of undress. His latest appearance in a Florida court comes three days after police in the Philippines asked prosecutors there to charge him with possessing and distributing child sexual exploitation materials. The complaints were filed in the city of Cebu, where Markle was living in a rented high-rise apartment from January 2025 before he moved to Florida to help him learn to walk again following his amputation nearly a year ago. Prosecutors are reported to be evaluating the three counts before they decide whether to issue a subpoena against 45-year-old Meghan’s father, whose legal team have 10 days to respond. That move came after four boxes of material, including computers, phones, hard drives and old video tapes, were handed to police last month by Markle’s son, 60-year-old Thomas Jr. His arrest in Florida also followed the Daily Mail discovering a stash of child pornography in a storage unit he rented in Rosarito, Mexico, where he lived for 14 years from 2011. In the space were magazines showing children being sexually abused, more than 30 black-and-white prints of naked Asian females – some who appear to be conspicuously young – as well as books sexualizing children. Additionally, there were strips of 35mm negatives depicting voyeuristic images of young children on a beach and dusty VHS tapes with titles including ‘Boys - Brother and Sister - Russian XX’ and ‘Manila Nites.' Mexican police raided the 5ft by 10ft unit, located 10 miles south of the US border, a day after the Daily Mail alerted the FBI to its contents. Markle began renting it in 2017 but stopped paying in June 2024. Guardia Nacional officers, who identified themselves as state sex crimes investigators, launched a midnight swoop on the 5ft by 10ft vault, which was three miles from Markle’s former rented ocean-front home. A copy of the search warrant obtained by the Daily Mail revealed investigators were authorized by a judge to look for ‘evidence consisting of child sexual abuse material’ and ‘the alleged commission of the crime of human trafficking in its aggravated form.’ Markle, for his part, has claimed to the Daily Mail: ‘I had no storage units in Rosarito!’
