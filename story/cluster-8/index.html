Cricket Australia widens sandpapergate probe to include 2017-18 Ashes

Unbiased summary

Cricket Australia has published terms of reference for an independent forum led by senior counsel Fiona McLeod to examine ball-management practices during the 2017-18 season, including the Ashes series and the South Africa tour. The forum will invite players and staff to share experiences confidentially, with no retrospective sanctions or adverse findings. The report and recommendations will be published within 30 days of receipt. The probe was reignited by Usman Khawaja's autobiography claiming the whole team knew about ball-tampering; Pat Cummins denied this, while David Warner and Cameron Bancroft supported Khawaja's account.
