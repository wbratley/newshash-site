British Airways flight BA431 aborts landing at Heathrow after tail strikes runway

Unbiased summary

On Thursday, British Airways flight BA431 from Amsterdam to London Heathrow, an Airbus A321neo, experienced a double tail strike during its first landing attempt at Heathrow. The aircraft's tail scraped the runway, and the landing was aborted, with the plane performing a go-around before landing safely on the second attempt at 10:49 am. No injuries were reported. The aircraft was taken out of service for engineering checks, and the incident was reported to the Air Accidents Investigation Branch. Separately, British Airways is being sued by American passenger Melissa Phillips for over £50,000 after she fell on damp steps while disembarking a flight from Israel at Heathrow in March 2023, fracturing her leg. BA denies liability and has offered to pay £6,987 for medical expenses.
