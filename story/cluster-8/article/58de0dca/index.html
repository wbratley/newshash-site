The England football legend profited from his brand deals with Adidas, McDonald's and the Bank of America during the 2026 World Cup in the US. He also took away hefty sums after starring as the face of the Belgian beer brand Stella Artois and the crisp company Lay's, known in the UK as Walkers. The 51-year-old's work in front of the camera has helped his media, fashion and sports empire, under DRJB Holdings, score record profits this year. It was revealed that the firm behind all his pre-tax profits nearly doubled to £50million in 2025. This means that Sir David received around £38.5million from his share in the dividends which also saw £38.9million paid out to shareholders. The holding company is owned by Beckham's Footwork Productions and US consumer giant Authentic Brands Group who invested in 2022. Footwork Productions owns a 45 per cent stake while Authentic holds the remaining 55 per cent of the company. Most notably, the former England number seven attended England's 2-1 semifinal loss against Argentina. But Sir David is not the only Beckham to rake in millions through their business ventures this year. Victoria's fashion and beauty brand made an eye-watering £7.3million making its first operating profit in its 18 years. Posh Spice's brand, which brought in £129.8million in 2025, scored double digit revenue growth for the fifth year in a row. Bosses at the Beckham-fronted brand are understood to be over the moon after suffering massive losses in its formative years which led to Sir David stepping in to keep the company afloat. It is understood that the brand's shift from luxury to an affordable model has changed its trajectory in recent years. Now Victoria and David have doubled their money, earning them a staggering net worth of £1.185billion.
