Don Toliver postpones London shows after Manchester arena evacuation over unknown substance

Unbiased summary

During a Don Toliver concert at Manchester's Co-op Live arena on Sunday night, an unknown substance was released into the crowd, prompting an evacuation of 12,000 people and a major incident declaration by Greater Manchester Police. One person was taken to hospital, and 89 people showed symptoms linked to exposure. Police initially believed the substance to be tear gas. As a result, Toliver postponed his scheduled London shows at The O2 on Monday and Wembley Arena on Tuesday because his stage equipment remained in Manchester. Live Nation said the O2 show was rescheduled to 15 December and the Wembley show to 14 December. An investigation into the cause is ongoing.
