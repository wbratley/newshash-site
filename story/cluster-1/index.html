Thomas Markle, 82, returned to custody after allegedly removing ankle monitor while awaiting trial on child sexual abuse material charges

Unbiased summary

Thomas Markle, 82, the estranged father of Meghan, Duchess of Sussex, was taken back into federal custody in Tampa, Florida, on Tuesday after prosecutors alleged he cut off his court-ordered ankle monitor at 1:45am. He had been released on bail the previous Friday following his arrest on one federal charge of possessing child sexual abuse material. The FBI seized approximately 78 items, including film negatives, slides, and books, from two Los Angeles storage units Markle had rented since 2008. A magistrate judge revoked his release, citing violations and an 'untenable' housing situation, and ordered a medical evaluation. Markle has denied the charges. The investigation reportedly began after his son, Thomas Markle Jr., discovered images in the Philippines and reported them to authorities.
