US Air Force responds to suspected unauthorised drone activity at RAF Lakenheath

Unbiased summary

On Friday evening, the US Air Force responded to reports of suspected unauthorised drone activity over RAF Lakenheath in Suffolk, a base used by the US military. A US European Command spokesperson said no personnel, aircraft or infrastructure were affected, and that security forces personnel responded. The UK Ministry of Defence said it was coordinating with relevant parties and that the situation was now resolved, working to establish the facts. West Suffolk MP Nick Timothy said authorities told him there was no threat to the public and the incident was resolved. Sirens were reportedly heard at the base, and residents near RAF Feltwell in Norfolk also reported hearing them. The incident followed the recent relocation of US aircraft from nearby RAF Mildenhall, and RAF Lakenheath had been on 'Charlie alert', the second-highest US threat level, since September.
