Labour MPs split over Rosebank oilfield as rival factions lobby PM

Unbiased summary

Nearly 100 Labour MPs, including some frontbenchers, have signed a letter urging Prime Minister Andy Burnham to block the Rosebank oilfield, citing climate concerns. Simultaneously, up to 80 pro-drilling MPs are reportedly considering a counter-letter or an amendment to energy legislation to support the project. The dispute has exposed divisions within the party, with Chancellor John Healey and many Scottish MPs backing Rosebank for jobs and economic reasons, while opponents warn of climate damage. Some ministerial aides may resign if the project is approved. The final decision rests with Energy Secretary Miatta Fahnbulleh, who is legally required to remain impartial. No decision has been announced.
