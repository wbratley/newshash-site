Andy Burnham blames Brexit for failure to stop Channel migrant boats and blasts 'un-British' demos - See more Daily Mail on Google - save us as a Preferred Source Andy Burnham today blamed Brexit for the failure to stop Channel migrant boats reaching Britain. The Prime Minister said the UK's decision to leave the EU had led to a 'loss of collaboration with neighbours' on migration as he faced criticism over a recent wave of arrivals. Although the overall number of people making it to the UK this year is down on the same time in 2025, some 781 migrants came ashore on Wednesday – the highest daily tally this year. It pushed the total since the General Election to 83,279, which the Tories pointed out was higher than the British Army's strength of 83,000 soldiers. Tackled over the numbers on a round of regional TV interviews today, Mr Burnham distanced himself from Education Secretary Lucy Powell, who this week said Labour could not stop every boat. The PM insisted this was his goal but added: 'I do point to the loss of collaboration with neighbours that came with Brexit. That hasn't helped. 'We had a more... co-operative system, if you like, in the past, so we are not able to have the same levels of collaboration that we used to have. 'But I don't put that forward as a reason why we won't keep working until we get the crossings right down. That is our goal, that we don't want to see any crossings at all.' His stance echoes that of his predecessor Sir Keir Starmer. But it appears to put him at odds with his own Home Secretary Shabana Mahmood, who in February said Brexit was not to blame for the boats. Andy Burnham is gaslighting us all to hide his own failures, with over 1,000 illegal immigrants coming in this week alone. Mr Burnham also used the interviews to hit out at anti-migrant demonstrations by black-clad extremists, saying they were 'not the British way'. The Prime Minister said that protests by masked extremists went far beyond expressing concerns about immigration and 'needs to be seen as such, and dealt with as such' by police. He spoke out after anti-migrant activist Daniel Thomas – known to his supporters as Danny Tommo – pleaded not guilty to criminal damage after he allegedly slashed a boat in the English Channel while an emergency responder was on board.
