The 37 best Black Friday deals under £50: lip stain, Lego and a bargain coffee machine

Article text

Black Friday began as a Thanksgiving hangover for Americans, but it may just as well have been invented as a Christmas shopping trap. The timing is perfect. It’s pay day-adjacent, close enough to Yuletide for a sense of pressie-buying urgency, and full of deals cheap enough to buy on impulse. Our bumper roundup of the best Black Friday deals includes brilliant gift ideas, from a classic toastie maker to a cute cashmere bandana, and at under £50 they’re an easier choice than, say, a £900 mattress. This is also a good opportunity to stock up on more mundane stuff. Essentials such as laundry detergent and underwear are generously discounted, including by sustainable brands whose usual prices can be on the high side. If you’re prepping for disaster, as certain sectors think we should, then you can currently get everything from loo roll to power banks at hoardable prices. Here’s our rundown of the best sub-£50 bargains we’ve found so far, all tried and tested by us at the Filter.
