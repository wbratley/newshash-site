In a shock twist this week, James Acaster panicked under the pressure and told everyone he was a Traitor before they voted him out. BBC/Studio Lambert/PA Paige Ingram Maya Jama opens up on her 'savage' last day Maya Jama, who was the only original traitor left in the game, said she would have betrayed a fellow traitor herself if she had survived the roundtable. She said: “That last day I was like, I have to just be a savage now. “I’ve been lovely and considerate and put other traitors’ feelings before mine this whole game, and now I’m going to have to look after myself, because it is really a solo game at the end of the day, isn’t it?” Paige Ingram Maya expected King Kenny to betray her TV presenter Maya Jama has said she expected her fellow traitor, social media star King Kenny, to betray her in the final roundtable of The Celebrity Traitors. The 32-year-old, who was on three votes, was ousted when Kenny decided to vote for her to be banished rather than to tie the poll with eventual faithful joint winner Romesh Ranganathan, who was on two votes. Speaking about Kenny’s decision after the final, she said: “I expected it. I knew that it was going to have to be one of us, and he was a lot more trusted at that time than me, and I felt like it would be selfish for me to expect him to try and save me when it was never going my way. “Although I didn’t expect Leigh-Anne (Pinnock, former Little Mix singer) to vote for Romesh, if Kenny had known that, he could have jumped on that train as well.” Paige Ingram Romesh and Bash's charities revealed as £83K raised Romesh and Bash revealed the charities that their portions of the £83,000 prize pot would be donated to. Romesh’s half will be going to Refugee Council – a charity which works with refugees and asylum seekers. Meanwhile, Bash’s half is being donated to Choose Love – a charity which provides humanitarian aid to refugees.
