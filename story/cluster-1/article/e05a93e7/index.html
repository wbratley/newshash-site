Harry Kane wins Ballon d’Or as emotional star’s voice cracks after becoming first England ace to bag award in 25 YEARS

Article text

HARRY KANE has been named as the winner of the 2026 Ballon d’Or. The Bayern Munich star had an incredible year for club and country last season, scoring 61 times in 51 appearances for the German giants alone. Ballon d’Or 2026 LIVE RESULTS And the star striker got incredibly emotional as he accepted the prestigious honour with his voice cracking as he delivered a powerful speech paying tribute to his Bayern and England team-mates. Kane said: “I’m almost speechless, luckily I have this list. First of all thank you to French football, the Ballon d’Or this amazing event being in London as well looked a good omen for me so thank you very much. “Thank you to everyone who voted for me. I can imagine it was really tough this year, [with] some of the incredible players I was up against – some of my team-mates [and] world class players in the room tonight. “Congratulations to all the winners, all the nominees, an impressive showing of what hard work and determination can achieve, so well done. My Bayern Munich team-mates – I’ve got a few here tonight, without you it wouldn’t have been possible. Liverpool star Michael Owen was the last to seal the title in 2001, becoming the second-youngest player ever to do so at the age of 22 years and four days. Owen beat competition from Raul, Oliver Kahn and David Beckham to take the glory after helping Liverpool to their historic domestic treble in the 2000/01 season. But Kane was up against potentially even stiffer competition, with LaLiga superstars Yamal and Mbappe hot on his heels going into the event at the London Palladium on Monday night. Yamal especially had quite the claim, helping his Barcelona side to the Spanish title while featuring heavily in Spain‘s triumphant World Cup campaign in July. However, the Barcelona superstar didn’t leave the capital empty handed as he won the Men’s Young Talent of the Year award for a third consecutive year. Yamal wasn’t the only Barca star to snatch gold as the club’s women’s manager Pere Romeu won Women’s Coach of the Year and his star players Vicky Lopez and Cata Coll won Women’s Young Talent of the Year and Women’s Goalkeeper of the Year respectively. And the winger’s Spain manager Luis de la Fuente won Men’s Coach of the Year as he led the Furia Roja to their second World Cup, just two years after beating England in the Euro 2024 final. Athletic Bilbao star Unai Simon was part of De la Fuente’s world champions and that is why he won Men’s Goalkeeper of the Year. And the big day for Spain was capped off with London City Lionesses star Alexia Putellas being named Women’s Ballon d’Or winner. But Kane’s CV was hardly shabby, being the main man in a Bayern side that completed the domestic treble this season under Vincent Kompany. He followed that up by joining up with his England team-mates in the US to captain Thomas Tuchel‘s side in the US. His leadership – and his six goals in seven games – helped the Three Lions reach the semi-final before going out in heartbreaking fashion to Lionel Messi‘s Argentina. England has a special relationship with the Ballon d’Or, which was first presented in 1956 by French outlet France Football. The award is voted on by one member of the press from each of the top 100-ranked Fifa member nations. The inaugural winner of the award (then called the European footballer of the year) was English Blackpool star Stanley Matthews, who beat Real Madrid legend Alfredo di Stefano to the accolade. It was in his honour that Monday’s event, marking 70 years since his triumph, was held in London for the first time – having been held at the Theatre du Chatelet in Paris since 2019. Kevin Keegan is the other England star to have won the award, and the only one to have won twice after taking back-to-back honours in 1978 and 1979.
