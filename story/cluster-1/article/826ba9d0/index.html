Liverpool is perfect fixture for Man City to stick two fingers up at Premier League after guilty verdict

Article text

MANCHESTER CITY will be inside a hostile cauldron of boos when they visit Anfield tomorrow. But it is the perfect fixture for Enzo Maresca’s under-fire side to stick two fingers up at the Premier League. The noise around City’s guilt over financial breaches has been unavoidable since the verdict was delivered on September 29. And when they travel down the East Lancs Road to face Liverpool, the players are going to experience it first-hand, and for the first time. They will have all seen the news over the last fortnight but have been protected by the international break, so have not been faced with the anger the rest of the Prem’s teams are feeling. That will change at Anfield. READ MORE IN FOOTBALL The fixture almost feels scripted, given that Liverpool are top of the list when it comes to the teams that have been hurt the most by City’s cheating and could have built a dynasty under Jurgen Klopp. It is no exaggeration to say Kop fans would be building statues of their old German boss if things had gone differently. The 60,000 Reds in the stands are going to be baying for blood when City walk out and will be doing everything they can to make their stance known. In my experience, Anfield is not quite as intimidating as it used to be — not since they widened their iconic tunnel. But you best believe the City players will feel like that tunnel is just as tight as it used to be when they are waiting to step out on the pitch tomorrow. I obviously can’t relate exactly to the position Maresca’s lads are in but I remember when I first played following my arrest. It was Peterborough away, not quite Anfield, but every fan from both sides was letting me know how they felt about me. The difference is, I knew I had done something wrong, these City players are not in the same boat. None of them asked for the executives and the financial guys to give them an undeserved competitive advantage. There’s no doubt City will be feeling the pressure pre-game but as soon as the referee blows his whistle, it will be back to business as usual. As a professional footballer, you are like a horse with blinkers on when the game starts and City’s stars will be no different for this match. For me, it is the perfect fixture for Maresca’s men, who go into it with a 100 per cent record after five Prem games. If they were playing Ipswich or Coventry, they would be expected to win and there would be nothing to talk about. This game gives them the chance to lay down a marker and make a statement to the rest of the league with three points against a Liverpool side they have huge history with. It is a wonderful position to be in, especially with current champs and title rivals Arsenal playing a high-flying Leeds side in the early kick-off today. City could feasibly end the weekend five or even six points clear at the top of the table, and wouldn’t that be the greatest way for them to stick two fingers up at the rest of the Premier League. If I was Maresca, I would be using the controversy and the drama to foster an ‘us-against-the-world’ mentality and use it to fuel a title charge. On the other hand, this match could have come at the worst time for Liverpool, who have won two and drawn three so far. Andoni Iraola looks like he is trying to get his team to play controlled and calm football — but the boisterous Anfield crowd will want anything but. Liverpool fans will be demanding crunching tackles, an aggressive press and a performance for their hero Klopp. We can talk forever about the narrative behind the clash but the exciting thing is this game will be decided on the pitch. We can finally do away with arguing about whose lawyer is going to beat whose lawyer in court and ditch lengthy chats about club execs not blinking on camera. I am a big boxing fan. I want to see Tyson Fury v Anthony Joshua, not Eddie Hearn v Dana White. It is the same situation here – let it be decided on the pitch. I want to see who is going to kick the s*** out of who this weekend and win the game.
