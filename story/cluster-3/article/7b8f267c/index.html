Teen who 'groomed' vulnerable girls worldwide and blackmailed them into sending naked images and self-harm videos is jailed for 14-and-a-half years

Article text

Teen who 'groomed' vulnerable girls worldwide and blackmailed them into sending naked images and self-harm videos is jailed for 14-and-a-half years A ‘psychopathic’ teenager blackmailed children around the world he met on social media into sending him sexually explicit images and gory self-harm videos, a court heard. Beau-Jack Mensforth, now 20, was part of a sick worldwide self-harm group which found vulnerable youngsters on gaming platforms before moving to encrypted online communications. Mensforth, one of the leaders of the group, which the Mail is choosing not to name, would demand sexually explicit images and then threaten to release them if their chosen targets did not comply with their warped demands. These included mutilating or killing their own family pets for ‘content’ or carving Mensforth’s alias ‘Yuri’ into their skin in what his own barrister described as offending of a ‘rarely encountered depravity.’ A number of his victims are now physically scarred for life. From his bedroom in Sunderland, Mensforth would pretend that he was in a romantic relationship with each of his victims so he could obtain explicit material. He would then ‘swat’ the girls – by reporting a gun threat at their home address so armed police would attend – if they refused his demands. He would also threaten to disseminate sexual images or ‘dox’ them – releasing personal details about their identities or whereabouts. Some images were released to groups containing at least 100 people interested in 'sexual violence' without the girls' consent. His nine known victims, as young as 12, were from Britain, the United States and Australia. Beau-Jack Mensforth was sentenced to more than 14 years in prison at Newcastle Crown Court He was part of a gory online group which targeted vulnerable girls and asked for sexually explicit images Mensforth ‘groomed, exploited and manipulated the girls’ when he was aged between 17 and 18 and continued offending even after he was first arrested in October 2023. At Newcastle Crown Court today, Mensforth was jailed for 14-and-a-half years after admitting 17 charges including participation in activities of an organised crime group, blackmail, sexual communication with a child, distributing indecent photographs of a child, making indecent photographs of a child, possessing prohibited images and extreme pornography. He was also ordered to serve an extended term of six years on licence following his release from custody after pre-sentence reports suggested he poses ‘a high risk of serious harm to members of the public.’ Judge Edward Binloss said: ‘This distressing case proves you are a calculating predator. ‘You groomed and blackmailed your victims by making threats to expose them and distribute intimate photographs you had coerced out of them. ‘They were images of victims self-harming that you shared with others who had a taste for sexual violence.’ In a victim impact statement, one girl said: ‘You are what I can only describe as a psychopath. You feed off pain because you need to feel like a big man.’ It is understood Mensforth was an online associate of Kyle Spitz, 27, who was sentenced to 77 years in prison in the US last year for similar offences. Such was the brazen nature of Mensforth’s crimes, that when he was aware the police were investigating his offending he contacted the National Crime Agency – Britain’s version of the FBI – claiming he had in fact ‘infiltrated a paedophile ring’ and asked if there was a ‘reward’ if he could obtain further information. In reality, Mensforth was continuing to extort intimate images and videos of girls self-harming to distribute to others. The NCA said they did not receive the email. The court heard the victims were ordered to carve Mensforth’s username into their skin or even write it on walls in blood. He told one 14-year-old, based in the US, to ‘kill you cat’ or he was ‘swatting you.’ In a statement, the mother of one victim based in Britain, said: ‘There is no treatment that can restore the innocence she lost. 'As her mother I witnessed the aftermath in ways that can’t be captured in a courtroom statement. ‘My child was harmed in ways I couldn’t see or understand what was happening. 'Someone was able to reach into her life through a digital platform and cause such destruction. ‘He may have thought these were just messages. But these were experiences that altered the course of her life. ‘Online exploitation is not less harmful because it appears in a digital environment.’ Another girl self-harmed so badly at Mensforth’s request that when paramedics were called they allegedly said it was ‘the worst self-harm they had ever seen.’ In mitigation, Robin Paton said psychiatric tests were ordered to find out how Mensforth had ‘descended into this moral squalor.’ He said he had autistic spectrum disorder and social anxiety disorder and had accessed the warped online platforms from the age of 14, firstly through gaming sites. After his sentencing, Malcolm McHaffie, of the CPS, said: ‘Beau-Jack Mensforth played a leading role in an online network that targeted vulnerable young girls for exploitation, humiliation and abuse. ‘Using social media and encrypted messaging platforms, members of this group manipulated children into sharing indecent images before subjecting them to threats, blackmail and coercion. ‘The offending in this case caused significant harm to vulnerable victims and involved the encouragement of self-harm, animal abuse and the sharing of child sexual abuse material. ‘Mensforth not only participated in these crimes but admitted helping to recruit and train others to carry out the same exploitation.’ Detective Chief Inspector Steve Brown, of Northumbria Police, added: ‘Beau-Jack Mensforth is a sickening and highly dangerous predator who played a leading role in a network which targeted and manipulated victims into committing vile acts. ‘This case shows there is no hiding place whatsoever for anyone who sits behind a username and preys upon vulnerable individuals anywhere in the world.’
