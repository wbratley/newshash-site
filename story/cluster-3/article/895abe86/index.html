'Cowardly monster' who blackmailed girls to self-harm jailed for 14 years

Article text

A "cowardly monster" who blackmailed girls into self-harming has been jailed for 14 years. Beau-Jack Mensforth, 20, led online harm groups which coerced more than half a dozen girls - all under 18 - into sending sexual images of themselves, hurting animals, and carving his online alias into their skin. An investigation found nine victims in the UK and US who had been groomed by Mensforth and pressured into sending him ever more extreme and explicit content. One victim said he was a "psychopath" who "fed off the pain of smaller beings because he needed to feel like the bigger man", Newcastle Crown Court heard. Operating under the username Yuri, Mensforth, from Sunderland, would contact girls as young as 12, and they would enter what his victims thought were romantic relationships. But soon they would be coerced into sending indecent images of themselves, which would then be used to blackmail them into sending material that was more sexually explicit or showed them self-harming. Mensforth, 17 at the time, would sickeningly share the contact with people in online harm forums, which the court heard amounted to an organised crime group. Victims were also forced to "sacrifice" animals as they were threatened with having their personal details leaked online, or false reports being made to police resulting in armed officers being sent to their home. The court heard that when one 14-year-old victim asked Mensforth why he made people hurt animals, he replied: "For content." A statement from a victim who attended the hearing by video link from the US revealed she had been lured into the group as a 16-year-old from a page on X, which promoted it as a support group. The victim said she had been forced to carve two names into her skin, with the photos then posted on a large platform with thousands of views. The statement, read in court by Ms Hole, said: "I begged them to take it down, but the more I cried about it, the more amusement it seemed to bring them." She said she had been "naive to this type of predatory behaviour" and "had no idea that cowardly monsters like yourself were hiding behind a screen tormenting our baby girl". The statement, read by Ms Hole, said: "Not only had you invaded the safety of our home, you invaded the safety of our daughter’s mind, filling her with fear and shame." Another victim, who joined the court by video link and Ms Hole read her statement, said she had been manipulated by Mensforth when she was just 13, adding that the groomer had stolen "not only my innocence but my education, my dignity and a part of my soul". She added: "You are what I can only describe as a psychopath – you feed off the pain of smaller beings because you need to feel like the bigger man." And another victim said they were manipulated and coerced while only 12. Mensforth, who was found with three different iPhones on three police visits, told Northumbria Police officers he was in the groups because he was "bored". In messages to other users, he talked about one of his victims being in a psychiatric ward, saying: "I'm hoping my girl gets out of the ward soon… she's a really good cutter." The defendant pleaded guilty to 17 offences, including blackmailing people for images of self-harm, sexual communication with children, making and distributing indecent images of children, and possessing extreme pornographic images of animals.
