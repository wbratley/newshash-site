However, should the necessary discussions and agreements be made prior to the window closing, teams can still sign players after 11pm. This comes by way of a 'deal sheet' being handed to the relevant governing bodies before the deadline. A deal sheet is an application to the Premier League or EFL which stipulates a transfer has been agreed between two clubs and the player in question’s representatives, if approved clubs then receive an extension to the transfer deadline in order to complete administrative details. These extension may be utilised in the final two hours of the transfer window, which affords clubs an additional two hours to dot the Is and cross the Ts on the acquisition of a player. Should a club apply for an extension beyond the 11pm deadline, the transfer will fall through as the Premier League or EFL’s Board must receive the deal sheet prior to when the window would ordinarily have shut. And should a deal sheet be submitted in this two-hour period, all the documentation must be fully completed in order for it to be allowed. This is usually why many deals are confirmed up until 1am following deadline day, as clubs are granted an extra two hours to get everything done. Clubs use the deal sheets to avoid situations where moves are unable to go through. That's that!
