UK net migration falls to 77,000 in year to June 2026, lowest outside pandemic since 1997

Unbiased summary

Office for National Statistics estimates show net migration to the UK was 77,000 in the year to June 2026, down 62% from 204,000 the previous year and the lowest outside the Covid-19 pandemic since 1997. Immigration was 738,000 and emigration 661,000. The decline was driven mainly by fewer non-EU nationals arriving for work and study, following tougher visa rules including higher salary thresholds. British nationals leaving outnumbered those arriving by 136,000 (245,000 out, 109,000 in). Home Secretary Shabana Mahmood said the government had 'restored control'. Experts, including former Cabinet Office chief economist Jonathan Portes, warned lower immigration may harm growth and public finances. Opposition leader Kemi Badenoch attributed the fall to Conservative-era reforms and criticised Labour policies for driving skilled Britons abroad.
