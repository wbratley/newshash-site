Duran Duran guitarist Andy Taylor dies aged 65 after cancer battle

Unbiased summary

Andy Taylor, founding guitarist of Duran Duran, died on Monday at age 65 after being diagnosed with stage four metastatic prostate cancer in 2018. His family confirmed his death in a statement, remembering him as a devoted husband, father, and grandfather. Duran Duran bandmates paid tribute, praising his musical contributions and his role in the band's early success. Taylor co-wrote hits including 'Rio' and 'Girls on Film'. Musicians such as Rod Stewart and Belinda Carlisle also shared condolences. Taylor had disclosed his illness publicly in 2022 when he was unable to attend the band's Rock and Roll Hall of Fame induction. He is survived by his wife Tracey Wilson and their four children.
