Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang admits affair with alleged Chinese spy; FBI and company board investigate

Unbiased summary

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang admitted in a company-wide email on Sunday to an extramarital affair with Alice Huang, a businesswoman whom US intelligence officials suspect of being a Chinese spy. Nvidia's board has launched an independent investigation into whether Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort, stating it has no reason to believe company secrets or national security were compromised. President Donald Trump said he spoke with Huang and that the FBI is investigating; Trump expressed support for Huang and said he believes Huang had no knowledge of the alleged espionage. China's foreign ministry rejected the allegations as groundless. Nvidia's stock fell sharply following the revelations, wiping billions from its market value.
