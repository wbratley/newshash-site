Far-right activist Daniel Thomas jailed for 15 months for slashing migrant dinghy in English Channel

Unbiased summary

Daniel Thomas, 37, known as Danny Tommo, was sentenced to 15 months in prison at Portsmouth Crown Court after pleading guilty to criminal damage. On 22 September, he took a boat into the English Channel and slashed an empty migrant dinghy with a knife while it was being assisted by the RNLI, Border Force, and French Coastguard. He livestreamed the incident to a large social media audience, saying 'This is what needs to happen' and 'This is England, this is not France any more.' Judge James Newton-Price KC described Thomas as a 'publicity seeker' whose actions interfered with law enforcement and rescue operations. Thomas was on bail for other matters at the time and faces further charges in June 2025.
