Russell Brand's prison message claiming innocence investigated for possible rule breach

Unbiased summary

Russell Brand, 51, is serving a 10-and-a-half-year sentence after being convicted of one count of rape and three sexual assaults at Southwark Crown Court on 5 November. In a YouTube video posted on 15 November, his wife Laura Gallacher read a message from Brand stating, 'I am not guilty of the offences I have been jailed for.' The Ministry of Justice is investigating whether this breached prison rules, which prohibit prisoners from making statements that could cause distress to victims or communicating with the media without permission. Downing Street and Justice Secretary Alex Norris called the message 'appalling' and 'completely unacceptable,' and said action would follow if rules were broken. Brand also mentioned his transfer to HMP Littlehey.
