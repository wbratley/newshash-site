Man, 23, charged with murder over house fire that killed girl, 7, and her aunt in Middlesbrough

Article text

A MAN has been charged with murder after a seven-year-old girl and her aunt were killed in a house fire in Middlesbrough. Young Valentina Foster and Natalie McDonald, 34, tragically died in the blaze at 5.23am on Wednesday. Valentina had tragically begged relatives to let her spend the night to help celebrate Natalie’s daughter’s 15th birthday. Her distraught gran Valerie Foster, seen hugging the youngster in a family photo, said: “Life is so cruel. My beautiful granddaughter, Valentina.” A 23-year-old man has been charged with two counts of murder, two counts of Section 18 GBH with intent, and two counts of attempted section 18 GBH with intent. Seven people, including two police officers, were killed in the horror – sparking a wave of violence in Middlesbrough. Assistant Chief Constable Dave Sutherland said previously: “Since the tragic incident last Saturday morning in which seven people sadly lost their lives… officers and staff have remained working around the clock to continue to serve the public of Cleveland. “In the same week, we have also seen a tragic house fire on Wednesday in which two people lost their lives, which is also understandably impacting the force and our local communities. “At this time, we are not linking this incident to the fatal collision on the A66. “Our thoughts remain with all those affected by these incidents as we continue our investigations into what happened.” A woman, 48 was pulled from the fire and taken to hospital in Newcastle upon Tyne in a critical condition. Three others were hurt, including one who jumped from an upstairs window. Their injuries are not said to be life-threatening. Aerial images showed the house’s charred roof beams and blackened windows as cops fought to contain the street violence. Witness Ellie, 20, who declined to give her surname, said: “I could hear kids screaming and was shouting for people to help. The house was scorching. “I couldn’t get near the front it was that hot. Two kids got out from upstairs.” Ellie stayed with the woman who leapt to safety from upstairs. She added: “She was crying, in pain and in a bad way.” Another witness who did not wish to be named said: “I saw the girl being carried by a firefighter.” The families of those involved are being supported by family liaison officers. You’re just a baby.” Meanwhile, Natalie’s mum Lisa Kirton told the BBC that Valentina, known as Tina, had been staying with the family over the school holidays. She said Natalie “had a heart of gold who would give anyone anything. We’re still in the hospital with the daughter. “ The 15-year-old and her friend “kicked out a window” and jumped to safety, Lisa said.
