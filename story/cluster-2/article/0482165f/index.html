The second series launch episode attracted a record-breaking nine million viewers on Thursday, surpassing all previous premieres for The Traitors UK. Overnight figures show the first episode peaked at just over nine million and averaged 8.9 million viewers – up almost three million from the first celebrity series, and 2.5 million from series four of The Traitors back in January. Last year's star-studded celebrity series, featuring the likes of winner Alan Carr, Jonathan Ross, Stephen Fry, Kate Garraway and Paloma Faith, launched with a peak audience of 6.5 million viewers, averaging 6.1 million across the episode. The last civilian series opener in January garnered 6.9 million peak viewers and averaged 6.4 million views. The total audience for the episode is expected to go even higher in the coming days, with viewers who streamed the show on iPlayer yet to be counted. It also beat ratings for Strictly Come Dancing's first live show, which have plummeted since Traitors presenter Claudia Winkleman and Tess Daly stepped away. Emma Willis, Josh Widdicombe and dancer Johannes Radebe have since replaced the iconic duo.
