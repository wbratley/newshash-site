Russell Brand hit with more sexual misconduct allegations by SIX other women after being jailed

Article text

Six more women have come forward to accuse Russell Brand of sexual misconduct and predatory behaviour following the comedian's conviction of rape and sexual assault last week. At Southwark Crown Court on Thursday, the 51-year-old was found guilty of one rape and three counts of sexual assault before being sentenced to 10 years and six months behind bars in HMP Wandsworth on Monday. The Metropolitan Police confirmed it had been contacted by "several people" during Brand's trial. Six further women have now come forward with allegations, including rape, against the comedian, an investigation by The Times has revealed. The majority of the fresh claims following a joint investigation by The Times, The Sunday Times and Channel 4’s Dispatches in September 2023 could not be published until the conclusion of his trial last week. The latest allegations include the comic having sex with a woman without her consent before offering her an apology after the act. Brand is also alleged to have continued having sex with a woman despite being told he was hurting her and asked to stop. Afterwards, the comedian is said to have told her: “Tonight was consensual. Nobody did anything they didn’t want to do.” Out of the six women to have made new allegations against him, three have claimed they were targeted at his comedy shows. Although Brand did not offer a response to the latest claims when approached by The Times, the actor has previously denied wrongdoing and claimed all his relationships were consensual. GB News has approached Brand's legal representatives for comment. One of the women – named Alex – said she had entered a relationship with Brand following a professional meeting at his London property where he had a “needy besotted puppy” as he made his way towards her. She alleges the comedian had sex with her without her consent but said she did not "fight it" because “who knows what he could do". Another woman – referred to as Vicky – said Brand approached her while standing in a queue to meet him in 2007 aged 19. LATEST DEVELOPMENTS After allegedly being invited back to his hotel and sleeping together, the pair are said to have met on a number of occasions. During their last meeting, she alleges Brand continued having sex with her after she asked him to stop twice, but she said the comedian ignored her and acted as if he “just wasn’t present”. A third woman — named as Zoe — claims Brand “grabbed my breasts” after she approached him at one of gigs, before she allegedly went back to his hotel, where he “grabbed me and manhandled me” and put his hand “on my throat”. A fourth, Georgia, said Brand approached her at a music festival in 2005 and she was later invited to a show in London, but after telling him she wanted to leave, she claims he told her he “might kill myself if you don’t come back with me”. Georgia claimed Brand acted “relentless to a crazy point” after she refused to have sex with him before he allegedly offered her money. Another woman — known as Rosie — claims Brand asked to speak to her about an "arrangement" after they met at a comedy show in 2006 before they had sex in the disabled toilets. She alleges they met several times over the next year before she said he told her to lick a toilet seat, which she said she believed was a test to see whether she would “do anything”. Rosie described Brand's alleged behaviour as "abusive". The last allegation comes from a woman referred to as Louise, who alleged the comedian made her feel like “a piece of meat”. She claimed while she was working at a London venue frequented by Brand, he grabbed her from a disabled toilet after attempting to lure her inside. Louise said she escaped into another room afterwards, where she "properly cried" after fearing he “would sexually assault me”, adding Brand never apologised for his behaviour. Following his conviction last week, Detective Superintendent Andy Furphy encouraged further victims to come forward, as the Scotland Yard's investigation into Brand continues. He commended women who have already brought forward allegations for their "bravery, courage, resilience and determination". "What we see in many situations like this is when you see other people come forward and they're courageous and brave, other people can get the similar kind of feelings and want to have their story told," he explained. "So if you've been a victim of a rape or sexual offence committed by Russell Brand and you haven't spoken to police yet, then please come forward.
