US Federal Reserve Raises Interest Rates for First Time Since 2023

Unbiased summary

The Federal Reserve raised its benchmark interest rate by 25 basis points to a range of 3.75%-4%, the first increase since July 2023. The decision was unanimous. Fed Chair Kevin Warsh cited persistently high inflation as the reason. President Donald Trump criticized the move, calling for lower rates. New projections suggest further hikes may occur this year. The rate increase also influences expectations for the Bank of England, which faces similar inflation pressures from rising oil prices due to the Iran conflict. The Fed's move aims to cool inflation, which has been above the 2% target for over five years.
