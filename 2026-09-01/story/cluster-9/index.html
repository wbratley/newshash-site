Victoria Beckham's fashion and beauty firm records first operating profit in 2025

Unbiased summary

Victoria Beckham Holdings Ltd, the fashion and beauty company founded by former Spice Girl Victoria Beckham in 2008, reported its first operating profit in 2025. Beckham acknowledged past financial difficulties, including being bailed out by her husband David Beckham, and said the brand is now seen as more than a celebrity venture.
