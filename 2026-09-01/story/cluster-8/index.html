Met Gala John Galliano Exhibition Cancelled Following Backlash Over Past Anti-Semitic Conviction

Unbiased summary

John Galliano announced on Monday that the Metropolitan Museum of Art's planned exhibition honoring his work, titled 'John Galliano: Horizons,' would not proceed. The exhibition had been scheduled to open with the 2027 Met Gala. The decision followed criticism over Galliano's 2011 conviction in France for hate crimes stemming from racist and antisemitic remarks he made in Paris. In a statement, Galliano expressed remorse, said he remained accountable, and acknowledged that an exhibition would be painful for some. The Met's director, Max Hollein, confirmed the mutual decision, and trustee Anna Wintour praised Galliano's decision as courageous. The exhibition's cancellation was reported across multiple outlets, with Galliano citing the need for continued responsibility and avoiding distraction from the Costume Institute's work.
