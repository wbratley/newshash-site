Dolly Parton Laid to Rest, Estate Announces DollyFest for 2027

Unbiased summary

Country music icon Dolly Parton died on 25 August at age 80 after a brief battle with cancer. Her family held a private funeral in Nashville on Friday, interring her at Woodlawn Memorial Park and Mausoleum beside her late husband. Family members, including great-niece Lainey Parton, shared tributes online. On Monday, Parton's estate announced DollyFest, a two-weekend music festival scheduled for 2027 in Nashville and London, replacing a traditional public memorial. The event was originally planned for January 2026 but was postponed due to Parton's health. Danny Nozell of CTK Enterprises said the festival was a dream of Parton's and would bring her vision to life.
