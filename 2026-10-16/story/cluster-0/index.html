Teen kills 3 children and teacher at Oregon elementary school, bystander stops shooter

Unbiased summary

A 16-year-old boy opened fire at Silver Rail Elementary School in Bend, Oregon, on Thursday afternoon. He shot at locked doors to force entry. Three children aged 5, 8, and 11, and one teacher were killed. A seven-year-old and a 54-year-old teacher were hospitalized in critical condition. A community member pursued the shooter into the school and shot him dead. Police arrived minutes later. The shooter was a Bend resident and it is unclear if he had ties to the school. Students were bused to a nearby church for family reunification. The FBI is assisting local authorities.
