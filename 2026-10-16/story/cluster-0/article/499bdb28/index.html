Teen gunman, 16, kills three children aged 5, 8 and 11 and a teacher in Oregon elementary school after shooting locked doors to get inside

Article text

A teenage gunman killed three children and a teacher when he opened fire at an elementary school in Oregon on Thursday afternoon. The unidentified shooter, 16, shot at the locked doors of Silver Rail Elementary School in the city of Bend on Thursday afternoon before killing three children, ages five, eight and 11, and one teacher whose age was not disclosed. He was then gunned down by a good Samaritan whom authorities said followed him into the building. A seven-year-old and another 54-year-old teacher were also transported to a local hospital with critical injuries. A motive for the shooting remains unclear, and authorities would not confirm on Thursday night whether the suspect knew anybody at the school or if he had attended it when he was younger. 'Today is an awful day for our community,' Bend Mayor Melanie Kebler declared. 'Our hearts are broken as we mourn this tragedy and the profound impact it has had on so many people, especially the children, families, educators and school staff whose lives have been forever changed. 'A school should be a safe place where children can learn and feel secure,' Kebler continued, as she called for the community to lean on one another. 'We are here for you. We grieve alongside you and we will get through this together.'
