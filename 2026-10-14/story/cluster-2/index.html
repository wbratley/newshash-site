Burnham rules out Labour coalition with Greens over 'Zionism is racism' motion

Unbiased summary

At Prime Minister's Questions, Andy Burnham said Labour would not form a partnership with the Green Party while it maintains a policy, passed by members at its Brighton conference, describing Zionism as a form of racism. He called the motion 'wrong' and 'antisemitic'. The motion defined Zionism as supporting an ethnonationalist Jewish state in historic Palestine to the exclusion or domination of non-Jewish people. It drew criticism from Jewish groups, four Green MPs and former leaders. Green leader Zack Polanski called the motion 'imperfect' but defended part of it. The issue arose because polls suggest a centre-left coalition could be likely, and Burnham has backed proportional representation.
