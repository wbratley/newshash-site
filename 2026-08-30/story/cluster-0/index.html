James Cleverly resigns from shadow cabinet to pursue London mayoral candidacy

Unbiased summary

Sir James Cleverly, Conservative MP for Braintree and former home and foreign secretary, has announced he is resigning from the shadow cabinet to run for the Conservative nomination for London mayor. He informed party leader Kemi Badenoch of his decision on Thursday. In an op-ed published in The Sunday Times/Times, Cleverly argued the capital needed a champion, blamed Labour for letting London's reputation slip, and accused Prime Minister Andy Burnham of treating the city as a cash cow. He will face a Conservative selection process, with the next mayoral election expected in May 2028. Sadiq Khan, the incumbent Labour mayor, has not confirmed whether he will seek a fourth term. Speculation about other candidates, including Labour's Dawn Butler, continues.
