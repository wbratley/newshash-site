Daniel Dubois stops Fabio Wardley in second round to retain WBO heavyweight title

Unbiased summary

Daniel Dubois retained his WBO heavyweight title with a second-round stoppage of Fabio Wardley at London's O2 Arena on Saturday. Dubois knocked Wardley down with a right hand after about 17 seconds of the first round and dominated the remainder of the round. Wardley survived to the bell, but Dubois continued his assault in the second round, prompting Wardley's trainer, Ben Davison, to throw in the towel after two minutes and 21 seconds. It was the second time in five months that Dubois had beaten Wardley, following an 11-round fight in May. Dubois improved his record to 24 wins (23 by knockout) and three defeats, while Wardley suffered his second loss in 23 professional fights.
