Violent protests across France lead to over 1,200 arrests and damage to historic buildings

Unbiased summary

On Saturday, protests across France against rising living costs and poor school conditions escalated into violence in several cities. While an estimated 550,000 people participated in largely peaceful marches, violent groups clashed with police, set fire to buildings including the Limoges town hall, and looted shops. Police used tear gas and water cannon, and made over 1,200 arrests. Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu said 150 police officers were injured and blamed the violence on 'ultra-left' and criminal elements. Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez condemned the 'opportunistic delinquents' who hijacked the demonstrations. The protests began weeks ago over teacher shortages and poor facilities in high schools.
