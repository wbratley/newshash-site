Police investigate disruption during Strictly Come Dancing live show

Unbiased summary

During the live broadcast of Strictly Come Dancing from Blackpool Tower Ballroom on Saturday, unusual noises interrupted the show as judges gave feedback to the first couple, Delta Goodrem and Kai Widdrington. Judge Motsi Mabuse paused and said, "It's not me." At the end of the episode, presenter Emma Willis apologised to viewers for any audio disruptions and offence caused. The BBC also issued an apology and said it was investigating. Lancashire Police confirmed they received a formal complaint, seized a mobile phone, and are looking into whether any criminal offences occurred. The incident was streamed on social media by content creator Daniel Jarvis, who appeared to make a phone call before the noises played.
