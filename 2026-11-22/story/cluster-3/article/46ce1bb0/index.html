Man appears in court charged with attempted murder after local councillor stabbed

Article text

A man has appeared in court charged with attempted murder after a local councillor was stabbed while working in the community. Arfan Ali, 32, is accused of attacking Bradford councillor Ibrar Ali, 52, at about 11am on Thursday in the West Yorkshire city. The alleged attacker, of Thornbury Avenue, Bradford, has also been charged with assault and possession of an offensive weapon in a public place, according to West Yorkshire Police. He appeared at Leeds Magistrates’ Court on Saturday, the Crown Prosecution Service confirmed. He was remanded in custody ahead of his next appearance at Bradford Crown Court on December 21. Penning a statement after the stabbing, the victim's family said the local politician had joined council wardens with community clean-up jobs at the time of the attack. They said: "The family would like to express their heartfelt gratitude to everyone who has offered prayers, kind words and messages of support during this difficult time. "They would also like to extend their sincere thanks to the police, paramedics and members of the public who courageously provided first aid and assistance at the scene. "Their swift actions and support are deeply appreciated." Cllr Ibrar had been "made aware of reports that the individual involved in the incident may be experiencing mental health difficulties", his family added. The statement continued: "Despite what has happened, he has expressed his sincere hope that the individual receives the appropriate care, support and treatment they may need. "Councillor Ibrar is grateful for the overwhelming kindness shown and hopes to personally thank everyone once he has recovered. "For now, the family kindly requests privacy and understanding to allow Councillor Ibrar the time and space he needs to rest, heal and recover. LATEST DEVELOPMENTS: "Please continue to keep him and his family in your duas." Police said armed officers were engaged in a tense standoff with the suspected knifeman donning a red jumper. At the time, a spokesman said: "A man believed to be the suspect is currently on the roof of a property on Thornbury Avenue. "Armed officers have been deployed and are working to bring the man down and resolve the incident safely." Cllr Ibrar's Bradford Moor colleague, Riaz Ahmed, shared his concerns in the wake of the shocking attack. He said: "As an elected councillor, I find incidents of this nature deeply disturbing. "No councillor, elected representative or member of our community should have to fear for their personal safety while serving the public. "Violence and intimidation have no place in our society or in local democracy. I hope the situation is resolved peacefully and that everyone involved receives the necessary support." Former Labour Party leader Jeremy Corbyn posted a message of support for Ibrar Ali, a Your Bradford Independent group councillor. The Your Party MP for Islington North said: "I have been proud to campaign alongside Ibrar for a more equal and peaceful society...I am appalled by the shocking news he has been attacked. "Sending solidarity and support to Ibrar, his family and the community of Bradford." In a statement, Your Party said it was "shocked and deeply saddened" by the incident.
