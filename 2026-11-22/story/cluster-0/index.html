Suffolk village holds symbolic vote to 'leave the UK' over asylum seeker housing plans

Unbiased summary

Residents of Barnham, Suffolk, held a symbolic referendum on Saturday regarding plans to house up to 931 male asylum seekers at the nearby former RAF Barnham base. Of approximately 500 eligible voters, 257 voted in favor of seeking 'crown dependency status' and 16 voted against, with one spoiled ballot. The vote is not legally binding. The referendum was organized by the Barnham Residents' Association in response to the government's proposal, which is part of a broader plan to move asylum seekers from hotels to disused military sites. Similar symbolic votes have occurred in Piddington, Oxfordshire. Local officials and politicians, including Reform UK's Zia Yusuf and Conservative leader Kemi Badenoch, commented on the vote. The government said it would listen to concerns.
