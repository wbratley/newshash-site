What to know as Russian cargo ship ‘on fire after drone attack’ in the Baltic Sea

Article text

A cargo ship bound for Russia’s Baltic enclave of Kaliningrad was struck by drones in international waters in the Gulf of Finland on Saturday, officials in the region have said. Sparta II was damaged west of Russia’s Gogland island, caught fire, and later drifted into Finnish territorial waters, Finland’s Defence Forces said. The 16-member crew evacuated onto lifeboats, then to a nearby Russian ship, and is on its way back to Russia, Finnish Border Guard Commander Ilja Iljin told public broadcaster Yle late on Saturday. Ukraine has sent waves of military drones towards Russia’s northwest Leningrad region this year, including some that strayed into the airspace of the Baltic region, leading to fears that the war was affecting NATO territory. Finland has tightened security measures in the area in response to the incident. Finnish Defence Forces communications director Aki-Mauri Huhtinen said it is likely Ukraine is behind the strike, local news outlet Yle reported. The vessel is known to have links to Russia’s armed forces, Finnish Border Guard ‌commander ‌Iljin said. Swedish combat aircraft, operating under ‌Finnish command, took part in safeguarding Finland’s and Nato’s territorial integrity following the incident, Sweden’s Defence Minister Pal Jonson said. Finland and Sweden are Nato’s newest member states, joining the military alliance in 2023 and 2024, respectively, in response to Russia’s full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022. “The consequences of fighting Russia’s aggression have reached the Baltic Sea. Another signal for the aggressor to stop. But nothing will stop us from supporting ⁠Ukraine,” Estonia’s Prime Minister Kristen Michal wrote on X. Estonia sent a navy vessel to assist in the rescue operation, including firefighting and pollution response, its government said. The vessel is subject to US sanctions and is linked to Russian shipping company Oboronlogistika, according to the US Treasury Department. Oboronlogistika provides logistics services for the Russian Defence Ministry, according to the company’s website. “At the moment, there is no confirmed information about what cargo is on board, but it cannot be ruled out that there is military materiel on board,” Iljin of the Finnish Border Guard said. The governor of Leningrad region, Aleksandr Drozdenko, said the ship had been attacked by two drones ⁠in international waters about three nautical miles from ​Russia’s ⁠border. The crew was being evacuated and the extent of the damage was being assessed, he added. Finland’s defence ministry said the military had reintroduced temporary aviation and maritime ⁠restrictions in the eastern Gulf of Finland, to protect civilians and enable ​authorities to ⁠operate if drones needed to ‌be intercepted. It also said a Russian helicopter ⁠is suspected of violating Finnish airspace, adding ​the ⁠suspected ‌violation may be linked to ‌rescue or ‌monitoring operations ⁠involving the cargo ship. Finland had informed its allies about the incident, the ministry said. Kaliningrad, an ‌enclave on the Baltic between Poland and Lithuania, has occasionally ‌been a flashpoint between Russia and Nato and ​the EU. Gogland lies in the eastern Gulf of Finland, about 25 miles from Finland’s coast. ‘War spilling into neighbouring areas’ Helsinki said ⁠it had introduced temporary aviation and maritime restrictions in the ‌eastern Gulf of Finland to protect civilians and allow authorities to operate if drones needed to be intercepted. “The war is spilling over into our neighbouring areas. Finnish authorities are handling the situation in the Gulf of Finland, where a ⁠drone strike has hit a Russian merchant vessel. Ukraine has the right to fight against the aggressor,” Finnish Prime Minister Petteri Orpo said on X. The Finnish Border Guard said in a statement: “The situation poses no danger to other maritime traffic… Preparations for potential rescue operations are being made in co-operation with other authorities in Finland and internationally.” Ukraine’s foreign ministry has not yet commented on the drone activity. Sparta II has reportedly been transporting weapons and equipment for Russia’s army. The US announced sanctions against it in May 2022, just after Moscow launched its full-scale invasion of Ukraine. Kyiv has also sanctioned the vessel. The governor of Kaliningrad, Alexei Besprozvannykh, said the incident posed no threat to supplies to ⁠the region. Drozdenko said 80 drones had been shot down over the Leningrad region, and lifted a drone threat warning. Streeting called it an “outrageous provocation… and in that context, there has to be a response. The UK’s Chief of Defence Intelligence has warned Putin’s aggressive approach to the West could result in a “significant miscalculation” with serious consequences. Lieutenant General Matthew Jones said he did not believe Russia was actively seeking war with Nato but its behaviour was becoming reckless. His comments, in a speech at the Cambridge Intelligence Seminar on Friday, were published on Saturday following the incident in Poland and accusations that Moscow was behind an assassination attempt in Denmark. “Putin is under pressure and that is leading to a more assertive and aggressive approach as he seeks to challenge us,” Lt Gen Jones said. “There are too many recent examples of reckless Russian behaviour where, if the incident had been slightly different, the results could have been extremely serious.” Additional reporting by agencies
