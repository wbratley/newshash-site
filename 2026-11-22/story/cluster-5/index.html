British father fined €3,000 after toddler son's fatal fall from Cyprus hotel window

Unbiased summary

Joshua Foster, 37, a British accountant, pleaded guilty to causing death through a reckless, rash or dangerous act after his two-year-old son Jaxon fell from a fourth-floor window at the King Evelthon Beach Hotel and Resort in Chlorakas, Cyprus, on 12 July. Foster had lifted the boy to see a waterpark, believing the window was closed. The child fell about 10 metres and died of severe head injuries at Paphos General Hospital. Foster was fined €3,000, the minimum penalty for the offence, which carries a maximum four-year sentence. He was held in custody for eight days after the incident. In an interview with the Daily Mail, Foster described his despair and said he will carry the burden for life.
