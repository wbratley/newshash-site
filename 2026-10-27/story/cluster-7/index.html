Russell Brand's former PA testifies about his sexual behaviour at rape trial

Unbiased summary

At Southwark Crown Court on Monday, statements from prosecution witness Helen Berger were read in the trial of Russell Brand, who faces three charges of rape, three of sexual assault, and one of indecent assault against six women between 1999 and 2009. Brand denies all charges. Berger, who worked as Brand's personal assistant from June 2006 to February 2007, described his 'insane sexual appetite' and said he was 'sleeping with people day and night.' She said his bad behaviour was 'normalised and dismissed' by those around him. She also recalled Brand often being in his underwear at his flat and using MySpace to find women. The defence case is expected to begin Tuesday.
