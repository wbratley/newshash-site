Burnham rules out EU rejoin vote in this parliament and freedom of movement without referendum

Unbiased summary

Prime Minister Andy Burnham stated in interviews on Monday that the UK would not rejoin the EU during this parliament, which ends in 2029, and that he would not return to freedom of movement rules without a referendum. He said he wanted an 'honest discussion' about the long-term relationship with the EU, referencing lost immigration control and the small boats crisis. A Downing Street spokesperson later clarified that Burnham was 'not proposing a return to freedom of movement, or any option' and was 'not leaning either way', with his focus on next month's UK-EU summit. Burnham also indicated he could campaign on rejoining the EU in a future election but insisted he would not move ahead of the public.
