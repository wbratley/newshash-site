Danny Tommo jailed after slashing migrant boat in Channel

Article text

Anti-migrant activist Daniel Thomas, known as Danny Tommo, has been jailed for 15 months after slashing a migrant dinghy with a knife in the English Channel. Thomas confirmed his name and said he pleaded guilty on November 12 to damaging property. The 37-year-old, wearing a grey sweatshirt, received the term for criminal damage at Portsmouth Crown Court, where he appeared by video-link from HMP Winchester. Thomas sat with his head in his hands as the sentence was being read to him by the judge, James Newton-Price KC. The founder of anti-migration group Patriot Platform had taken a boat out into the Channel and observed migrants in a dinghy being assisted by the RNLI, Border Force and French Coastguard on September 22. He had then instructed his boat to be steered towards the empty rhib that was adrift and then armed himself with a knife as rescue crews also approached the dinghy and a Frenchman boarded from a rescue vessel. Thomas was heard in the video footage to say: “This is what needs to happen,” and: “This is England, this is not France any more,” after slashing the boat with a knife. The events were livestreamed to a “large audience” from Thomas’s social media platform, Portsmouth Crown Court previously heard. Thomas had initially pleaded not guilty to the offence but changed his plea at a hearing earlier this month. Mr Newton-Price treated the sentencing as an either-way offence, which carries a maximum sentence of 10 years. The judge told the court that the damaged migrant boat has since been destroyed and there is “no way” to quantify the value of the damage. Defence barrister Simon Pentol KC, representing Thomas, told the court it cannot be proved the value of the damage is above £5,000 and that should be "heavily reflected" in the court’s sentencing. He said: “There has been no additional or more serious charge preferred of a religious or racially aggravated form of criminal damage. Nor has there been any further charge of political violation or one suggesting in any way this is a terrorist offence or a public order offence. "He’s not been charged with any matters of that sort." He added the political landscape should be viewed "from a very narrow prism", adding: "He’s not been charged with a political offence." Mr Pentol added: “It’s quite clear in that video Daniel Thomas makes it quite clear that there should be no violence. “The damage that he did inflict on that Rhib was after disembarkation of any entrants into this country and no-one was put in any danger, and it’s not been suggested they were. “In respect of his actions, it’s plain to see that whatever personnel were on that vessel none were troubled by it, they were unmoved by it as was the Rhib itself and it did not go below the waterline one jot. “It did not submerge and whatever he did didn’t cause it to. The irony is that when it was returned to France, the authorities destroyed it, that is perhaps the supreme irony.” LATEST DEVELOPMENTS: Mr Pentol said that Thomas said his action to slash the boat was a “spur-of-the-moment” action. He said: “It was not premeditated, the vessels came across one another, the knife was part of the equipment stored on the boat, it was not his and was not taken to commit any offence whatsoever. “He wished to cause no harm to any person. This was nothing more than performative activism on matters he feels very strongly about, as do many others whether they are ordinary folk in Crowborough or Piddington, it should not be turned into something it is not.” Joel Smith KC, prosecuting, told the court that Thomas had five previous convictions. He said: “On 2nd February 2006 he was sentenced to a conditional discharge for using threatening, abusive or insulting words and behaviour, with intent to cause fear or provocation of violence. “On 2nd August 2007 he was sentenced to a conditional discharge for using disorderly behaviour, or threatening, abusive or insulting words and behaviour, likely to cause harassment, alarm or distress. “On 16th May 2011 he was fined for being carried in a stolen vehicle. On 7th October 2013 he was conditionally discharged for a fraud matter. On 17th June 2016 he was sentenced to two years’ imprisonment for kidnapping." Mr Smith added: “The defendant is a founder and leader of ‘patriot platform’, an anti-migrant movement which publicly promotes ‘taking our country back’ and ‘reclaiming our borders’. It is a movement which is heavily reliant on social media. “At the time of this offending, the defendant was on bail awaiting trial in Kent for offences of aggravated trespass, public order offences and maritime offences committed in Dover. “In March of this year, the defendant is alleged to have entered a migrant processing centre by scaling a perimeter fence and, as he was escorted from the premises, he became aggressive. “A month later he was found on a disused pier filming the same processing centre and as officers approached, he threatened to jump into the sea, resulting in a two-hour stand-off. “He also repeatedly shouted comments concerning migrants in the centre, likening them to terrorism, and shouting at life boat volunteers. “Later in the same month, it is alleged that the defendant entered the western docks in Dover, climbing a fence and interfering with the activities of Border Force and security personnel, this taking place after he had previously been personally directed by the harbourmaster not to return to the port.” Mr Smith KC then outlined the details of the criminal damage offence on September 22, telling the court: "The defendant and others were in the English Channel on a boat, a rigid hulled inflatable boat, or Rhib. The defendant was filming and live streaming another Rhib carrying migrants across the Channel. “The occupants of the boat were being assisted by the RNLI, coastguard, the Border Force and French lifeboat. On his livestream, the defendant can be heard to broadcast the postcode of the place to which the migrants were to be taken, seemingly to encourage protest. “There is no suggestion that he was encouraging violence, indeed he seems to be discouraging this. The migrants who had been on the Rhib were brought on to RNLI lifeboats. “This having been done, the defendant asked another person on his boat to approach the Rhib, saying ‘let’s get rid of that boat’. “The defendant was shouting about migration and said, amongst other things, ‘This is what needs to happen to every one of these boats’ and ‘You’re not using these boats, no more migrants in this country. The defendant thought that he was in English waters. “As the defendant’s boat approached the Rhib, a French rescue worker stepped onto the Rhib. The defendant told him that he was in English waters. He then slashed the Rhib with a knife, causing a hole to the side of the Rhib and a hissing sound. “He then shouted ‘Get off the boat, you’ve got time to get off the boat’ and ‘This is not France, take them back, no more of this, we pay you too much money, don’t take that back to France, traitors’."
