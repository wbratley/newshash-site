Bayern Munich confirm Harry Kane ‘out for next few months’ with ankle injury

Article text

Bayern Munich have confirmed Harry Kane will be “out for the next few months” with ankle ligament damage sustained in their 1-1 draw with Cologne on Friday. Kane was substituted after 20 minutes of the Bundesliga game, having been left limping after a challenge from the Cologne defender Joël Schmied. The Ballon d’Or winner was substituted and the returning Bayern coach, Vincent Kompany, said that the injury would take “months” rather than weeks to heal. A club statement on X said: “Harry Kane suffered a tear of the syndesmosis ligament in his left ankle in Bayern Munich’s Bundesliga home match against Köln on Friday evening. This was confirmed by an examination by Bayern Munich’s medical department. The 33-year-old will therefore be out for the next few months.” The Tottenham loanee Mikey Moore fired the visitors ahead before Kane’s injury, but Bayern levelled when Nathaniel Brown scored before the break. Bayern also lost Michael Olise to injury for the second half, and Said El Mala missed an excellent late chance to earn Cologne’s first win at Bayern since 2009. The 33-year-old may face a race to be fit for the Nations League quarter-finals, the first leg of which takes place in Norway on 27 March – just over four months away. The Bayern sporting director, Max Eberl, said on Friday that club doctors were “discussing whether or not to recommend surgery” for Kane’s injury. Part of Kane’s recovery period will come during the Bundesliga’s winter break from 20 December to 8 January. He will, however, miss Champions League games away to Lille next week and at home to Slavia Prague next month, as well as the January trip to Manchester United and the league phase finale at home to Real Betis. Kane’s setback adds to an extensive Bayern injury list, with Olise also going off during Friday’s game with a foot contusion. Luis Díaz, Serge Gnabry, Alphonso Davies, Ismael Saibari and Manuel Neuer were already unavailable, leaving Kompany short of options for a busy month of fixtures. Kompany returned from his own month-long absence for Friday’s game with his arm in a sling, having dislocated his shoulder when he collapsed on a training pitch in October. The Belgian was taken to hospital for meningeal irritation treatment after his collapse, and has been recovering at home. “I think this result was fair and Cologne deserved the point,” Kompany said after the game. “We have other worries now, I have to honestly say. We will all try together to make something strong out of this situation. FC Bayern does that time and again, and that’s what we want to do now too.” Cologne took a surprise lead with 12 minutes gone when Moore started and finished an excellent break. The teenage winger pounced on a mislaid Olise pass and drove forward, finding Linton Maina who laid off for El Mala, whose no-look backheel pass allowed Moore to round keeper Jonas Urbig before sliding the ball home. Kane was then brought down in the Cologne box from behind by Schmied, who got the ball first but clattered into Kane’s left leg in his follow-through. Kane’s penalty appeals were waved away, but he was left visibly limping and received treatment on his ankle before being replaced by Tom Bischof on the 20-minute mark. The hosts levelled 17 minutes later when full-back Brown sliced a low shot into the bottom-right corner, but suffered another blow just before the break when Olise went down. The France star did not return for the second half and was seen leaving the stadium shortly after on crutches – but it appears unlikely he will miss a significant amount of time. Just before the hour mark, El Mala and Maina forced a spectacular double save from Urbig. El Mala, recently called up by Germany, then had a golden chance to put Cologne in front with eight minutes to go when one-on-one with Urbig – but he cut his close-range shot inches wide of the post. Now a point behind Bayern, Borussia Dortmund can move into outright first place if they win at bottom club Mönchengladbach on Saturday. Freiburg can also move level on points with Bayern if they can defeat promoted Elversberg. This month Kane signed a new deal with the Bundesliga giants until June 2029, which is reportedly worth in excess of £400,000 a week, making him the highest-paid player in Europe before bonuses. Since arriving from Tottenham three years ago, Kane has scored an incredible 156 goals in 163 appearances, helping Bayern win a league and cup double last season.
