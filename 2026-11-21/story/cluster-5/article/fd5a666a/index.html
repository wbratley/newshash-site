The best Black Friday beauty deals I’d actually buy, from LED face masks to skincare staples

Article text

Few of us may be able to justify the cost of a big-ticket TV this Black Friday, but beauty is one area where we can usually afford a treat or two. Thank you, lipstick effect. It can feel like a no-brainer to stock up on our regular favourites and get a few Christmas presents crossed off the list while prices are lower. But deals do something funky to our brains. Retailers know how to lure us in, using big discounts and “selling fast” pop-ups that cause our typically rational brains to short circuit. Add a high-pressure countdown, and before you know it, you’ve been enticed into buying countless products you didn’t set out to buy. Impulse beauty buys are easy to regret later when you snap back into consciousness – not only because of the money you’ve spent, but also because of the waste they create. To avoid getting caught up in the frenzy, always write a wishlist before you shop. Consider yourself and the people you’re buying gifts for, and then stick to researching the products you actually need. And if a new product catches your eye but you’re uncertain about it, remember that the Boxing Day sales aren’t far away. That said, if you’ve landed on this page because you’re hoping to upgrade your beauty routine for less, or because you’re searching for a gift for a loved one, I’ve dug out a selection of genuinely good savings on beauty products we’ve tested and love at the Filter. How we selected these Black Friday beauty deals We’ve tested our way through hundreds of beauty products, from eye creams to hot brushes and LED masks. Our team of expert beauty journalists have decades of experience between them, so they truly know how to sort the wheat from the chaff. These deals are hand-picked from the most effective products in skincare, makeup and hair care. Not all Black Friday discounts are what they seem. Some retailers have inflated their prices over the past few weeks, only to drop them to normal under the guise of a Black Friday discount. This happens every year, so to avoid misleading deals, we always check the price history of every discount we recommend. To do this, we use the free Camelizer browser extension, which unveils the price history of any Amazon listing. I recommend using this any time you shop on Amazon; I’ve been covering Black Friday for half a decade and have found thousands of dud deals using this tool. The deals below are legitimate savings on products our beauty experts recommend and actually use in their beauty routines. You’ll also find a list of particularly generous site-wide sales from some of our favourite retailers. It’s important to remember that Black Friday itself isn’t until 27 November. While many deals go live before the big day, some won’t drop until later, so check back when it’s crunch time to find an updated list of beauty bargains.
