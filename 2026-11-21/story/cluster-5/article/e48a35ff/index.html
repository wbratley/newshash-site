Want to shop more sustainably this Black Friday? The best UK deals on eco-friendly products we love

Article text

Ethical and sustainable probably aren’t the first words that spring to mind when you think of Black Friday. The annual price-slashing jamboree sits cunningly in wait for your final payday before Christmas, hoping that the dangled carrot of a few quid off will make you more likely to overconsume and overspend. Approach it with care, though, and Black Friday is a good opportunity to buy eco-friendly products that might normally stretch your budget. Many of our Filter recommendations tick either the green box or the affordable box, but we (and you, I expect) would be a lot happier if more of them ticked both. As a Filter reader, you already know the value of researching and planning your purchases before you buy. To make Black Friday work for you rather than the other way around, go in knowing which eco-friendly products caught your eye but never made it into your shopping basket. Intrigued by Who Gives a Crap toilet paper but balked at its price? Wanted to upgrade your mattress to a sustainable one, but without needing a mortgage? Then keep reading. How we selected these sustainable Black Friday deals Sustainable shopping can get expensive, even in the sales, because these brands tend to pay more for ethical labour and responsible sourcing than, say, fast-fashion brands. For this rundown, though, we’ve found some genuinely good deals that make more sustainable choices more affordable. As ever when rounding up Black Friday deals, we’ve researched each item’s price history using tools such as PriceRunner and the Camelizer to ensure that these price cuts are rare enough to recommend. We’ve tried hard to avoid greenwashing, too. Just because a product has the word “natural” in its branding doesn’t mean it’s environmentally friendly. All the recommendations below are from companies making notable sustainability efforts, such as zero-waste manufacture and offering refills. Most brands here are B Corp-certified. This means the companies have passed stringent assessments of what B Lab UK calls their “social and environmental performance, transparency and accountability”, so it can provide a useful indication of their ethical and environmental practices. The best sustainable Black Friday deals for 2026 Plastic-free sponge scourers Seep eco sponge scourers x 12, £28.50 (was £36) Help avert the plastics crisis from your kitchen with these pan-safe scourers made from loofah and wood pulp cellulose, with zero microplastics. B Corp-certified Seep says a pack should last you a whole year. Annoyingly, this price cut takes the pack below Seep’s free delivery threshold, but the site has plenty of gift-friendly plastic-free cleaning packs too. Filtered water kit Phox the Gym Buddy (jug, bottles and refills), £49.99 (was £54.99) “As someone who previously fretted about single-use plastic filters, Phox has definitely changed my life for the better,” wrote Charlotte, one of many Filter readers who wrote in to suggest their favourite sustainable brands. This starter pack includes a filter jug, chic blue gym bottle and three months of refills, with a fiver off. Phox also offers filters on subscription in letterbox-friendly, plastic-free packaging. Upcycled cream shampoo UpCircle shampoo creme, 100ml, £14 (was £16.99) “My hair likes it very much,” wrote Hannah Rochell of this semi-solid vegan shampoo in 12 sustainable cleaning and toiletries subscriptions. The appropriately named UpCircle uses upcycled ingredients including coffee grounds, oat husks and, for this shampoo, leftover berries from the fragrance industry. The B Corp brand’s sustainable products aren’t the cheapest, but you can now get 30% off subscriptions site-wide, plus 25% off one-off purchases. For more, read our guide to the best Black Friday beauty deals on tried-and-tested products Minimalist natural deodorant UpCircle refillable deodorant, £11.90 (was £16.99) Another 30%-off subscription from Upcircle, this natural refillable deodorant impressed Mabel Banfield-Nwachi with its ease of use and “pared-back design” in our guide to the best natural deodorants. It’s normally a little pricey, especially given that Mabel “needed a top-up most days”, but its eco credentials are on point, with natural-origin ingredients and a bamboo and bio-resin case you can compost at home. Skin-kind refillable deodorant Fussy deodorant starter pack, £9 (was £12) Dragons’ Den-backed Fussy also featured in our guide to the best natural deodorants and in Hannah Rochell’s roundup of 12 sustainable toiletries subscriptions. The refillable cases are made from 50% recycled plastic and come in a range of striking designs, while the deodorant itself is made using natural ingredients and proved “effective [and] kind to the skin” in Hannah’s tests. The code BLACK gets you 25% off site-wide for Black Friday. Refillable hand wash Fussy natural hand wash starter pack, from £10.25 (was from £12) The Filter’s deputy editor Monica Horridge is a big fan of Fussy’s gorgeous-smelling hand washes, which you can buy one-off or on subscription. The refills come in cans, which are easier to recycle than some brand’s refill packs that come in soft plastic. Subscription is cheaper, at the usual price of £12 for a starter pack that includes a bottle designed to last, but you get a bigger Black Friday discount with the one-off purchase, which drops from £16 to £12 if you enter the code BLACK at checkout. Fragrance-free laundry liquid Bio-D fragrance-free laundry liquid 1l, £5.05 (was £6.35) Kindness to sensitive skin is a common theme with eco-friendly toiletries and cleaning products. When I set out to discover which eco-friendly laundry detergent could actually clean my clothes, Bio-D’s biodegradable formula left my clothes and sheets smelling fresh (it was no match for my tougher stains, though). Abel & Cole has some brilliant early Black Friday deals, undercutting retailers including Amazon on oodles of Bio-D cleaning products. Plant-based detergent Faith in Nature laundry liquid (1l), from £6.47 (was £9.99) Another delicious-smelling laundry detergent that was kind to my skin but not quite aggressive enough on my stains, Faith in Nature’s non-bio liquid comes in a recycled plastic bottle and boasts a wealth of certifications including vegan, cruelty-free, UK-made and living wage employer as well as B Corp. Stock up on this brand’s range of cleaning products and toiletries, including refills and plastic-free washing bars, with up to 40% off site-wide. Chic vegan laundry liquid Eat Dirt non-bio laundry liquid 1l, £17.82 (was £19.80) I loved Eat Dirt’s artful tin container so much that I called it a detergent you’ll want to display as well as use. Its sustainably sourced, vegan formula also washed well enough to earn a place in our guide to the best eco-friendly laundry detergents, where I said its mandarin, neroli and warm amber scent “left my towel smelling sensational”. Bamboo toilet paper Who Gives a Crap 100% bamboo toilet paper (12 pack), £13.52 (was £15.90) “The colourful paper each roll comes wrapped in also doubles as wrapping paper, so I always save it,” wrote Filter reader Marcel in our guide to 20 simple ways to cut down on plastic, and at this time of year that’s more than enough to recommend this sustainable bamboo toilet paper. At more than £1 a roll it’s normally more than double the price of supermarket recycled loo roll, so this 15%-off deal from Abel & Cole is very welcome. Sustainable toothbrush Suri Pro 2.0 electric toothbrush, £74 (was £89) “If you care about sustainability in any way, [Suri is] the best choice,” wrote Alan Martin of this colourful toothbrush in his Suri Pro 2.0 review. Its plant-based heads are made from cornstarch with castor oil bristles, and Suri will recycle them for free, even giving you a self-addressed compostable bag to send them in. Enter the code at checkout to get the deal, which takes up to £25 off anything you add to your basket – and throws in a free washbag worth £14.99. … and a more affordable sustainable toothbrush Suri Core 1.0 electric toothbrush, £49.99 (was £65) The Suri Pro 2.0 has more bells and whistles, but our toothbrush tester Alan was even more impressed by its cheaper, more lightweight sister model, the Suri Core 1.0. The battery is 100mAh smaller and the aluminium body is replaced with a bio-circular plastic made from waste cooking oil, but you still get a pressure sensor, wireless USB-C charging and recyclable heads. Snuggly bamboo duvet Panda the Cloud bamboo duvet (double), £110.46 (was £129.95) I loved this duvet so much I bought it twice – I’ve now got its summer edition (4.5 tog) as well as this lightweight but snuggly 10.5-tog version. Its breathable bamboo-infused material feels deliciously fresh against my skin at any time of night, even inside a duvet cover. B Corp-certified Panda sources its bamboo from managed forests, and runs sustainable initiatives including free mattress recycling. For more, read our guide to the best duvets Luxury wool all-seasons duvet Panda’s duvet was my top pick, but this was yours, flying off the shelves after I featured it in our best duvets roundup last winter. Woolroom isn’t B Corp certified, but this machine-washable duvet has superb eco credentials including fully traceable British wool, unbleached cotton casing and Oeko-Tex 100 certification. It’s still pricey, but with 30% off it’s an outstanding lifetime investment, including a lighter duvet and a toastier duvet that you can fasten together for full winter warmth. Cosy mattress topper The Fine Bedding Company Spundown mattress enhancer (double), £52.50 (was £75) The Fine Bedding Company’s cosy Dual Layer topper featured in my guide to the best mattress toppers, but it’s not included in the Black Friday sale yet. The Spundown topper is, though, and it has similar specs to its dual-layer cousin, notably a cosy filling of “Smartfil” microfibre made from recycled PET bottles. This B Corp-certified company’s eco credentials are excellent, including its zero-waste factory powered by 100% renewable energy. The best sustainable mattress Woolroom Standen wool double mattress, £1,442.35 (was £2,219) My choice for best sustainable mattress in our roundup of the best mattresses, this magnificent creation is blissfully comfortable and about as eco-friendly as a hulking great mattress can be. My goodness, though, it’s expensive, with an insomnia-triggering usual price of £2,219 for a double, now slashed by 35% in the sale. No Black Friday deal can do much about its weight, though – this is definitely not a mattress to try turning on your own. Chic cotton bedding Piglet in Bed gingham washed cotton duvet cover (double), £69.30 (was £99) Like Woolroom, Piglet in Bed is a UK company that’s not yet certified by B Corp, but we’ve included it here because we rate its environmental credentials and approach. The brand champions “buying less and buying better” and makes its products from responsibly sourced 100% linen and cotton. It was mentioned repeatedly when we asked interior designers for their favourite bedding, and the stripey sets proved particularly popular, although they’re pricier than the gingham.
