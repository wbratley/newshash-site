Gogglebox cast give their savage verdicts on Twilight star Robert Pattinson as James Bond: 'I don't want Bond pale and interesting. Get back in your coffin Edward'

Article text

The cast of Gogglebox have given their savage verdicts on the news that Robert Pattinson is set to become the new James Bond. After months of speculation, it was revealed on Monday that the 40-year-old, who shot to global fame portraying vampire Edward Cullen in the Twilight saga, will become the seventh actor to play 007. However, while many have lauded the casting, the stars of Gogglebox have not been so forthcoming with their praise as viewers will see on Friday night, with sisters Ellie and Izzi Warner moaning that they 'don’t want Bond pale and interesting'. Referring to his vampire role in teenage flick Twilight, they scathingly added: 'Get back in your coffin Edward!' Meanwhile, Andrew admitted of Twilight that he'd 'never watched it', with Alfie quipping: 'That’s because you’re not a teenage girl!' As the cast continued to watch a BBC News bulletin on Robert's new role, several expressed their confusion at who the star actually was, with Jenny admitting: 'Never even heard of him. Robert was a late entry in the race to play the secret service agent, as it had been previously reported that Jack Lowden, Callum Turner and Josh O'Connor made up the original shortlist. It's thought that much of Pattinson's casting decision stemmed from Lauren Sanchez, who is married to Amazon mogul Jeff Bezos and was 'captivated' by the actor's audition when she saw a copy. Bezos himself has confirmed he was 'heavily involved' in the casting, with the next Bond film set to be produced by Amazon MGM Studios after the Broccoli family handed over creative control of the franchise.
