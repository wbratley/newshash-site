On-the-run female chemistry teacher arrested 70 miles away in Hampshire after dodging sentence for having sex with 14-year-old pupil up to 30 times Police have arrested a female chemistry teacher who slept with a 14-year-old pupil up to 30 times and seduced another after she failed to turn up to court to be sentenced. Sally-Anne Bowen, 65, lured the two boys into sexual relationships when they were students at Christ's College, Finchley, in north London in the 1980s. She had sex with one of the boys 20 to 30 times in five months in the spring and summer of 1988 in a room she rented near the school. Bowen also asked the second pupil to fondle her breasts while kissing him on different occasions. A warrant was issued for her arrest after she failed to turn up to be sentenced at Harrow Crown Court this morning for her historic sex crimes. Judge Karim Ezzat was informed by prosecution counsel Neelan Gomersall that Bowen was not at court at roughly 10.30am. Then just after 11am Judge Ezzat said in open court: 'I will issue a warrant not backed for bail. I would ask that if she is arrested today she is brought to court.' A Met spokesman confirmed Bowen was tracked down in Hampshire, around 70 miles away. He said: 'A warrant was issued for her arrest and officers promptly launched enquiries to locate her. At around 15:00hrs, officers from a neighbouring force found the woman at a location in Hampshire and arrested her on suspicion of failing to appear at court. 'She has been taken into police custody. A new sentencing date will be set by the court in due course.' The court heard prior to today's hearing that there had been communication between Bowen and the police which was 'suggestive of refusal rather than inability' to attend. Defence barrister Mark Kimsey said she had been in contact with solicitors and had engaged with a pre-sentence report, adding there had been 'concerns as to her welfare due to the content of the messages' from her. When police officers arrived at Bowen's home in Hemel Hempstead, she was not there but her partner Darren Scotchbrook, was at the address. After being convicted Bowen's bail conditions did not require her to hand over her travel documents to the police. Mr Scotchbrook was home she was not. 'Enquiries are ongoing trying to locate her.' Mr Kimsey, added: 'Instructing solicitors tried to contact her. The phone rings she does not answer.' Judge Ezzat said he will deal with her sentence when she is arrested. Bowen’s neighbours said today that they last saw the former teacher walking her black Labrador a few days ago. One neighbour, whose children attended The Cavendish School, a secondary comprehensive in nearby Hemel Hempstead while Bowen taught there as a supply teacher for a year or two, said: ‘When I saw her walking she seemed nervous. There was something about the way she walked. ‘She walked with her head down and was quite quiet, but she was very nice to speak to when we did chat.’ Bowen lived in a secluded row of houses about a ten-minute drive outside of the small town in Hertfordshire. Over the two decades she lived there she tended to her front garden, which was immaculately kept with a low sitting bench and a flowerbed edged around a small grass lawn. ‘There was always something a bit strange about her, but in recent years she became more reserved,’ another neighbour added. ‘I used to see her walk her black Labrador twice a day. She would walk past my front window while I was watching TV. ‘We both had our hips fixed at roughly the same time so I knew her enough to say hello to, and she would ask me how I am getting on. But there was always something about her.' Neighbours confirmed seeing a police car with two uniformed police officers arriving at her house at roughly 11.30am after a warrant was issued for her arrest. ‘I read about this story online, but I did not connect the woman pictured going into court looking so glamourous with the woman at the end of the street,’ one of her neighbours added. 'She always used to walk around wrapped up because of the weather and for want of a better word she looked "homely". ‘It is strange to think that she lived so close to me. When I spoke to her, she was very reserved, but I had no idea about the case. I thought that was just her personality.' Bowen will now be held in custody until she is brought back to court for sentencing. At the time of her offending Bowen was aged 25. During her trial prosecution witnesses said she wore tight tops with short skirts and discussed sex with the boys – aged 14 to 15 – to lure them in like the 'Pied Piper'. Bowen denied the allegations and claimed that she had a medical condition which meant she could only have sex for a short time. She called Mr Scotchbrook to give evidence at her trial earlier this year in an effort to convince the jury she was telling the truth about her condition. But jurors convicted her at Harrow Crown Court of seven counts of indecent assault by a majority of ten to two after six hours and 29 minutes of deliberation. Bowen was banned from teaching by the Teaching Regulation Authority in December 2023 in relation to the abuse. Bowen denied the allegations saying she couldn't have sex for long periods of time At the trial jurors were informed of the disciplinary process but were not told of the decision to bar Bowen from the classroom out of concern it would prejudice them against her. The teaching profession's disciplinary board described her as 'calculated' and motivated 'by the desire to seek her own sexual gratification'. One former pupil who was not targeted by Bowen but remembered her flirting with other boys was called to give evidence during the trial. He said: 'She would often chew on the end of her hair or cardigan. She would brush her hand over her breasts. 'It was more like what you would see in a Sabrina Carpenter video than what you would see in a classroom. 'The topics of conversation in class often strayed away from chemistry and became quite sexual.'
